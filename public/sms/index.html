RetailHQ VMS · Text reminders

How staff opt in to text reminders

RetailHQ VMS is the internal vendor management app of Shaver Lake Sports Inc. Only our employees have logins. Each employee chooses, on their own Tasks page, whether task reminders reach them by text.

vms.shaverlakesports.com/tasks  ·  Tasks  ·  bottom of the page

How I get reminders

Pick one, two or all three. Also used to tell you when someone finishes a task you gave them.

559-555-1234

By saving with this box checked you agree to receive task reminder texts from Shaver Lake Sports VMS at this number. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop or untick By text here any time.

Save

What the texts are

Reminders for tasks the employee set or was given inside VMS (for example a vendor call-back or an order to send), and a note when a task they handed off is finished. Nothing promotional, never sent to customers.

Opting out

  • Untick By text on the Tasks page and click Save, or
  • reply STOP to any reminder. Reply HELP for help.

Example messages
