How staff opt in to text reminders

RetailHQ VMS is the internal vendor management app of Shaver Lake Sports Inc. Only our employees have logins. Each employee chooses, on their own reminder settings page inside VMS (behind the login), whether task reminders reach them by text. This is what that page shows.

How I get reminders

Pick one, two or all three. Also used to tell you when someone finishes a task you gave them.

Yes, sign me up for texts

The By text box starts unticked for everyone. Texts go out only after the person ticks it, enters their own number and clicks the button.

What the texts are

Reminders for tasks the employee set or was given inside VMS (for example a vendor call-back or an order to send), and a note when a task they handed off is finished. Nothing promotional, never sent to customers.

Example messages

  • VMS reminder: Call Columbia rep about backorder — due today. https://vms.shaverlakesports.com/tasks Reply STOP to stop.
  • VMS: Jarrett marked "Send Smith Optics order" done. Reply STOP to stop.

Opting out

  • Reply STOP to any reminder, or
  • untick By text on the reminder settings page and click Save.
