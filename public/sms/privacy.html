RetailHQ VMS · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026. This policy covers RetailHQ VMS ("VMS"), the internal vendor management application of Shaver Lake Sports Inc. ("we", "us"), including its text message (SMS) reminders.

Who uses VMS

VMS is used only by employees and owners of Shaver Lake Sports Inc. There is no public sign-up. Customers of our stores do not have accounts and never receive messages from VMS.

What we collect

  • Your name, work email address and role, entered by the administrator when your login is created.
  • Your mobile phone number, only if you enter it yourself on the reminder settings page to receive text reminders.
  • The tasks, notes and vendor records you create or are assigned inside VMS, which are business records of Shaver Lake Sports Inc.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show you the parts of VMS your role allows.
  • To send you task reminders and task-completion notices by the channels you chose: dashboard, email and/or text message.
  • We do not use your phone number or email for marketing or promotions.

Text messaging and sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as the messaging provider that delivers our texts, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Texts are delivered through Twilio, our messaging provider, which processes your phone number and the message content solely to deliver the message.

Opting out

You can stop text reminders at any time by replying STOP to any message, or by unticking By text on your reminder settings page in VMS and clicking Save. Reply HELP for help, or contact us below.

Retention and security

Your phone number is kept only while text reminders are turned on for your account; unticking By text removes it from use, and deactivating your login ends all reminders. Data is stored with our hosting provider (Supabase) with access limited to signed-in staff according to their role.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do. Questions: Dana Powell, Shaver Lake Sports Inc., Shaver Lake, California, dana@shaverlakesports.com.
