## Building the Name

> Build every Item Name in this order, skipping any part that doesn't apply to the item. Everything is entered in **ALL CAPS**, with a single space between parts.

<tr class="odd">
<td>1</td>
<td><strong>Brand</strong></td>
<td>The brand printed on the item's <strong>tag, label, or packaging</strong>. Most items have one — check the tag even when the invoice doesn't list a brand.</td>
<td>Items with a brand on the tag or label</td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td>2</td>
<td><strong>Vendor</strong></td>
<td>The vendor's name, used <strong>instead of</strong> a brand only when there is no brand on the tag, label, or packaging. Never include the buying source — LAZY ONE, not LAZY ONE (WWD).</td>
<td>Items with no brand on the tag</td>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<td>3</td>
<td><strong>Sizing category</strong></td>
<td><p>Who the item is sized for: ADULT, UNISEX, MENS, WMNS, LADIES, YTH, KIDS, BOYS, GIRLS, JR, TODDLER, or INFANT. ADULT, UNISEX, TODDLER, and INFANT are</p>
<p>always spelled out; sizing words that have a shorthand always use it (WMNS, YTH, JR — never WOMENS, YOUTH, or JUNIOR), KIDS is always KIDS (never KID), and BABY is never a sizing category (use INFANT) — BABY can still be used in the description, like BABY SUNSCREEN.</p></td>
<td>Items that come in sizing</td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td>4</td>
<td><strong>Description</strong></td>
<td>Built from the invoice and the product tag together, simplified if the wording would be confusing. There is no fixed word list — product words follow the invoice and tag, so they vary from vendor to vendor. Includes distinguishing details (hood, zip-up), any measurement (40 OZ, with a space before the unit; foot and inch marks attach to the number, 6'), and the pack count for multi-packs (3 PK). The always-used shorthand (sizing words, sizes, measurement units, pack and count words (PK, PR, CT, PC, DZ), ASST and MULTI as colors, SL, W/, W/O) is used every time; other approved shorthand only when the name runs long.</td>
<td>Every item</td>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<td>5</td>
<td><strong>Color</strong></td>
<td>The full color name, placed right before the size. Never abbreviated — the only exceptions are ASST for assorted colors, MULTI for multicolor, and fishing color shorthand on Fishing items only to stay within 52. Two or three colors are joined with a slash (BLUE/WHITE); four or more use MULTI.</td>
<td>Items that come in colors</td>
</tr>

</table>

## Rules to Follow

<table>
<colgroup>
<col style="width: 24%" />
<col style="width: 75%" />
</colgroup>
<thead>
<tr class="header">
<th><strong>Rule</strong></th>
<th><strong>Details</strong></th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr class="odd">
<td><strong>ALL CAPS</strong></td>
<td>Every item field is entered in ALL CAPS — Item Name, Custom SKU, Tags, and so on.</td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td><strong>Punctuation</strong></td>
<td><p>No apostrophes, commas, pound signs (#), or ™ or ® symbols anywhere in the name. <strong>No accented letters anywhere, including the brand</strong> — use the plain letter instead (POKÉMON becomes POKEMON, JALAPEÑO becomes JALAPENO), because accented letters are hard to look up in Lightspeed. There are two exceptions: the <strong>brand</strong> is always written exactly as it appears on the tag, including any apostrophes, periods, &amp;, or other marks (see Brand — where to find it), and the <strong>vendor ID</strong> is always copied exactly as printed on the invoice, including any #, periods, slashes, or spaces (see Vendor ID — how to add it). Everywhere else: Periods only as a decimal point (8.5, 1.5 OZ, 0.5 MM, or a caliber like .22).</p>
<p>The marks that are allowed: a <strong>dash (-)</strong> anywhere it's needed, with no space on either side (0-3 MO, ZIP-UP, 12"-27"); an <strong>X</strong> used as the "by" sign in dimensions, with no spaces (7X13, 3/8"X75'); a <strong>slash (/)</strong> in the description, sizes, and multi-color items (W/ HOOD, 1/4 ZIP, 7/8,</p>
<p>BLUE/WHITE); <strong>parentheses</strong> around the number size in youth sizing (LG(14/16)); the <strong>foot (')</strong> and <strong>inch (")</strong> marks right after a number (6', 12"); <strong>percent (%)</strong> in the description (2% MILK, 40% DEET); <strong>plus (+)</strong> for strengths, ages, and weight ranges (+1.75, AGE 14+, 90+ LBS); and an <strong>asterisk (*)</strong> only as a do-not-reorder marker at the start of the name (* or **, see below). Use straight ' and " marks, never curly ones pasted from a website or document.</p>
<p>Drop any other mark rather than replacing it — for example, WOMEN'S becomes WMNS, and a phrase or design name like I'M A KEEPER becomes IM A KEEPER.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<td><strong>Pound sign (#)</strong></td>
<td>Never use # in the name (a # inside a vendor ID or in a brand as printed on the tag stays). For hook, lure, and numbered footwear sizes, use SZ instead — for example SZ 6, not #6. Clothing never uses SZ.</td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td><strong>Do-not-reorder markers</strong></td>
<td><p>Asterisks at the very start of the name tell staff not to order the item again. There are two markers: <strong>*</strong> (one asterisk) means the item is discontinued or can no longer be ordered from the vendor — for example *COGHLAN'S WATER BAG 9748. <strong>**</strong> (two asterisks) means we chose not to reorder it because it doesn't sell well or the quality is poor — for example</p>
<p>**ROOSTER TAIL 206 1/16 OZ FROG SPOT 368655. Markers are added and removed by staff only, never automatically — VMS reads them and warns staff, but never adds, removes, or changes them. Don't use * anywhere else in the name. The asterisks count toward the</p>
<p>52-character limit, so shorten the name if adding them pushes it over.</p></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<colgroup>
<col style="width: 24%" />
<col style="width: 75%" />

<tr class="header">
<th><strong>Rule</strong></th>
<th><strong>Details</strong></th>
</tr>

<tbody>
<tr class="odd">
<td><strong>Name length</strong></td>
<td>Keep the whole name to <strong>52 characters or fewer</strong>, counting spaces and any do-not-reorder asterisks (* or **) — that is what fits on a shelf tag. If a name runs long, shorten it in this order: (1) make sure the always-used shorthand is applied (sizing words, sizes, measurement units, pack and count words (PK, PR, CT, PC, DZ), ASST and MULTI as colors, SL, W/, W/O); (2) other approved shorthand; (3) for Fishing items only, the fishing color shorthand; (4) simplify the description. Never drop the brand, size, or vendor ID to make it fit. If a name still can't get to 52 after every shortening step (for example, some Ty names with a birthday, size, and vendor ID), leave it as is — that is an accepted exception.</td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td><strong>Spacing</strong></td>
<td><p>One space between every part and word. Besides the no-space marks below (dashes, the dimension X, slashes — except W/ and W/O, which always take a space after them — and the foot and inch marks), the only other no-space cases are a letter size and the parentheses that follow it (LG(14/16)), a do-not-reorder marker and the word after it (*COGHLAN'S), and</p>
<p>% or + next to its number (2% MILK, AGE 14+, +1.75). Always put a space between a number and its unit (8 OZ, 2 LBS, 6 MO, 0.5 MM, 3 PK). SPF is treated as a unit, so it is separated from its number by a space (SPF 15, SPF 50+). Never put a space next to a dash (ZIP-UP, 0-3 MO, 12"-27"), and never around the X in a dimension (7X13, 21"X6"). A</p>
<p>do-not-reorder marker attaches to the first word with no space (*COGHLAN'S, **ROOSTER TAIL). Always put a space after W/ and W/O (W/ HOOD, not W/HOOD). The foot and inch marks are the exception and go right after the number (6', 12").</p></td>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<td><strong>Brand — where to find it</strong></td>
<td>Take the brand from the item's tag, label, or packaging, not from the invoice — invoices often leave the brand off or show only the vendor's name. If the tag and the invoice disagree, use the tag. The brand must always match the tag: write it exactly as it reads there, in ALL CAPS, keeping any apostrophes, periods, or &amp; (COGHLAN'S, O'NEILL, A&amp;W). The brand is exempt from the Punctuation rule, with two exceptions: leave off ™ and ® symbols, and replace accented letters with plain ones (POKEMON). If the tag shows both a parent company and a product brand, use the product brand — for example, School O' Minnows lures are made by Luhr Jensen, so the name starts with SCHOOL O' MINNOWS, not LUHR JENSEN. Use the vendor's name only when the item has no brand anywhere on it, and never with the buying source attached (LAZY ONE, not LAZY ONE (WWD); COGHLANS, not COGHLAN - WWD).</td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td><strong>Brand field and item name</strong></td>
<td>The Lightspeed Brand field and the brand in the item name both match the tag exactly, punctuation included (COGHLAN'S in both). The Brand field is always in ALL CAPS, like every other field (STICKER PACK, not Sticker Pack). The parent company, if different, does not go in the item name.</td>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<td><strong>Shorthand</strong></td>
<td><p>Use only shorthand from the Approved Shorthand List in section 3 (for example, W/ for WITH). SHAVER LAKE is always written SL, and WITH and WITHOUT are always written W/ and W/O, so every item turns up in the same search. If a name runs too long, shorten common words with approved shorthand rather than truncating it or dropping information.</p>
<p>Only shorthand on this list may be used — a new shorthand has to be added to the list before anyone uses it.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td><strong>Color</strong></td>
<td><p>Always spell the color out in full and never abbreviate it, so it can't be misread as a size or other code. Two exceptions: when an item comes in assorted colors, use ASST in the color slot; when a single item has four or more colors, use MULTI, either alone or with its main color (MULTI or MULTI BLUE). Fishing category items only (never clothing or other items) may also use the fishing color shorthand in section 3 (CHART, FLO, GLO, HOLO, and so on)</p>
<p>— but only when spelling the color out would put the name over 52 characters; otherwise spell it out (CHARTREUSE, FLUORESCENT, ORANGE). Use the color name printed on the tag, including named colors like ASPEN VIOLET or MIDNIGHT BLUE. Always spell it GRAY, never GREY. For items with more than one color, list the colors in full with a slash between them and no spaces — for example BLUE/WHITE or BLACK/RED/WHITE.</p></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<td><strong>Pack counts</strong></td>
<td>Always include the pack count for multi-pack items, at the end of the description right before the color, written as the number, a space, and PK — for example 3 PK, 6 PK. If the tag counts pairs instead of packs, use PR (6 PR).</td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td><strong>Size</strong></td>
<td><p>Use the approved size abbreviations (XXS, XS, SM, MED, LG, XL, and REG for Ty plush) instead of spelling sizes out. Oversizes are always written as listed — 2XL, 3XL, 4XL, never XXL or XXXL. Shoe half sizes are always written as decimals (a tag reading 8 1/2 becomes 8.5); sock size ranges are written as on the tag (6-8 1/2). Ty plush uses REG for regular size; larger Ty plush uses the size on the tag (MED, LG). All footwear (shoes, boots, sandals, flip flops, water shoes, slippers) with a numbered size always puts SZ before the size (SZ 9, SZ 8.5). Footwear sized with letters (SM, MED, LG, SM/MED) does not use SZ — the letter size stands on its own (TY SLIPPERS ... SM). Clothing, socks included, never uses SZ or the word SIZE (except ONE SIZE) — the size stands on its own (WMNS TANKINI BLUE 16).</p>
<p>Items with one size use ONE SIZE, never OS. For a pre-pack that holds mixed sizes, use ASST in the size slot. If the colors are assorted too, write ASST only once, not ASST ASST (WMNS FLIP FLOPS ASST).</p></td>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<td><strong>Kids' sizes</strong></td>
<td><p>Kids' items use YTH, KIDS, BOYS, GIRLS, or JR as the sizing category — use whichever the tag shows (if the tag and the invoice disagree, follow the tag). Enter kids' sizes exactly as written on the tag, with a slash and no spaces — for example 7/8, 10/12, 14/16. Girls' mid sizes with an X are kept as written on the tag (6X, 4-6X, 6/6X). Never split them into separate numbers or convert them to letter sizes. A kids' letter size (YTH ... SM) uses the normal size abbreviations. When a tag says youth and also says BOYS or GIRLS, don't put the two side by side (no YTH GIRLS or YTH BOYS). BOYS or GIRLS stays in the sizing category slot, because it tells you who the item is for, and YTH moves right next to the size (PULSE GIRLS JACKET ASPEN VIOLET YTH LG 21-3241-J — Pulse is a World Famous Sports brand, so the vendor ID goes last). This works the same with number sizes (GIRLS ... YTH 14/16).</p>
<p>YTH is only added when the tag says youth; a tag that says only GIRLS, BOYS, KIDS, or JR uses just that word. When the tag shows both a letter size and a number size, include both: the letter size first, then the number size in parentheses with no space between them — for example LG(14/16), MED(10/12). If a vendor ID follows, it goes after the closing parenthesis with one space.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td><strong>Toddler sizes</strong></td>
<td>Toddler items use TODDLER as the sizing category, always spelled out. Enter toddler sizes exactly as written on the tag, number and T together with no space — for example 2T, 3T, 4T. Don't change a toddler size to a kids' size or a letter size, even when they seem to overlap (4T is not the same as YTH 4).</td>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<td><strong>Infant sizes</strong></td>
<td><p>Infant items use INFANT as the sizing category, always spelled out. Never use BABY as the sizing category; BABY is only allowed as a word in the description (BABY SUNSCREEN).</p>
<p>Write infant sizes as the number of months, a space, then MO — for example 6 MO, 12 MO, 18 MO, 24 MO. For a range of months, use a hyphen with no spaces around it and MO once at the end — for example 0-3 MO, 3-6 MO, 6-12 MO. Don't change an infant size to a toddler size, even when they seem to overlap (24 MO is not the same as 2T).</p></td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td><strong>Descriptive words</strong></td>
<td>Always include distinguishing words that are available on the item itself, even if the invoice doesn't call them out — for example, that a jacket has a hood or is a zip-up — so the name reflects the actual product.</td>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<td><strong>Measurement-based sizing</strong></td>
<td>If the size is a measurement (ounces, pounds, liters), put it in the description instead of the size slot — for example, 40 OZ WATER BOTTLE. Fishing line strength always uses LB, never LBS (8 LB, 20 LB). Feet and inches are written with the foot (') and inch (") marks right after the number, with no space — for example 6' LEASH or 12" RULER.</td>
</tr>

| **Rule**                         | **Details**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|----------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Vendor ID — when to add it**   | Only for Fishing category items, or items bought from Coghlans, World Famous Sports, Lazy One, or Ty. "Bought from" means the actual supplier (Coghlans, World Famous Sports, Lazy One, Ty), even when the invoice comes through WWD — not the brand and not WWD — so a World Famous Sports house brand like PULSE still gets the vendor ID. Every other item is named without a vendor ID (the ID still goes in the Vendor ID field).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Ty birthdays**                 | Ty plush items keep the birthday from the tag. Write it as the 3-letter month, a space, and the day (JAN 23, MAR 6, SEP 10 — never MARCH, SEPT, or JUNE), placed right before the vendor ID so the vendor ID stays last: TY BEANIE BOOS MEADOW FOX ORANGE REG MAR 6 36379. The birthday counts toward the 52 characters.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Vendor ID — how to add it**    | Copy it exactly as printed on the physical invoice (keep leading zeros, and keep any \#, periods, slashes, or spaces — the vendor ID is exempt from the Punctuation rule), in ALL CAPS, as the very last part of the name. If the invoice has no vendor ID for the item, leave it off rather than using a placeholder. Naming can start before the invoice arrives (from the tag and the order confirmation); add the vendor ID as soon as the invoice is in. On a long name, shorten the description — never drop the vendor ID.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Vendor ID — which number**     | Always use the number on the physical invoice. If the number on the product itself is different, use the invoice's number, not the product's. World Famous Sports items sometimes have an invoice ID that differs slightly from the one on the product — still use the invoice's. For WWD orders that have both the vendor's own invoice and an EdenRed invoice, use the vendor's invoice; if the two show different vendor IDs, flag it for a staff member to check before the name is saved.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **No duplicate names**           | Every item name must be unique — no two items in Lightspeed can have the exact same name. Before saving, search Lightspeed for the name. If another item already has it, add whatever tells them apart (size, color, pack count, or model).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Items sharing one vendor UPC** | Don't lump genuinely different items into one entry just because the vendor put them under one UPC. Create a separate entry for each, give each a unique UPC using its Lightspeed System SKU, and tell them apart in the name by whatever makes them different (size, color, and so on).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Renaming an existing item**    | If a rename changes what the name says — different words, size, color, or vendor ID — print a new shelf tag, replace the old one on the shelf, and verify it against the register **within 7 days**. Cosmetic and shorthand-only renames don't need a new tag on their own: capitalization, spacing, curly quotes, removing marks the SOP doesn't allow (™, ®, commas, apostrophes outside the brand), replacing accented letters, adding or removing a \* or \*\* marker, or switching a word to its approved shorthand (YOUTH to YTH, SHAVER LAKE to SL, L/S to LS, GREY to GRAY). Because the price is printed on the tag, **any price change needs a new tag the same day**, verified against the register — whether the name changed, changed only cosmetically, or didn't change at all. |
