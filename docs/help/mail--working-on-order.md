While an order goes back and forth with a rep, flag the conversation so you can keep track of it.

## How to
1. Open the conversation.
2. Click **Working on order**. It goes on your dashboard box **Orders I'm working on**.
3. The button now reads **Working on order ✓**. Click it again to stop tracking.

## Good to know
- The flag is yours: it shows on the list of the person who clicked it.
- Its status follows the mail: the rep wrote last means **Needs an answer**, we wrote last means **Waiting on rep**.
- See "Orders I'm working on page" to mark it **Working** or **Completed**.
