When Edenred's invoice lines don't agree with VMS, a card comes to the review queue. VMS never changes the order on its own.

## Freight differs from Edenred
1. The card shows the freight in VMS and the freight in Edenred. Click **Open the order** to look. Under it, **From WWD Seq # …** lists the WWD invoices Edenred's freight comes from; click one to open its PDF.
2. Click **Use Edenred's** to put Edenred's freight on the order, or **Keep** to keep ours.

## WWD invoice PO doesn't match its order
1. The card shows the PO on the WWD invoice and the PO of the order it is tied to. Click the **WWD Seq #** to open the invoice PDF.
2. Click **Right order, keep it**, **Move to another order** (pick the order, then **Move to order**), or **Untie it**.

## WWD credits: which invoice?
1. The card shows the credit, its PO and its lines. Click its **WWD Seq #** to open the credit's PDF.
2. Pick the invoice in **The invoice it is for**. The credit then shows on that invoice as "Credit note: received WWD credit on (date) for (amount)".
3. Click **Not for one invoice** when it isn't for a single invoice.

## Good to know
- "(no PDF on file)" next to a WWD Seq # means the PDF hasn't been filed yet.
- Freight cards are under the **Freight** tab; PO and credit cards are under **Needs a hand**.
