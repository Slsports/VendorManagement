Everyone at Worldwide Distributors, for the rare times we need them: a vendor not cooperating, a warehouse problem, a missing invoice.

## How to
1. Click **WWD contacts** in the menu (or **Worldwide people and phone numbers** on a WWD vendor's page).
2. Search by name, department or vendor letter.
3. Click a phone number to call, or an email address to write from VMS.

## Good to know
- **Key contacts** come first, then everyone by department. "vendors A–F" means that person handles vendors starting with those letters.
- WWD's member number and main phone are at the top.
- Dana edits the list in **Settings**, **Worldwide** (link at the bottom of the page).
- WWD is emailed only as a last resort.
