Two answers on a "Who is this mail from?" card are for senders that are not vendors.

## Which one to pick
- **Marketing**: ads from a company we have never bought from. Their mail goes to **Offers & catalogs** in Mail, out of the way.
- **Other – not a vendor**: not a vendor and not ads, but someone who may need an answer (a customer, the bank, a neighbor). Their mail stays in **Needs attention** for you.

## How to
1. Open **Review queue** and find the sender under **Who is this mail from?**
2. Click **Marketing** or **Other – not a vendor**.

## Good to know
- The answer covers every email from that sender, now and later.
- You can give the same answers email by email in the sender's emails panel (see "Look at a sender's emails").
