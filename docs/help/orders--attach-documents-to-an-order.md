Keep the paper behind an order on the order itself: our order, the LS PO, the vendor's confirmation, the invoice, packing slips and payment proof.

## How to
1. Open the order and find the **Documents** box.
2. Click **Attach**.
3. Pick the **Type**: Our order, LS PO, Order confirmation, Invoice, Packing slip, Payment or Other.
4. Add a **Label** if you like, then choose a **File**, or paste a link in **or a link**.
5. Click **Save**.

## Good to know
- Click a document's name to open it. Click the trash can to remove it. A file Claude saved from an email has **from an email**, which opens that email.
- A confirmation or invoice you upload is checked by Claude against the order. The result shows in the **Paperwork** box in a minute or two.
- Files are also saved to the vendor's documents.
- Viewers can open documents but cannot add or remove them.
