Some vendors credit the freight back if we pay by a date. The dashboard card **Pay on time for the freight back** reminds you which orders to pay first so the credit is not lost.

## What it shows
- Unpaid orders that carry a freight allowance, soonest pay-by date first (up to six, then "and N more").
- Each row: the vendor, how much comes back (or "freight back" if the amount is not known yet) and the pay-by date.
- Dates in the last week, or already passed, are in red and the card is flagged.
- Click a row to open the order.

## Good to know
- The card only appears when at least one order has an allowance waiting.
- To add an allowance to an order, see "Freight allowance for paying on time" (Orders).
- To have a vendor's orders always show the reminder, tick **Credits the freight back when we pay on time** on the vendor's edit form.
- Once the order is marked paid, it leaves the card. Cancelled orders are left out.
