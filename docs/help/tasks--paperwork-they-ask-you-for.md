When an email asks the store to fill out, sign or return paperwork (a registration, an application, an agreement, a W-9), VMS makes a task for it right away, so it never sits unnoticed in the mailbox.

## What happens
- The task is named **Paperwork for [who]: [what they want]**, with the deadline when the email gives one. **Go to it** opens the email, with its forms attached.
- It reminds you at once, then every week, then 3 days before the deadline, then on the deadline, by dashboard, email or text as your reminder choices say. With no deadline it reminds weekly until done.
- It finishes itself when a reply with attachments goes out on that conversation. You can also tick it done yourself.
- billing@ asks go to Dana in Accounting › Tasks; orders@ asks go to whoever owns the conversation, else Dana.

## Good to know
- A bill, a newsletter, an offer or paperwork they send for your records does not make a task. Only a real ask of you does.
- If VMS missed one, open the email and add a task from it the usual way.
