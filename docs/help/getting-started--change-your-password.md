Change your password at any time while you are signed in.

## How to
1. Click your name or initials at the top right of the screen.
2. Click **Change password**.
3. Type the new password in **New password** and again in **Confirm new password**.
4. Click **Save password**.

## Good to know
- Use at least 8 characters. The two boxes must match.
- You stay signed in afterwards; VMS shows "Password updated. You are signed in."
