A vendor page shows what we have paid the vendor and, for WWD vendors, their WWD invoices. Each box appears only once there is something in it.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and scroll to **Payments** and **WWD invoices**.
2. In **Payments**, each line shows the date, invoice number, method and amount. Click **Order** to open the order it paid.
3. In **WWD invoices**, each line shows the **WWD Seq #**, date, the **Vendor Inv #**, our PO (tagged when it isn't ours), the amount and any discount, and when it was paid ("Not paid yet" in amber). **Show the lines** lists the items billed; a credit shows on the invoice it is for. When the invoice PDF is on file, click the **WWD Seq #** to open it.
4. To tie a WWD invoice to an order, click **Tie to an order** and pick the order. **Change order** picks another; **Untie** removes the link.
5. Click **Show all** when there are more than 8 invoices.
6. Near the vendor's name, a tag shows how often they use our PO, and **No longer with WWD** when Edenred says they have left.

## Good to know
- The top of the WWD invoices box shows how much is not paid yet.
- VMS ties WWD invoices to orders on its own when the PO or amount matches; those say "tied automatically" next to the order. Use **Change order** or **Untie** if it is wrong.
- Credits, debits and membership fees are tagged.
