**Mail for you** counts the conversations waiting on you: vendor replies to mail you own, and mail you sent that has had no answer.

## How to
1. On the dashboard, click **Mail for you**.
2. Each line shows the vendor, the subject, a bit of the message and how long it has waited.
3. Lines marked **No answer yet** (red) are emails you sent where the vendor has not replied by the follow-up date.
4. Click a line to open the conversation. Click **Open Mail** for the Mail page, or **See all** when there are many.

## Good to know
- The number turns red when anything is marked No answer yet.
- The follow-up wait (5 days to start with) is set by Dana in Settings > Mail.
- If you are ticked to see freight, freight mail is included too.
- The same number shows next to **Mail** in the side menu.
- Each morning from 8 AM, VMS sends you this list too, by email or text the way you get reminders (see **How I get reminders**). Click a line in the email to open that conversation.
