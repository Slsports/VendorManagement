The four big tabs split mail by what kind it is, the way Outlook had Focused and Other.

## How to
1. Click **Mail** in the menu.
2. Click one of the tabs:
   - **Primary**: real correspondence. Replies, reps and vendors writing to us, confirmations, invoices, shipping, questions.
   - **Promotions**: ads of any kind. Specials, what is new or in stock, price lists, catalogs and newsletters, even when a rep sends them from their own address.
   - **Freight**: mail from carriers (PartnerShip, Worldwide Express, Priority One, Pinnacle and others).
   - **Everything**: all mail, for searching.

## Good to know
- Mail opens on **Primary**.
- VMS sorts each email on its own, so a vendor's ads go to Promotions while their invoices and their rep's notes stay in Primary. When the rules are unsure, Claude reads it; anything still unsure stays in Primary.
- Conversations we wrote in always stay in Primary.
- Companies marked **Marketing** (ones we have never bought from) always go to Promotions.
- If something lands in the wrong tab, open it and click **Move to Promotions** (or **Move to Primary**). A move by anyone sorts it for everyone. See "Move mail to Promotions (or back)".
- Your own inbox has the same tabs.
- Price lists and catalogs from promotions are saved to the vendor's Documents on their own.
- Freight bills also wait on the Freight bills page.
