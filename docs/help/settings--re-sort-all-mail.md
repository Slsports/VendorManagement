Sorts every email into **Primary** or **Promotions** again, using what VMS knows now (for example after filing many senders).

## How to
1. Open **Settings** and click **Mail**.
2. Click **Re-sort all mail**.

## Good to know
- VMS says how many emails changed view, or "Everything was already sorted".
- Conversations you moved by hand stay where you put them.
