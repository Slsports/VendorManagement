One email per order tells the vendor what to do about freight. It lists the four freight terms (free, half, a cap, or we pay) and what each one means for them, with **Click Here** links to our freight form for that order. Our routing letter is attached, with that order's form link printed in it, so it still works if they pass the letter on.

## When it is made
- Once the vendor's confirmation has been checked against our order (the order is **Confirmed**), the Freight email waits in Trevor's **Mail › Drafts** with a task **Send the freight email: (vendor), PO …**. Nothing goes until someone presses **Send**.
- If the confirmation already shows free freight, no email is needed.

## Open it with one click
Click **Freight email** and the email opens ready to change or send:
- at the top of any order (Sent, Placed, Confirmed or Shipped)
- in the order's **Shipping & receiving** box
- on the vendor page, in the **Freight email** box, next to each open order
- on freight tasks (**Get freight quote**, **No shipment details yet**, **Check freight on the confirmation**, **Ship and receive**)
- next to an open order in the vendor's **Activity feed**

The same draft opens each time until it is sent. After it is sent, the button says **Send again**.

## After it is sent
- The order shows **Sent** with the date in **Shipping & receiving**, and VMS waits 3 business days before the follow-up email is ready for Trevor.
- Their reply is read by Claude: "ships free" or "confirming the cap" goes on the order; the form's answers become a freight quote for Trevor.
- The "click here to confirm you received our routing instructions" link records that person on **Freight › Routing letter**.

## Change the wording
Open **Templates** in the menu, find **Freight email (one per order)** and click **Edit**. New emails use it from then on.
