When a vendor has more than five documents, a search box appears above its folders.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and find **Documents**.
2. Type in **Find a document: invoice number, amount, name…** (for example an invoice number, or an amount like 125.50).
3. The matches show with their folder and year, each with **Open**, **Download**, **Rename**, **Move** and **Remove**.
4. Clear the box to go back to the folders.

## Good to know
- It searches the name, file name, document number, total, season and notes.
- A leading # or $ is ignored, so "#12345" and "12345" find the same thing.
- Numbers, dates and totals are read from scanned invoices in the bulk import, and show in bold beside the file.
