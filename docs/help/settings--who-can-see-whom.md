Dana chooses whose work each person can see. Trevor and Jarrett are set to see each other, so either can cover when the other is out.

## How to
1. Open **Settings** and click **Users**.
2. In the person's **Can see** column, tick the names they may see. Untick a name to take it away.
3. Next time that person opens VMS, their top bar has the **Showing** switch with those names.

## Good to know
- They see that person's dashboard, Tasks, Review queue and Orders, and can take or finish the work.
- Personal mail and Accounting are never shown this way, and a task tied to personal mail stays private.
- Only Dana changes these ticks. Dana always sees everyone.
