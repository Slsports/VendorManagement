Two kinds of freight cards come to the carrier's owner (Trevor) when Claude cannot finish on its own.

## A delivery receipt with no vendor
1. Read the carrier, PRO number, delivered date, shipper and PO. Click **Open the email and receipt** to look.
2. Pick the vendor in **Which vendor shipped it?**. The email and receipt are filed to that vendor and its order.

## A payment with no bill
1. Read the amount, date, method and invoice. Click **The email** or **The receipt** to look.
2. Pick the bill in **Which bill did it pay?**, then click **Mark this bill paid**.

## Good to know
- See "Delivery receipts" and "Bills marked paid automatically" (Freight) for what VMS does on its own.
