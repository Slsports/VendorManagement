When Claude is not sure whether a vendor is waiting on us to approve artwork, a proof or a design, it asks with a card.

## How to
1. Open **Review queue** and find **Artwork to approve?**
2. Read the sender, date, a bit of the email and Claude's note. Click **Open the email** to read it all.
3. Click **Yes, artwork to approve** to put it on the **Artwork approvals** card on the dashboard, or **Not artwork**.
