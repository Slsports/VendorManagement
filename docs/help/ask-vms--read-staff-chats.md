You can read every Ask VMS chat, to see whether your staff are asking the right questions to get the data they need.

## How to
1. Click **Ask VMS** in the menu.
2. Use the **Showing** box above the chat list: **my chats**, **everyone**, or one person.
3. Click a chat to read it. Only the person who started a chat can write in it.
4. Under each answer, **What I looked up** shows the steps Claude took and what that answer cost.

## Turn Ask VMS on or off for someone
1. Go to **Settings › Users**.
2. Tick or untick **Ask VMS** on their row. You always have it.

## Good to know
- Each person can spend $5 a day; the box stops for them at $5 until the next day.
- It uses Claude Opus 5.5, the stronger model, because buying questions need careful number work.
