Click a sender's name to see every email with them, newest first.

## How to
1. Open a conversation and click the sender's name at the top of a message. Outside addresses in the **To** and **Cc** line work too.
2. The **Mail with** page lists every email from, to or copied to that address.
3. Tick **Everyone at this company** to widen it to everyone at the same address ending (for example @acme.com).
4. Click any row to open its conversation. Click **Back** to return.

## Good to know
- Columns **Date**, **From / to**, **Subject** and **Vendor** sort when you click them.
- An arrow shows whether we sent it or they did.
- **Everyone at this company** is not offered for Gmail, Yahoo and similar free addresses.
- Our own addresses are not links.
