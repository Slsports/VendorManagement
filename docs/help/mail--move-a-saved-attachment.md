If Claude saved a file in the wrong folder, move it right from the email.

## How to
1. Open the email and find the green **Saved to …** tag beside the file.
2. Click the tag.
3. Pick the right **Folder** and **Year** and click **Move**.

## Good to know
- The tag updates to the new folder and year.
- Moving a file into Confirmations or Invoices starts Claude's order check; moving it out sets that check aside.
- You can also move it from the vendor's Documents.
