Every email sent from VMS ends with the Shaver Lake Sports logo and your signature. You can see it, and change it, before you send.

## How to
1. Write your email as usual; do not type a signature.
2. Under the message box is **Signature**, already filled in with your name, title and the store's details, with the logo above it.
3. To change it for this one email, type in the **Signature** box. Your usual signature stays the same for the next email.
4. Click **Send**. The signature goes under your message and above any earlier mail in a reply.

## Good to know
- The signature only shows orders@, never a personal email address.
- **Save draft** keeps the signature as you changed it.
- To change your usual name, title or direct phone, ask Dana. She sets them in **Settings > Mail**.
