Every email sent from VMS gets the sender's own signature at the bottom automatically.

## How to
1. Write your email as usual; do not type a signature.
2. Under the message box it says **Your signature is added when it sends.**
3. If it says no signature is on file yet, ask Dana. She adds signatures in **Settings > Mail**.

## Good to know
- Email from orders@ goes out as your name, and replies come back to orders@.
