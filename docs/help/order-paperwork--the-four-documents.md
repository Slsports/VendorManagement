Every order has four papers: **Our order**, the **LS PO**, the vendor's **Confirmation** and the **Invoice**. The **Paperwork** box on the order shows which ones are on file.

## How to
1. Open the order and find the **Paperwork** box on the right.
2. Each of the four shows a green tick when it is on file, or an empty circle when it is missing. A number in brackets means more than one is on file.
3. Below them, Claude's checks are listed. Click one to open it.

## Good to know
- To add a missing paper, use **Attach** in the **Documents** box just below (see "Attach a document to an order").
- Each paper has its own folder on the vendor's documents: Our orders, LS POs, Confirmations, Invoices.
- A PO we emailed the vendor from orders@ is saved to Our orders.
- Two checks are run: **Confirmation vs. our order** and **Invoice vs. confirmation**.
