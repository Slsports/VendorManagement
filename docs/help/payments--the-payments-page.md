The Payments page is where payment records come into VMS: Worldwide (WWD) payments and Bill.com payments. Each payment marks its freight bill or vendor order paid and stays as history on the vendor.

## How to
1. Click **Payments** in the menu (under Operations).
2. From top to bottom you find:
   - **Worldwide (WWD) payments**: load WWD's files and your payment sheets.
   - **WWD names to match** and **Freight to tie to a vendor**: only show when something needs you.
   - **WWD payments**: every WWD payment, newest first.
   - The **Bill.com** box: load Bill.com's Payments export.
   - **Recent payments**: the last 50 Bill.com payments.

## Good to know
- Only Dana (admin) and the managers (Trevor, Jarrett) see Payments in the menu.
- Nothing is saved from a file until you click **Import**. Importing the same file twice adds nothing.
