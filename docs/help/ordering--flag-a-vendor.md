Two flags put a vendor on the Ordering card: **Check if we need it** (Check stock) and **Remind me to order** (To order).

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page. In the **Ordering** box, click **Check if we need it** or **Remind me to order**.
   You can also click them on a "Does this need an answer?" card in the review queue (the email must be filed to a vendor).
2. Pick **Who handles it** (it starts with the vendor's assigned buyer, or you).
3. Add a **Note** if you like, for example "low on 26 inch saucers".
4. Click **Add to Ordering**.

## Good to know
- A vendor is on the card once. Flagging it again moves it to the other list.
- The vendor page shows where it is ("On the Ordering card: Check stock"). The **x** takes it off.
