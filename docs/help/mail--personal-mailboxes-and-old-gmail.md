Besides the shared orders@, Dana, Trevor and Jarrett each have their own mailbox in VMS, and Dana has the Old Gmail.

## How to
1. Click **Mail**, then **My mail** to see your own mailbox.
2. Reply and work it like Orders mail. New emails can go **From** **Me** instead of orders@.
3. Click **Share to Orders** on a conversation the team should see.

## Good to know
- Your mailbox is private: only you and Dana see it, until you share a conversation.
- Except vendor mail: when a known vendor, rep group or freight company writes to you, that conversation is shared to Orders by itself so the team and Claude can use it. Dana's own mailbox is left out; the Old Gmail is included.
- Only the owner can send from a personal mailbox. Dana can read it but not reply from it.
- Sending vendor business (an order, a return, a problem) from your own mailbox asks first whether to send it from orders@ instead.
- Every conversation in your mailbox is yours; vendor rules do not hand it to someone else.
- Claude reads it like orders@: it files mail to vendors, saves documents and spots artwork and replies.
- Private mail never makes cards in the Review queue, so nothing private shows there. The Team page counts Orders mail only.
- The **Old Gmail** is shaverlakesports@gmail.com, forwarded into VMS. Only Dana sees it. It has mail from when forwarding was turned on.
- Personal mailboxes brought in their last 90 days, then keep up every minute. Dana sees their status in **Settings > Mail**.
- The same email in orders@ and your mailbox is only filed once.
