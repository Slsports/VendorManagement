**My tasks** shows how many tasks are on your list, and turns red when one is late.

## How to
1. Click **My tasks** to see the list, or click **+** on the box to add a task.

## Good to know
- Reminders that are due appear at the very top of the dashboard.
- For everything about tasks, see the Tasks section.
