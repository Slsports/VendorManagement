The **Worldwide** tab holds our WWD member number, their main phone, website and notes, and their staff roster. It feeds the WWD contacts page everyone sees.

## How to
1. Open **Settings** and click **Worldwide**.
2. To change **Our member number**, **Main phone**, **Website** or **Notes**, type in the box and click away. It saves when you leave the box.
3. To add someone, click **Add person**, fill in **Name** (and Department, Title, Extension, Email if you have them), tick **Key contact** if wanted, and click **Save**.
4. Tick the box at the start of a person's line to make them a key contact; untick to undo.
5. To remove someone, click the bin icon on their line and confirm.
6. Type in **Filter…** to find a person.

## Good to know
- Key contacts are listed first on the WWD contacts page.
- The roster is grouped by department.
- The WWD contacts page has an **Edit the roster in Settings** link for Dana.
