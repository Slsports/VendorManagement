The **Bill.com** card on the dashboard (for people who see Accounting) shows two things from Bill.com, refreshed every 15 minutes.

## What it shows
- **Bills due within 7 days** (and anything overdue, in red): vendor, invoice number, amount and due date, read from Bill.com. Each one also becomes a pay-by task, "Pay [vendor] $[amount] by [date] (Bill.com)", which closes itself once Bill.com shows it paid or scheduled.
- **Sent to Bill.com's inbox, not entered yet**: invoices VMS forwarded that are not a bill in Bill.com yet. Click the vendor to open the email.

## Good to know
- **Open Bill.com** opens the Bill.com site. **Settings** opens Settings › Bill.com.
- Paying happens in Bill.com, never in VMS. **I paid it** on the task just closes the task.
