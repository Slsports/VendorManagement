Each conversation has one owner. It lands on that person's Mail list and dashboard until it is answered or handled.

## How to
1. Open the conversation.
2. In **Owner**, pick the person. A message confirms "Handed to …".
3. To clear it, pick no one.

## Good to know
- Everyone can still see every Orders conversation; the owner only decides whose list it lands on.
- Later replies in the conversation go to the owner.
- Sending from VMS makes you the owner.
- New mail gets an owner on its own (see "Who each email goes to").
- Mail in a personal mailbox always belongs to that mailbox's owner.
