Every column in the mail list sorts when you click its header.

## How to
1. On the Mail page, click a column header: **From**, **Subject**, **Vendor**, **Owner**, **Status** or **Last**.
2. Click the same header again to reverse it. The arrow shows which way it is sorted.

## Good to know
- The list starts newest first.
- **Status** sorts Needs an answer first, then No answer yet, Waiting on vendor, Handled.
- Conversations back from snooze always sit at the top until opened.
- On a phone, the Vendor and Owner columns are hidden to save room.
