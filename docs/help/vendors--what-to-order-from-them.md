The **What to order from them** box lists product types and single items to reorder first, keep, watch or stop. (The standing says whether to order from the vendor at all.)

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and find **What to order from them**.
2. Click **Add rule**.
3. Choose **Product type** or **One item**. For one item, type its **Vendor ID**.
4. Type the product type (for example "Squish") or the item name.
5. Pick the rule: **Reorder first**, **Keep ordering**, **Watch** or **Stop ordering**.
6. Write why in one line, then click **Save**.

## Good to know
- Rules are grouped into **Product types** and **Items by Vendor ID**, best first.
- To remove a rule, click the trash can on its row.
- The box says where the rules came from (for example Dana's analysis, or "Added in VMS").
- Viewers only see this box when a vendor has rules.
