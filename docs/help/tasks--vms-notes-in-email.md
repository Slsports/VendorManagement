Forward an email to yourself or to orders@ with a note starting **VMS:**, and VMS makes the reminders for you, with the email to the vendor ready to send on each date.

## How to
1. Forward the vendor's email (for example, their backorder list) to your own address (dana@, trevor@, jarrett@) or to orders@.
2. On the first line, before anything else, write **VMS:** and what you want, for example:
   *VMS: Please create a task reminder and an email to check on these backorder items on Nov 1, 2026, Dec 15, 2026, Jan 30, 2027 and Mar 21, 2027.*
3. Send it. Within a minute or two you get an email from VMS saying what it made: one task per date, each reminding you at 9 am that day, tagged to the vendor and linked to your email.
4. On the day, open the task and click **Open the email to send**. Look it over and click **Send**. Sending it ticks that task done.

## Good to know
- Only a note that **starts with "VMS:"**, from you, Trevor or Jarrett, sent to one of our own mailboxes counts. Mail from anyone else, or "VMS:" in an email to a vendor, does nothing.
- If VMS can't tell the vendor or the dates, it emails you one question instead of guessing. Forward the email again with **VMS:** and the missing part.
- The email goes to whoever sent the original email, or else the vendor's rep. With no address on file, the task says so; add the rep on the vendor page.
- When the vendor answers one of these emails that everything shipped (or won't come), VMS closes the rest of the reminders and emails you why. They are under **Tasks › Closed by VMS** so you can check.
- Notes sent to the Billing (accounting) mailbox are not read.
- Mail from your own mailbox stays private: only you (and Dana) see the tasks and emails.
