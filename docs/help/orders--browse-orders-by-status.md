The Orders page lists every order, from placement to payment. Tabs across the top let you look at one status at a time.

## How to
1. Click **Orders** in the menu.
2. Click a tab at the top: **All**; **On order**, which is every order sent and never received, whatever its stage; or one status such as **Sent**, **Shipped** or **Ready to pay**. Each tab shows how many orders it holds.
3. Click the date in the **Ordered** column to open that order. Click the vendor name to open the vendor.

## Good to know
- Only statuses that have orders get a tab, so the row stays short.
- VMS closes an order out on its own, every hour, so **On order** stays true: a Worldwide invoice for the shipment or the vendor's invoice on file makes it **Received** as of that invoice's date; when all its Worldwide invoices are paid, or Bill.com shows the bill paid, it is **Paid** as of the payment. The order's notes say which paper did it.
- You can also jump straight to a status from the menu: pick it from the list under **Orders**.
- Each row shows the order date (and the show it came from, with a "?" when the show was guessed from the date), vendor, status, what was ordered and the PO, store, cost, ship date and paid date.
- Under the cost you see **Free freight**, **1/2 freight** (with the freight when known), **Freight capped** at a percent, or the freight charged ("+$45.50 freight"). Blank when nothing is known yet; set it in the order's **Shipping** box.
- The list shows the first 500 orders that match. Use the search box to narrow it if you need more.
