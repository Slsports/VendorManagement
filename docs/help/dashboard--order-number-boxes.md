Two number boxes count orders to watch: **Payments due** and **Future-dated orders**. Click one to see the orders. Open, placed, confirmed and overdue orders are on the **Ordering** card (see "The Ordering card").

## How to
1. On the dashboard, click a number box.
2. The list opens with each order's vendor, PO number, what it is, ship date, estimated cost and status.
3. Click an order to open it, or **Open on the Orders page** to sort and search the same list.

## What each box counts
- **Payments due**: entered in LS, not yet paid (Entered or Ready to pay).
- **Future-dated orders**: pre-bookings shipping more than 30 days from now.

## Good to know
- These boxes count every order in the company, whoever placed it.
