Four number boxes count orders: **Open orders**, **Overdue orders**, **Payments due** and **Future-dated orders**. Click one to see the orders.

## How to
1. On the dashboard, click a number box.
2. The list opens with each order's vendor, PO number, what it is, ship date, estimated cost and status.
3. Click an order to open it.
4. Click **Open on the Orders page** to see the same list on the Orders page, where you can sort and search it.

## What each box counts
- **Open orders**: placed, not yet received (Open, Awaiting confirmation, Confirmed or Shipped).
- **Overdue orders**: not received and more than 30 days past their ship date. The number is red when there are any.
- **Payments due**: entered in LS, not yet paid (Entered or Ready to pay).
- **Future-dated orders**: pre-bookings shipping more than 30 days from now.

## Good to know
- These boxes count every order in the company, whoever placed it.
- The list shows the first 25; "and … more" opens the rest on the Orders page.
