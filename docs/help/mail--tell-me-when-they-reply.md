Waiting to hear back from someone? VMS can tell you the moment they write.

## How to
1. Open the email from (or to) that person.
2. Click **Tell me when they reply** in the row of buttons at the top.
3. A yellow note says **Watching for a reply from [name]**.

When their next email comes in, VMS gives you a task, **Reply from [name]**, with a reminder right away: on your dashboard, by email and/or by text, however you set your reminders. Click it and their reply opens.

## Good to know
- Any email from them counts, even in a new conversation.
- It tells you once. To keep watching after that, click the button again on their reply.
- Changed your mind? Click **Stop watching** on the yellow note.
- Only you are told. Each person sets their own.
- A reply that lands in someone else's private mail doesn't count, since you can't open it.
