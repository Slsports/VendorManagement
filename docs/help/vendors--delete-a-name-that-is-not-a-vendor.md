Some names came in from LS or the mailbox that were never vendors (a carrier, an internal account). Delete them from a review card.

## How to
1. Open the review card about that vendor (in the **Review queue**, or on the vendor's page).
2. Click **Not a vendor, delete it** at the bottom right of the card.
3. Click **OK** to confirm.

## Good to know
- The vendor disappears from VMS completely, and its name is remembered so imports never bring it back.
- Vendors with orders are never deleted. For a vendor you simply stopped using, make it inactive instead.
- Deleted by mistake? Dana can go to **Settings**, **Organization**, find **Deleted as not a vendor** and click **Allow again**. The name can then be added or imported again.
