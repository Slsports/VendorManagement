Snooze hides a conversation from your lists until a time you choose, then brings it back to the top.

## How to
1. In the Mail list, click the alarm clock on a row. Or open the conversation and click **Snooze**.
2. Pick **Later today (3 hours)**, **Tomorrow morning**, **In 2 days** or **Next Monday**.
3. Or choose a time under **Pick a date and time** and click **Snooze until then**.
4. To bring it back early, open the same menu and click **Unsnooze now**.

## Good to know
- Mornings mean 8 AM.
- A snooze is yours only: it leaves your mail lists, dashboard and Mail count, not anyone else's.
- When the time comes, it returns at the top of your list marked **Back from snooze** until you open it.
- A reply from the vendor ends the snooze at once.
- The **Snoozed** tab lists yours; with **Everyone** picked it shows everyone's, with who and until when.
- You can snooze several ticked rows together.
