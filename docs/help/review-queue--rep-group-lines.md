When mail comes from one of our rep groups, Claude reads which lines (brands) the rep sells us. Vendors with no rep group are added to that rep group on their own. Two cases come to you as a **Rep group lines** card in the Review queue (**Vendor changes** tab).

## How to
1. Open **Review queue** and click **Vendor changes**.
2. Click **See the mail** to read the rep's emails the card came from.
3. For a **Rep group lines** card:
   - The vendor is already with another rep group: click **Move to (new rep group)** or **Keep with (old rep group)**.
   - The rep sells a brand that is not one of our vendors: click **Add as a vendor** (it joins the rep group) or **Skip**.

## Good to know
- A rep group is recognized by its email, any of its reps' emails, or Claude reading the name or signature. The people writing from it are added to the rep group's contacts.
- Each rep email is tagged to every line it names, and filed to the vendor when it names just one. It shows on each of those vendors' pages.
- Claude looks again after every 10 new emails from the rep, so new lines are picked up.
- A card for a brand that "is not one of our vendors" changes when one of our vendors gets that brand as another name (see "Edit a vendor"). It then says the brand is our vendor under another name, with **Add (vendor) to (rep group)** or **Skip**.
