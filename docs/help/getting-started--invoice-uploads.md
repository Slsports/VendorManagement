Take photos of a paper invoice, or pick a PDF or photo, and press **Submit**. VMS reads each invoice, sees which store it is for, and files it to the vendor.

## How to upload
1. Open **Invoice Uploads**. An Invoice Uploads login lands there straight away; others find it in the menu.
2. Click **Take photos** to use the camera (one photo per page; click **Take another page** for the next), or **Choose files** to pick a PDF or photo from the phone's or iPad's files, or from the computer. On a computer you can also drop files on the box.
3. Check the pages. Click the **×** on a page to remove it, or **Start over**.
4. Click **Submit**.

## What happens next
- Your upload shows under **My uploads**, in a folder for today. It says **Reading…** for a minute or two.
- Each invoice then shows its vendor, number, store, date and total, and:
  - **Filed to Invoices**: it is in the vendor's Documents.
  - **Sent to Bill.com**: for vendors whose invoices go to Bill.com.
  - **Needs a look: the office will file it**: VMS could not tell the vendor or the store, or the scan is unclear. Nothing more to do.
  - **Already submitted by (name) on (date)**: someone already sent this invoice. It is not saved twice.
- Click any line to see the file.

## Good to know
- You never pick the store. VMS reads it from the invoice, even when the vendor calls our stores by its own names (see the vendor's **Invoice alias**).
- Several invoices can go in one upload; VMS splits them.
- An Invoice Uploads login sees only this page and only what that person uploaded.
