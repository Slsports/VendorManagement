A rep often reps several companies, so one email from them may be about two or three vendors. VMS keeps every conversation with a rep group in one place on the rep group's page, and files each conversation to the vendors it is about so it also shows under those vendors.

## Where to look
- On the rep group's page, the **Mail** box lists every conversation with that group's people: their emails to us and ours to them, newest first. Click the subject to open it.
- Under each conversation, **filed to** names the vendors it is about. Click a name to go to that vendor; the same conversation is in that vendor's Mail box.
- **not filed to a vendor yet** means Claude has not read it for vendors yet, or it names none. Reading happens in the background, a few emails a minute, oldest history included.

## Good to know
- Claude reads the words of the email for vendor names. An email about "the Toysmith order" files to Toysmith even if the rep group reps twenty companies.
- Conversations you start with a rep are filed the same way, from your own words, before the rep replies.
- A rep's person has to be on the rep group's page (under its people, with their email) for our outgoing mail to them to count.
