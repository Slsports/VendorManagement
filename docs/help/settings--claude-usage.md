The **Claude this month** box on the Mail tab shows what Claude's mail reading has cost so far this month.

## How to
1. Open **Settings** and click **Mail**.
2. Look at **Claude this month**: the total, how many emails and senders were read, and the split between **Sorting unclear emails** and **Reading unknown senders**.

## Good to know
- The monthly limit is $25, set outside VMS in the Claude Console.
- "No Claude use yet this month" means nothing has been read yet.
