If an ad landed in Needs attention, or a real email landed in Offers, move it. VMS learns from it.

## How to
1. Open the conversation.
2. Click **Move to Offers & catalogs**, or **Move to Needs attention** if it is in Offers.

## Good to know
- The sender's other emails move too, and a message says how many. Their new mail follows.
- A small **Offers & catalogs** tag shows at the top of a conversation that is in Offers.
