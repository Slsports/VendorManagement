When you decide to buy from a line, turn it into a vendor in one click.

## How to
1. Find the line on the **Lines** page or under **Other lines they carry** on a rep group's page.
2. Click **Make a vendor**.
3. Click **OK** to confirm.

## Good to know
- The new vendor keeps the rep group, route and catalog link, and its page opens.
- Lines never become vendors on their own.
