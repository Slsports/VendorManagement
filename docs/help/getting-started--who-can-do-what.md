Each login has a role. The role decides which pages you see in the menu and whether you can change things. Your role shows under your name in the account menu.

## The roles
- **Administrator** (Dana): everything. Only the administrator sees **Team** and **Settings**, and only she can choose anyone in the **Showing** switch.
- **Manager** (Trevor, Jarrett): everything except Team and Settings, including **Payments**.
- **Buyer**: can add and change vendors, orders, mail, freight bills, rep groups and lines, and settle the review queue. No Payments, Team or Settings.
- **Viewer**: can look at vendors, orders, mail and the rest, but cannot change anything. No Review queue, Payments, Team or Settings.
- **Invoice uploader**: only sees the **Upload invoice** page.

## Good to know
- If you open a page your role can't use, VMS says "You don't have access to this page". Click **Go back home**.
- Only Dana is an administrator. There is no screen in VMS yet to add people or change roles (Settings > Users is coming).
- Personal mailboxes are private: only the owner and Dana see them.
- Everyone sees their own dashboard, mail and tasks; Dana can look at anyone's with **Showing**, and people she ticks under **Can see** (Trevor and Jarrett see each other) get the switch for those names.
