Save any attachment from a vendor's email into that vendor's folders.

## How to
1. Open the conversation in **Mail**. It must be filed to a vendor.
2. Beside the attachment, click **Save to documents**.
3. Check the **Folder** (VMS guesses it from the file name) and the **Year** (the year of the email).
4. Click **Save**.

## Good to know
- The attachment then shows a green "Saved to Folder › Year" tag instead of the button. Click the tag to move it if it is in the wrong place.
- Saving to **Confirmations** or **Invoices** starts Claude's check against the order.
- For approved artwork, save the final proof to **Approved proofs**.
