Procedures are the handbook: how each bookkeeping job is done, in plain steps, so someone who has never done it could follow along. Tasks open their procedure.

## How to
1. Click **Accounting** in the menu, then **Procedures**. They are grouped by category.
2. Click one to read it. **Edit** changes it; **Delete** removes it.
3. **Write a procedure**: give it a title, a category and a one-line summary, then write the steps. Lines starting with ## are headings, 1. 2. 3. are steps, - are bullets, **text** is bold. The right side shows how it reads.
4. On a task, pick the **Procedure** so the steps are one click away.

## Good to know
- Write it for your replacement: when it happens, who to call (with numbers), which account pays, what to check, and what has gone wrong before.
- Send Claude a document (a guide, a spreadsheet, a letter) and it drafts a procedure for you to fix.
- The first two are the property insurance renewal and sending proof of insurance.
