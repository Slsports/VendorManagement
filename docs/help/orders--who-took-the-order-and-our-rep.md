The **Reps** box on an order shows who wrote the order (often the rep at a show) and our assigned rep, who gets the follow-ups.

## How to
1. Open the order.
2. In the **Reps** box, click the pencil beside **Order taken by**.
3. Type the name (VMS suggests the vendor's people as you type) and click **Save**.

## Good to know
- **Our assigned rep (follow-ups)** is the contact starred on the vendor page, with their email to click. To change it, star a different contact on the vendor page.
- If no one is starred, the box says so.
- You can also fill in Order taken by when you add an order.
