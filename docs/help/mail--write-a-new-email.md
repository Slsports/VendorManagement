Start a fresh email to a vendor, a rep or anyone else from inside VMS.

## How to
1. On the Mail page, click **New email**.
2. Pick **From**: **Orders (orders@)**, **Me** (your own mailbox) or, if you have the Accounting tick, **Billing (billing@)**. People without their own mailbox or the Accounting tick always send from orders@.
3. In **To**, start typing a name, an email or a company ("jarr", "Worldwide", "Smith"). The matching people pop up; click one, or use the arrow keys and Enter, and their address goes in. Keep typing for the next one, or type a full address yourself.
4. To copy someone, do the same in **Cc**, for example to copy Worldwide on a vendor email. Then add the **Subject** and **Message**.
5. Attach files if you like (see "Attach files to an email").
6. Click **Send**. Press Esc or **Cancel** to close without sending.

## Good to know
- Your signature shows under the message with the logo; change it there for this email if you like.
- The people that pop up are our own staff, the Worldwide contacts, rep group reps and every vendor contact. Contacts of an inactive vendor say "(inactive)"; people who have left (former) never come up.
- You can also email from a vendor's page (the Email button beside each address), and that email is filed to the vendor at once.
- Clicking an email address inside a message also starts a new email.
