Conversations you flagged **Working on order** in Mail, while an order goes back and forth with the rep.

## How to
1. On the dashboard, click **Orders I'm working on**.
2. Each line shows the vendor and subject; "needs an answer" (amber) means the rep wrote last.
3. Click a line to open the conversation, or **Open the list** for the full working-orders page.

## Good to know
- To add one, open the conversation in Mail and flag it **Working on order**.
- With **Showing** set to someone else, the card reads "Orders Trevor is working on".
