If an order is filed under the wrong vendor (for example a Worldwide Warehouse order, once the invoice names the real vendor), move it to the right one.

## How to
1. Open the order.
2. Click **Change vendor** at the top right.
3. Start typing the right vendor's name in the box (**The real vendor**) and pick it from the list.
4. A message confirms "Order moved to …". Click **Cancel** instead if you change your mind.

## Good to know
- The order's documents and its emails move with it to the new vendor.
- The move is recorded in the activity log.
