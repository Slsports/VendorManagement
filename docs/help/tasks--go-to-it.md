Click a task and VMS takes you to where the work is done, already on the step.

## Where each task takes you
- **Create PO: [vendor], [their number]**: the order, with the **Create PO in LS** box at the top and highlighted. If VMS doesn't know the order yet, the confirmation check opens instead, with Claude's best guess already picked; click **Use this order**.
- **Reply** tasks from your own promises ("I'll look at this by tomorrow"): the email, with your reply already open.
- **Get freight quote**: the freight quote.
- **Approve / Review the LS PO**: the LS PO.
- **Approve the proof from [vendor]**: the vendor's Proofs folder, open. **Credit from [vendor]**: the vendor's Credits folder, open.
- **Pay [vendor] by…**: the task box opens with the bill, **Pay in Edenred** or **Pay in Bill.com**, and **I paid it** (see "Pay-by reminders").
- Tasks you made yourself: whatever they're tagged to (the order, the email, then the vendor).

## Good to know
- Click **Edit** next to a task to change its due date, reminder, who it's for or what it's about.
- Inside the task box, **Go to it** does the same, and each tag under **About** opens what it's tagged to.
- This works from **My tasks** on the dashboard, the **Tasks** page and the reminders at the top of the dashboard.
