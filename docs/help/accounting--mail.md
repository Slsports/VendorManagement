The billing@ mail works like the Orders mail, sorted by who it is with instead of by vendor.

## How to
1. Click **Accounting** in the menu, then **Mail** (or **Open the mail** on the overview).
2. Pick a view: **Primary** for real correspondence, **Promotions** for ads, **Everything** to search, **Sent** for what went out.
3. Pick where it stands: **Needs an answer**, **No answer yet**, **Waiting on them**, **All open**, **Handled**.
4. Narrow it to a kind of contact with the round buttons: **Taxes**, **Insurance**, **Banking** and so on. **No contact yet** shows mail from people not on the contacts list.
5. Click a subject to open the conversation. Reply, forward and mark handled as in Orders mail; replies go out from billing@.
6. **New email** writes a fresh email from billing@.

## Good to know
- The **Who** column is the contact the conversation is with. Open a conversation to put it on a different contact.
- The category buttons follow the contact's category, so confirming contacts with the right category is what makes them useful.
- This mail never shows on the Orders Mail page and cannot be shared there.
