Make a to-do tied to a conversation, so the task links back to the email.

## How to
1. Open the conversation.
2. Click **Task**.
3. Fill in the task and click **Add task**.

## Good to know
- The task is already tagged to this email and its vendor.
- Open tasks about the email are listed near the top of the conversation.
