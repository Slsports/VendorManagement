Each person picks how they get task reminders: on the dashboard, by email, by text, or any mix.

## How to
1. Click your name at the top right, then **Notifications**. (Dana can also use **Settings → Notifications**. The Tasks page has a link too.)
2. Find the **How I get reminders** box.
3. Tick **On my dashboard**, **By email** and/or **By text**.
4. For texts, type your **Cell number**. The small print under it says what you are agreeing to: reminder texts only, how often, message and data rates may apply, reply STOP or untick **By text** to stop, with links to the Terms and Privacy Policy.
5. Under **Text me only between**, keep 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM, or set your own hours.
6. Under **Text me about**, tick the kinds of reminders you want by text: **Price lists and catalogs arrived**, **Payments due and discounts**, **Insurance and contracts**, **Orders and shipping**, **Freight**, **Proofs and artwork**, **Credits and returns**, **Questions waiting on you**, **Tasks from a person** and **Weekly and admin work**. Everything you leave unticked still comes to your dashboard and email.
7. Click **Yes, sign me up for texts** (it says **Save** when you are only changing the other boxes).

## Good to know
- To change it for one task, open the task and pick **Text me**: **As my text settings say**, **Always text me about this task** or **Never text me about this task** (it shows once the task has a reminder).
- When a rep answers your email asking for a price list, catalog or order writer and sends one, you get a task **[Vendor] sent the price list you asked for** that opens their reply. It goes to whoever asked (from the name signing the email), so you only get your own. It is in **Price lists and catalogs arrived**.
- Everyone starts with dashboard and email.
- Texts only come in your texting hours (8 AM to 6 PM unless you change them). A reminder due at night still shows on your dashboard and in email right away; its text waits until your start time, and is skipped if the task is done by then.
- The same choices are used when someone finishes a task you gave them.
- Texts start working once the texting service is set up; until then the Tasks page shows "not all sent" under the reminder time.
- The public page vms.shaverlakesports.com/sms/ explains the text opt-in (needed by the texting service).
- **By text** starts unticked for everyone; nobody gets texts until they tick it themselves and enter their own number.
- Every morning from 8 AM you also get one message listing the orders@ emails waiting on you (the same ones as **Mail for you**), with a link to each. It comes by email, text or both, the same way as your reminders. No message on a morning with nothing waiting.
