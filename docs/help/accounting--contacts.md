Accounting contacts are the people and companies in the billing@ mail: the accounting firm, insurance agents, banks, payroll, utilities and the rest. VMS finds them in the mail; you confirm them and say what kind of contact each one is.

## How to
1. Click **Accounting** in the menu, then **Contacts**.
2. **To confirm** lists who VMS found. Click a name to open the card.
3. Fix the **Company** and **Person**, add the **Role** (agent, CPA, banker…), pick the **Category** and click **Confirm and save**. Tick ✓ in the list to confirm one as it is, or ✕ to ignore it.
4. **Add contact** makes one by hand. Put their email addresses one per line; a bare company domain (for example mbsaccountancy.com) means everyone at that company.
5. **Find new in the mail** looks through the mail again after more has come in. VMS also does this on its own as mail arrives.

## Good to know
- The category sorts their mail under Accounting → Mail and, later, decides where their documents are filed.
- Same person or company twice? On one card, pick the other under **Same person or company twice?** and click **Merge**. Emails, conversations and counts move over.
- Ignored contacts stay under **Ignored** and can be brought back. Newsletters and one-off senders are never proposed.
- When you change a contact's email addresses, every billing@ conversation is matched again, old mail included.
