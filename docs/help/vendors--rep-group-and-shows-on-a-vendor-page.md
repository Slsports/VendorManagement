Two boxes on a vendor page show who reps the vendor and which Worldwide shows they were at.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page.
2. In **Rep group**, click the group's name to open its page. Under **Also reps** you see the other vendors that group carries, and how many other lines. Click **See everything they carry** for the full list.
3. In **Worldwide shows**, read each show the vendor was at, with the booth number, the exhibitor name, a **new exhibitor** tag, and who shared the booth. Click a booth mate to open it.

## Good to know
- To set or change the rep group, click **Edit** and use the **Rep group** box (it also has **+ Add new rep group…**).
- "Not on a show list we have loaded" does not mean the vendor is not WWD.
