Put the dashboard boxes and cards in the order you like, and hide the ones you don't use. Only you see your arrangement.

## How to
1. On the dashboard, click **Customize** (top right).
2. A list of every box and card appears, top to bottom.
3. Click the up or down arrow next to one to move it.
4. Untick a box to hide it; tick it again to bring it back.
5. Click **Done**.

## Good to know
- Changes save as you make them, to your login, so they follow you to any computer or phone.
- Anything can go anywhere, including cards above the number boxes. Number boxes next to each other share a row of four; cards go three to a row.
- **Reset to the standard dashboard** puts everything back the way it came.
- Cards added to VMS later show up after yours, in the standard order.
- Some cards only show when they have something in them, even if ticked.
