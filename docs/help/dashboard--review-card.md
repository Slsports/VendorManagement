One card across the dashboard counts everything waiting on a decision, by kind. It replaced the old "Confirmations & invoices to review" and "Review queue" cards.

## How to
1. On the dashboard, find **Review**.
2. The card starts closed with one number (everything waiting). Click it to open; it stays open until you close it. Read the counts:
   - **Confirmations**: vendor confirmations Claude checked against our order.
   - **Invoices**: invoices Claude checked against the confirmation.
   - **Needs a hand**: paperwork Claude could not read or could not match to an order.
   - **Needs an answer?**: emails Claude is not sure need an answer, and artwork it is not sure about.
   - **Who is this?**: senders Claude could not place.
   - **Vendor changes**: possible duplicates, name and category clean-ups, Lightspeed markers.
   - **Freight**: delivery receipts and freight Claude could not match.
3. Click a count. The **Review queue** opens on that tab.

## Good to know
- Under a count, "**3 with you**" in amber is how many are yours. It follows the **Showing** switch: Dana can see "with Trevor" or, set to Everyone, just the totals.
- A paperwork count turns red when something has waited 2 days or more.
- Items you snoozed don't count until they come back.
- **Open the review queue** at the top right opens the whole queue.
