The top of the **Mail** tab shows whether orders@ is syncing and how much of the mail is filed.

## How to
1. Open **Settings** and click **Mail**.
2. Read **Last checked** (it checks every minute) and any red **Last problem** message.
3. Click **Sync now** to check for new mail right away.

## Good to know
- **Emails in VMS**: how many emails have been loaded.
- **Filed to a vendor**: the share of emails matched to a vendor.
- **Senders to identify**: senders waiting in the review queue under "Who is this mail from?". One answer files all their mail, now and later.
- **First load**: whether the 12 months of past mail has finished loading.
