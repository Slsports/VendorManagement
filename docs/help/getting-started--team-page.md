The Team page shows, for each person, what is waiting on them and for how long.

## How to
1. Click **Team** in the menu (under Overview).
2. Read across each person's row: **Needs an answer**, **No answer yet**, **Review items**, **Working on**, **Vendors** and **Last email sent**.
3. Click a number to open that list as that person sees it. The **Showing** switch changes to them.
4. Click a person's name to open their dashboard.
5. When done, click **Back to mine** on the purple bar.

## Good to know
- A number in red means something has waited more than 3 days; "oldest" shows how long.
- **Needs an answer**: vendor replies waiting on them. **No answer yet**: mail they sent with no reply past the follow-up date.
- **Working on**: conversations they flagged "Working on order", with how many need an answer.
- **Last email sent** counts mail sent from VMS only. Personal mailboxes are not counted.
