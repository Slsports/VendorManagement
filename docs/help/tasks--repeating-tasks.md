A repeating task comes back on its own: when you check it off, the next one is created with its next due date.

## How to
1. In the task, open **Repeat**.
2. Pick **Every week**, **Every month** or **Every year**, or **Custom…**.
3. For **Custom…**: type how many and pick **days**, **weeks**, **months** or **years** (for example every 2 weeks).
   With weeks you can also pick the days, for example **Mon** and **Thu**.
4. Optional: set **Stop repeating after** to a last date.
5. Click **Add task** or **Save**.

## Good to know
- A repeating task needs a due date; with none, it counts from today.
- The reminder moves with it: reminded 1 day before the due date stays 1 day before.
- Finished late? Dates that already went by are skipped.
- To stop it, change **Repeat** to **Doesn't repeat**, or delete the task.
