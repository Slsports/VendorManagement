One signature template is used for everyone, with the logo above it. Each person's name, title and direct phone fill it in.

## How to
1. Open **Settings** and click **Mail**.
2. Find **Signatures**. Change the **Template for everyone** box if needed; {name}, {title} and {direct} fill in for each person. **Looks like** shows the result.
3. Click **Save template**.
4. Below, click a person's name to open it. Fill in **Name**, **Title** and **Direct phone**, then click **Save**.
5. For someone who wants a different signature, tick **Use their own version instead of the template** and type it.

## Good to know
- Leave **Direct phone** empty and that part is left out.
- Anyone can still change the signature on a single email before sending it.
- The signature says Shaver Lake Sports (no "Inc"), with orders@ and the website. No Facebook links.
