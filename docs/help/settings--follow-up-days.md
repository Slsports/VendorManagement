When someone emails a vendor from VMS and gets no answer after this many days, the conversation comes back to their dashboard as **No answer yet**.

## How to
1. Open **Settings** and click **Mail**.
2. Find **Follow-up**.
3. Type a number in **Days to wait** (1 to 60).
4. Click **Save**.

## Good to know
- It starts at 5 days.
- It applies to everyone.
