Most pages have a short paragraph under their title that says what the page is for. You can change it right on the page and see it as you type.

## How to
1. Open the page.
2. Click the small pencil at the end of the paragraph under the title.
3. Change the words in the box.
4. Click **Save**. Everyone sees your wording from then on.

## Put the original back
Click the pencil, then **Use the original**.

## Good to know
- Only the admin sees the pencil.
- Pages about one record, like a single vendor or order, build their paragraph from that record, so they have no pencil.
- If the built-in wording changes later, a page you changed keeps your wording.
