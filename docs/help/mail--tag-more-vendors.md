One email can be about several vendors (a Worldwide pallet email can name five). Tagging shows it on each vendor's page.

## How to
1. Open the conversation.
2. In the **Also about** box, click **Also tag a vendor**.
3. Pick the vendor (or **+ Add new vendor**).
4. To remove one, click the **x** on its name.

## Good to know
- Mail from services, carriers and rep groups is tagged to every vendor it names on its own.
- The conversation stays filed to its main vendor; tags are extra.
