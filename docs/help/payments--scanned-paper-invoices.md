Old paper invoices that were scanned (back to 2010) can be loaded in bulk. Claude reads every page, splits a stack into separate documents and files each with the right vendor.

## How to
1. Click **Vendors** in the menu, then **Bulk import documents**.
2. Tick **Scanned paper** before adding files.
3. Click **Choose a folder** or **Choose files**, or drop them in the box.
4. Wait while Claude reads the scans (a progress bar shows). A stack becomes one file per document, with its page numbers.
5. Check **Need a look**: faded, handwritten or unclear pages, or a vendor name VMS does not know. Pick the vendor and folder, or click **Looks right**.
6. Click **File N documents**.

## Good to know
- Each document gets its vendor, folder (Invoices, Credits, Shipping for packing slips...), number, date and total. The date sets the year folder.
- Each vendor's Documents has a search box to find a scan by number, amount or name.
- Files over 50 MB are left out. Files already in VMS are skipped.
