VMS works on a phone. The side menu is tucked away behind a menu button so the page has the whole screen.

## How to
1. Tap the menu button (three lines) at the top left.
2. The menu slides in from the left. Tap the page you want; the menu closes by itself.
3. To close it without choosing, tap the **X** or tap the dimmed page beside it.

## Good to know
- The search box is replaced by a magnifying glass at the top. It opens the Search page, which is not ready yet; to find a vendor on a phone, open **Vendors** and use its search box.
- Your account menu (change password, sign out) is your initials at the top right.
- Long edit forms keep **Save** and **Cancel** pinned to the bottom of the screen.
- Wide lists can be slid sideways with your finger.
- Dana's **Showing** switch appears as a small bar just under the top bar on a phone.
