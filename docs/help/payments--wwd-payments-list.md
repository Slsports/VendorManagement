**WWD payments** on the Payments page is one place for every Worldwide payment, with what each one paid.

## How to
1. Click **Payments** in the menu and find **WWD payments**.
2. Click any header (**Date**, **WWD payment #**, **Vendors**, **Discounts**, **Amount**) to sort; click again to reverse.
3. Click a payment to open it. You see each vendor it paid, with WWD #, their invoice #, a link to the order, the invoice amount, any discount, credits and the membership fee, and the **Total paid**.
4. Click **Show all** to see more than the latest 25.

## Good to know
- A payment known only from your payment sheet shows **From your sheet** until the next Payment History export brings its WWD payment #.
- If WWD's total differs from the lines, it shows "WWD says ..." in amber.
- Lines VMS could not place say "not matched yet" or "freight, vendor not picked yet".
