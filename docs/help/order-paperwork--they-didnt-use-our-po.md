Sometimes the rep doesn't pass our PO along and the confirmation comes back with their own number, or with none. VMS writes a friendly email asking them to use ours and send an updated confirmation, then keeps track of their answer.

## How it works
1. On the confirmation review, click **Open the email asking them to use our PO** (or **…to add our PO** when they listed none). Check it and click **Send**. Nothing is sent on its own; the email also waits in **Mail › Drafts**.
2. The order's **Our PO** box shows **Asked to use our PO** with the date until they answer.
3. When they reply, Claude reads it and logs their answer in the **Our PO** box, with **Their answer** one click away.
   - **They said yes:** VMS waits for the updated confirmation. When it comes in with our PO, it is checked like any confirmation and marked **Final (replaced PO …)**. The old one moves to **Old** inside the Confirmations folder (kept, not deleted).
   - **Too late to change:** a thank-you for trying waits in **Mail › Drafts** for you to send. The order switches to their PO, with a note on the order. If the PO was already sent to Lightspeed, Dana gets a **PO in Lightspeed to change?** card in the Review queue: **Change it to … in Lightspeed**, or **Keep** ours.
   - **Anything else** (a question back, an answer about something else): nothing changes; the box still shows **Asked to use our PO**.

## Good to know
- Each confirmation is asked about once. Once they say it's too late on an order, VMS doesn't ask again for that order.
- Claude only reads replies in the same email conversation as our email.
