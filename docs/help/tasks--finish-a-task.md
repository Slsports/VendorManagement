Check a task off when it is done. Whoever gave it to you is told.

## How to
1. Click the round check next to the task (on the Tasks page, the dashboard box, or a vendor, order or email page).
2. If someone else gave you the task, a box asks for an optional **Note** for them. Click **Done**.
3. Made a mistake? Click **Undo** on the message that appears, or open the **Done** tab and click the green check to reopen it.
4. To delete a task, click the trash can at the end of its row on the Tasks page.

## Good to know
- The person who gave the task sees "… finished …" at the top of their dashboard (and by email or text if they chose).
- Finished tasks are on the **Done** tab.
