Take a file or link out of a vendor's Documents.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page, find **Documents** and open the folder.
2. Click **Remove** under the file's name.
3. Click **OK** to confirm.

## Good to know
- If you only want it somewhere else, use **Move** instead.
- Viewers do not see Rename, Move or Remove.
