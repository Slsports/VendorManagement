When a vendor's confirmation is approved and the order becomes **Confirmed**, VMS hands the order to Trevor for shipping, the LS PO and receiving.

## What VMS does when the order is confirmed
1. **The shipping email is ready** in Trevor's **Mail › Drafts**, to the vendor's orders, shipping and rep contacts. It asks them to ship by our freight routing instructions and to send the ship date, freight quote and tracking. Our routing letter is attached when one is uploaded (**Freight › Routing letter**).
2. **Trevor gets a task** "Ship and receive: (vendor), PO …". Clicking it opens the order's **Shipping & receiving** box.

## The Shipping & receiving box (on the order page)
1. **Open the shipping email**: check it, change anything, and send it. Once sent it shows **Sent** with the date.
2. **Route the freight and finish the LS PO** in the **Freight quote** and **LS PO** boxes on the same page.
3. **Mark received** when the product arrives. The order becomes **Received** with today's date, and Trevor's task closes.
4. If the order came on a pallet with other vendors, pick the bill in **Came on a shared freight bill?** and click **Add this order**. See "Split one freight bill across several orders".

## Good to know
- Web-order vendors (like Kelli's) are left out, the same as for the routing letter.
- A confirmed order with no shipping email yet shows **Make the shipping email and task**.
- The wording of the shipping email and the routing letter is still being written; the email can be changed before it is sent.
