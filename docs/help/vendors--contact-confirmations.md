Each person at a vendor gets an email with a link to a short form showing what we have for them. They click **Looks good** or fix what's wrong, and they're marked **Confirmed**.

## How to
1. Click **Vendors**, then **Contact confirmations**.
2. Open **The email** to check or change the message. Keep {form_link} in it.
3. Click **Prepare emails**. One email per person at the vendors we buy from lands in **Mail › Drafts**.
4. In **Mail › Drafts**, open a few to check them, then click **Send all**.
5. For one vendor, open its page and click **Ask them to confirm** in **Their info and what they sell**.

## What's on their form
- Their name, title, email, phone numbers and what they do for us.
- The company's name, address, phone and website, and the other people we have there (they can fix them, add someone or tick **No longer works here**).
- Who should get our purchase orders, freight quotes and tracking, and invoices.
- How they take orders (email, phone, NuORDER, website), their minimum order and any freight programs.
- Other vendors they represent, one per box, and the product categories they sell.
- Once our routing letter is in VMS: **Did you receive our routing instructions?** Yes or No. Yes marks the letter received for them. No puts the routing letter email for them in **Mail › Drafts**, ready to send. The answer shows in the **Got routing letter?** column.

## Good to know
- Changes go straight into VMS. A new company name, and vendors VMS doesn't know, go to the **Review queue** for you.
- Their freight programs fill in the vendor's free-shipping settings when VMS had none.
- Anyone who hasn't answered after a week gets one reminder. An email that bounces is marked **Bad email**.
- Contacts show **Confirmed** with the date on the vendor's page. The vendors list can be filtered by Confirmed, Waiting, No answer, Bad email or Not asked yet.
- People not confirmed in 12 months are included the next time you prepare emails.
- The email goes only to people we know: never former contacts, and never people only the Worldwide portal lists (tagged **From WWD · not confirmed**). Those still show on the vendor's form, so the people who know can tick **No longer works here**.
- Someone ticked **No longer works here** is kept on the vendor page under **Former contacts**, not deleted, so their old mail can still be found.
