Contracts holds every license, lease and loan: who it is with, the term, who signs, the insurance they require, the payment schedule, the season it covers and when to start the renewal. Tasks come from it on their own.

## How to
1. Click **Accounting** in the menu, then **Contracts**. The list shows the term end, the next payment and a badge when the renewal window is open.
2. Click a contract for the facts, its payments and its open tasks. **Edit** changes the facts.
3. **Add a payment** puts a payment on the schedule (for example each January's license fee). When the check goes out, click **Paid** next to it: the date and account are recorded and the task closes.
4. **Follow up on**: enter a date and what you are waiting for (an offer letter sent, a DocuSign awaited). A task appears on that date; click **Done** on the contract when it is answered.
5. Pick the **Renewal procedure** so the "Start the renewal" task opens the steps.

## Good to know
- A payment task appears 30 days before each unpaid payment is due.
- "Start the renewal" appears at the lead time before the term ends (18 months for the SCE licenses, which is when SCE starts the paperwork).
- Tick **Which of our policies they need proof of** and the contract's partner can be put on **Who needs proof** under Insurance, so certificates go out at each renewal.
- The first contracts are the two SCE licenses: 9.4763 Shaver Lake Marina and 9.4764 the Happy Camper store at Camp Edison.
