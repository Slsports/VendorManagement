A rep group's page shows its details, its reps, the vendors we buy from through it, and the other lines it carries.

## How to
1. Click **Rep groups** in the menu and click a group.
2. Read **Reps**, **Vendors we buy from** (with route tags, **WWD 0% upcharge** and **Do not order**) and **Other lines they carry**.
3. To change its details, click **Edit**, change the **Name**, **Main email**, **Main phone**, **Website**, **Address** or **Notes**, and click **Save** in the bar at the bottom.
4. Click a vendor's name to open it.

## Good to know
- **Other lines they carry** are catalogs to look at. Nothing there is a vendor until you click **Make a vendor**.
- **Back** returns to where you came from (the list, or the vendor page that linked here).
