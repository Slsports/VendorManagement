Companies Claude thinks we have never bought from: wholesalers, brands and services that send sales mail. Their mail goes to **Promotions**. Look the list over now and then, in case one is a vendor we buy from under another name.

## How to
1. Open **Review queue** and click the **Never bought from** tab.
2. Each row is one company: its name, every email address it wrote from, its website and phone (from email signatures), how many emails, and the latest one.
3. Use the search box, or click a column header to sort. **To check** shows the ones nobody has looked at yet.
4. Click one:
   - **This is…**: pick the vendor it really is. Its mail is filed to that vendor from now on, and the company's name is kept as another name for the vendor.
   - **Make it a vendor**: check the name, website and phone, then **Add vendor**. The vendor page opens to fill in the rest.
   - **Leave it**: it stays on the list as checked. **Check again** puts it back.

## Good to know
- New companies like these land here on their own as mail comes in.
- The number on the tab is how many are still to check. It is not counted on the dashboard.
- Only orders@ mail is counted; personal inboxes stay private.
