Change a vendor's name, contact details, routes, settings or notes.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and click **Edit** (top right).
2. Change what you need. The form has five parts: **Basics**, **Contact and address**, **Logistics**, **Ordering windows** and **Notes**.
3. Click **Save changes** in the bar pinned to the bottom of the screen. Click **Cancel** to leave without saving.

## Good to know
- **Aliases** are other names for the vendor, separated by commas (abbreviations, the LS name). Search and mail matching use them. When you add or remove a name and click **Save**, VMS checks all the old mail and the open review cards again within a minute or two: mail that names the vendor by the new name is connected to it, mail that named it only by a removed name no longer is, and **Who is this?** and **Rep group lines** cards are updated. The vendor page shows what it found under **Name re-check**, with **See the mail**.
- **Orders email** is where orders go. **Shipping contact** (name, phone, email) is under Logistics, with the **Will-call pickup address** and **Pickup hours**.
- **Payment terms** left on **Organization default** uses the company's usual terms.
- **Ordering frequency**: Weekly, Monthly, Seasonal, Annual or As needed. **Minimum order** is free text, like "$250 or 12 units".
- **Product types** is a short list of what they sell, like "Fly line, leader, tippet".
- **Paperwork only**: for vendors whose orders we don't keep in VMS (Rossignol: Tyler orders and pays on their website). Their confirmations and invoices are filed, with no **Which order?** check.
- **They pay the freight and ship it**: for a vendor who ships at their own cost (the State of California for Sno-Park books, by FedEx). No freight boxes on the vendor or order pages, no shipment details email, no routing letter, no freight checks; their orders are free freight from the start.
- **No routing letter**: for a website or a local who drops off (Amazon, Ali Express, Rods Power Bait…), where we have no control over how they ship. They never get the freight routing letter and leave the "no email address" list on the Routing letter page. Retail stores and vendors billed through Faire are left out on their own.
- **Their prices are set for the season**: for a vendor whose prices do not move during the season (the State for Sno-Park permits). Their orders have no Check-in pricing or Report used cards.
- **Ask for their freight quote once we approve the order**: VMS writes the email asking the vendor for their freight quote (see "Ask a vendor for their freight quote").
- **Their order sheets are our order: spreadsheets from**: the email address of a rep who sends the order sheet we agree to (Planet Cotton: Maryellen). Those spreadsheets file to **Our orders** (see "A rep's order sheet is our order").
- **How their Vendor IDs are built**: in plain words, like "garment # / design #, like PT 6934/R6886; each garment takes the DESIGN line below it". Claude reads their confirmations and invoices that way.
- Reps are not on this form: add them on the vendor page under **Reps & contacts**.
- If an email address does not look right, the form says so and does not save.
