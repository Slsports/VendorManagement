Save a web address (an online catalog, a price list on the vendor's site) in a vendor's folder, next to the files.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and find **Documents**.
2. Click **Add a link**.
3. Paste the **Link** and give it a **Label** if you like (for example "Fall 2026 catalog").
4. Pick the **Folder** and **Year**; add a **Season** or **Notes** if useful.
5. Click **Save**.

## Good to know
- Click **Open** on the link to open it in a new browser tab.
- Links cannot be downloaded or zipped; "Download all" skips them.
- Catalog and price-list links in offer emails are saved on their own.
