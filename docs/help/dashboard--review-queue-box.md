Shows how many review items are assigned to you, and how many are waiting in total.

## How to
1. On the dashboard, click **Review queue**.
2. Read the line, for example "12 items waiting for a decision, 3 assigned to you".
3. Click **Open the review queue** to work through them.

## Good to know
- The number is the items assigned to you. With **Showing** set to Everyone, it is the total.
- Items you snoozed don't count until they come back.
- Viewers see a dash here; admin, managers and buyers settle the review queue.
