A conversation page shows every message in one email thread, with the tools to answer and file it.

## How to
1. On the Mail page, click a subject.
2. The newest message is open at the top. Older ones are closed to one line; click a line to open it, click again to close it.
3. Emails open formatted, the way they look in Gmail. Click **Plain text** (at the top of the email, beside **Reply**) to see just the words, or **Show formatted** to switch back.
4. In plain text, click **Show earlier messages in this email** to see the quoted mail underneath.
5. Click **Back** at the top to return to the list you came from.

## Good to know
- The top line shows where it stands, the vendor it is filed to, how many messages, and which mailbox it is in.
- **Open in Gmail** opens the same conversation in Gmail, as a backup.
- Messages we sent are outlined in green and marked "sent".
- Each message shows when it came and how long ago.
- If a freight rate is quoted in the mail, a yellow note shows the percentage.
