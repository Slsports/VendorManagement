Ordering windows record when you usually order from a vendor, like the February show or a pre-season order in the fall.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and click **Edit**.
2. Scroll to **Ordering windows** and click **Add window**.
3. Pick the **Kind**: **Spring show (Feb)**, **Fall show (Aug/Sept)**, **Pre-season**, **Reorder**, **Delivery route** or **Custom**.
4. Give it a **Label** if you like (for example "Winter goods").
5. Click the **Months** it covers. Clicked months turn green.
6. Add **Notes**, for example "order by Oct 15 for Nov delivery".
7. Click **Save changes**.

## Good to know
- To remove a window, click the trash can beside its notes, then save.
- The windows show on the vendor page in the **Ordering windows** box, with their months and notes.
