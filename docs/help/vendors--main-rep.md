The main rep is the person we place orders through. They show at the top of the vendor's page and get the follow-ups. Everyone else in **Reps & contacts** is there for customer service, shipping or billing questions.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and click **Reps & contacts**.
2. Find the person and click **Make main rep** on their row. They get a gold star and the **Main rep** tag, and show at the top of the page.
3. To change anyone's details, click their card, change it and click **Save contact**.

## Good to know
- When the vendor has a rep group, the rep group's rep is the main rep by itself, even when the company's own people write to us. Use **Make main rep** only to pick someone else.
- People VMS learns from email at a rep-group vendor are added as **Customer service** (or shipping, billing), not **Rep**.
