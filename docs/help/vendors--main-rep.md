The main rep is the person we place orders through. They show at the top of the vendor's page and get the follow-ups. Everyone else in **Reps & contacts** is there for customer service, shipping or billing questions.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and click **Reps & contacts**.
2. Find the person and click **Make main rep** on their row. They get a gold star and the **Main rep** tag, and show at the top of the page.
3. To pick the alternate, the person to try when the main rep doesn't answer, click **Make alternate** on their row. They get the **Alternate** tag and show under the main rep at the top of the page as **Alternate:**.
4. To take the alternate off, click **Not alternate** on their row.
5. To change anyone's details, click their card, change it and click **Save contact**.

## Good to know
- When the vendor has a rep group, the rep group's rep is the main rep by itself, even when the company's own people write to us. Use **Make main rep** only to pick someone else.
- People VMS learns from email at a rep-group vendor are added as **Customer service** (or shipping, billing), not **Rep**.
- With no one picked and no rep group, the main rep is the vendor's rep we emailed most recently about this vendor.
- Someone only the Worldwide portal lists (tagged **From WWD · not confirmed**) is never the main rep, and neither is a former contact.
- VMS never copies the alternate on the emails it sends. If the main rep doesn't answer, write to the alternate yourself: click their email at the top of the page.
- Making the alternate the main rep takes the alternate off. Someone who leaves (former) is no longer the alternate.
