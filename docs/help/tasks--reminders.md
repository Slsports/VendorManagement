When a task's remind time comes, you get a reminder the ways you chose: on the dashboard, by email and/or by text.

## On the dashboard
A yellow bar appears at the top of the dashboard with the task. Its buttons:
- **The round check (Done):** checks the task off.
- **Later:** pick when to be reminded again (later today, tomorrow morning, in 2 days, next Monday, or a date and time).
- **Dismiss:** hides the reminder. The task stays on your list until you check it off.

## Good to know
- Reminder emails come from orders@ as "VMS Reminders" and link straight to the task.
- Changing a task's **Remind me** time sends a new reminder at the new time.
- To choose how you are reminded, see "Choose how you get reminders".
