Some files in a vendor's Documents and in Accounting Documents came from Dana's Dropbox rather than from an email or an upload. They are filed by the same rules as everything else.

## How to tell
- The file's note says **From Dropbox:** followed by the folder and name it had in Dropbox, so you can find the original.
- The vendor, folder and year were decided by what the paper says, not by the Dropbox folder it sat in.

## Good to know
- A file that was already in VMS was linked, never saved a second time.
- Only the admin sees and runs the Dropbox filing, under **Settings** › **Dropbox**.
