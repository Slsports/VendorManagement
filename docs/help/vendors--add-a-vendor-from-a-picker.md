Anywhere VMS asks you to pick a vendor (filing an email, bulk import, orders), you can add a new one on the spot.

## How to
1. Click in the vendor box and start typing the name.
2. Choose **+ Add new vendor "…"**, always the first line of the list.
3. In the small box, check the **Name**, pick **Billed through** (**WWD (Worldwide)**, **Faire** or **Direct**) and add the **Orders email** if you have it.
4. Click **Add vendor**. It is picked for you right away.

## Good to know
- Fill in the rest later on the vendor's page with **Edit**.
- Inactive vendors are listed after the active ones, grayed out with "inactive". Picking one asks **Reactivate this vendor?**; click **Reactivate and use** to bring it back and use it, without leaving the screen.
- Use the arrow keys and Enter to choose without the mouse.
