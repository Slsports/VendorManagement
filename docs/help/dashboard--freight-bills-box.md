For the person who handles the freight companies (Trevor) and anyone ticked to see freight: freight bills still to match, and bills still to pay.

## How to
1. On the dashboard, click **Freight bills**.
2. **To match** lists bills that still need a PDF or matching to orders.
3. **To pay** lists unpaid bills with amount and due date, soonest due first.
4. Click a bill to open it, or **Open Freight bills** for the full page.

## Good to know
- A bill past its due date shows in red, and the number turns red.
- Who sees freight is set by Dana in Settings > Mail > Freight.
- In **Customize** this card is called "Freight".
