When mail comes to orders@ from a sender VMS does not know, Claude reads it first. If it is clearly one of our vendors, Claude files it there (you can undo it on the vendor page). If it is a company we have never bought from, it goes to the **Never bought from** tab. If it is one of our rep groups, Claude files it to the rep group and adds the lines it reps (see "Rep group lines Claude finds in the mail"). Freight companies go to the Freight page. Only the senders Claude is unsure about get a card here. One answer files all their mail, now and later.

## How to
1. Open **Review queue** and click the **Who is this?** tab.
2. Read the card: how many emails, the sender's name, recent subjects, and what Claude made of the mail.
3. Click one answer:
   - **Yes, (vendor)**: VMS's guess is right.
   - **Another vendor** / **Pick or add the vendor**: choose a vendor, or add a new one.
   - **A rep group**: pick the rep group. Each email is then filed by the vendor it names.
   - **A service like Bill.com or Faire**: sends mail for many vendors; each email is filed by the vendor it names.
   - **A freight carrier**: pick or add the carrier, then **This carrier**. Their mail moves to Freight.
   - **Our staff**: someone who works for us, writing from their own email. Their supply requests go to **Supplies**.
   - **Marketing** or **Other – not a vendor**: see "Marketing or Other – not a vendor".
4. A message says how many emails were filed.

## Good to know
- Not sure? Click **See the N emails** first (see "Look at a sender's emails").
