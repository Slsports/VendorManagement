When mail comes to orders@ from a sender VMS does not know, Claude reads it first. If it is clearly one of our vendors, Claude files it there (you can undo it on the vendor page). If it is a company we have never bought from, it goes to the **Never bought from** tab. If it is one of our rep groups, Claude files it to the rep group and adds the lines it reps (see "Rep group lines Claude finds in the mail"). Freight companies go to the Freight page. Only the senders Claude is unsure about get a card here. One answer files all their mail, now and later.

## How to
1. Open **Review queue** and click the **Who is this?** tab.
2. Read the card: how many emails, the sender's name, recent subjects, and what Claude made of the mail.
3. Click one answer:
   - **Yes, (vendor)**: VMS's guess is right; it's that company's own staff.
   - **Pick rep group / vendors**: choose everything that applies, then click **Save**. The card stays open until you save.
     - **A rep** (they sell for several companies): pick their **Rep group**, or **+ Add new rep group…**, and add the vendors they write about. Each email is filed to the vendor it's about, and those vendors join the rep group as its lines (a vendor that already has another rep group stays with it).
     - **A company's own staff**: leave the rep group on **Not a rep** and add just that vendor. All their mail goes to that vendor.
     - Several vendors and no rep group: each email is filed to the vendor it's about.
   - **New rep group: (name)**: shows when Claude read the mail as a rep agency that isn't in VMS yet (like Anthony & Associates). It opens the new rep group with the name filled in; add the lines, then **Save**.
   - **A service like Bill.com or Faire**: sends mail for many vendors; each email is filed by the vendor it names.
   - **A freight carrier**: pick or add the carrier, then **This carrier**. Their mail moves to Freight.
   - **Our staff**: someone who works for us, writing from their own email. Their supply requests go to **Supplies**.
   - **Marketing** or **Other – not a vendor**: see "Marketing or Other – not a vendor".
4. A message says how many emails were filed and which lines joined the rep group.

## Good to know
- Rep or vendor? If they write about more than one company, or their signature says "rep" or "& Associates", pick the rep group. Picking one vendor sends **all** their mail to that vendor.
- Vendor mail forwarded to orders@ from one of our own addresses is filed by the vendor it was forwarded from, not as mail from us (see "Vendor mail forwarded from another address").
- Not sure? Click **See the N emails** first (see "Look at a sender's emails").
