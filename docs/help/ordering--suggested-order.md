The suggested order is what LS says we need minus what is already coming in.

## How to
1. On the vendor's page click **Suggested order** (or **Suggested order** on the Ordering card).
2. Each line shows **Vendor ID**, **Description**, **Sold**, **In stock**, **Need**, **On order** and **Order**.
3. Change any quantity in the yellow **Order** column. Set it to 0 to leave an item out.
4. The bottom shows units, items and cost.
5. Then click **Order placed**, **Order it** (moves the vendor to To order) or **Don't need it**.

## Good to know
- **Need** = sold minus in stock. Overstock in one item never offsets another.
- **On order** counts confirmed orders (from the vendor's confirmation), orders shipped or invoiced but not yet in LS (from the invoice), and placed orders (our order's items). Each links to its order with its ship date.
- Open orders with no item list are named at the top; check them before ordering.
- **Download the report** gets the formatted sales report.
