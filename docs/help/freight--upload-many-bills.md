Use this to catch up: drop a pile of freight bill PDFs at once and Claude reads each one.

## How to
1. Click **Freight** in the menu, then **Upload bills** at the top right.
2. Drag the PDFs into the box, or click **Choose PDFs**.
3. Wait while each says **Reading…**. Each file then gets a result:
   - **New bill**: added.
   - **Filled the waiting bill**: it was the PDF a waiting bill needed.
   - **Already on file, skipped**: a copy of a bill VMS has.
   - **Pick the billing company**: open it and pick who sent it.
   - **Could not read**: the reason shows beside it.
4. Click **Open** beside any file to go to its bill, then match the lines.
5. Close the box with the **X** when finished.

## Good to know
- Our UPS number (or our account number) printed on the bill tells VMS which billing company it belongs to.
- Only PDFs are taken. Two files are read at a time; each takes a few seconds.
