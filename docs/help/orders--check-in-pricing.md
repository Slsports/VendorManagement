The **Check-in pricing** box on an order lists the items from the vendor's invoice with a retail price for each, worked out from cost, margin, freight and the WWD upcharge.

## How to
1. Open the order and scroll to **Check-in pricing**.
2. Add items one at a time: fill in **Vendor ID**, **Item**, quantity and **Unit cost**, then click **Add**.
3. Or click **Paste from a spreadsheet**, paste rows (Vendor ID, item, quantity, unit cost) and click **Add these lines**.
4. Click **Save prices** to keep the calculated retail prices.
5. To set a price yourself, type it in the **Retail** box and press Enter. Click the small round arrow beside it to go back to the calculated price.
6. To remove an item, click the trash can on its row.

## Good to know
- The grey line at the top shows how the price is figured: freight %, margin, WWD upcharge (or "No WWD upcharge") and the total %.
- Prices are exact, not rounded. Prices you changed by hand show an amber border and are never overwritten.
- When the freight changes, the button reads **Recalculate N prices**; when nothing needs saving it reads **Prices are up to date**.
- The WWD upcharge applies to orders billed through Worldwide, unless the vendor is marked zero-upcharge.
