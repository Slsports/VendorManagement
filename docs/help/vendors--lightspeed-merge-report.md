Vendors merged in VMS still need merging in LS. The Lightspeed merge report lists them so you can do each one in LS and tick it off.

## How to
1. Click **Review queue** in the menu, then **Lightspeed merge report** (top right).
2. Each box says the name to **Keep in Lightspeed** and, below, each name to **Merge … into it**.
3. Do the merge in LS, then tick **Done in LS** on that line.
4. Click **Everything** to see all merges, or **To do in LS** for just the ones left.
5. Click **Download CSV** for a spreadsheet of the list on screen.

## Good to know
- Only confirmed merges of names that exist in LS are on the to-do list. Vendors that only exist in VMS say "VMS only, nothing to do in LS".
- Each line shows its route, whether it was merged at import or by hand, the date, and a status: **Awaiting your OK**, **Confirmed** or **Split back out**.
- The heading counts how many are still waiting for an OK in the review queue.
