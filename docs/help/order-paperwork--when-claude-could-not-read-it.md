Claude usually reads and compares a new confirmation or invoice within a minute or two. Here is what to do if it is slow or fails.

## Still working
- The check page says Claude is reading the document or comparing it with the order. The page fills in when done.
- To hurry it, click **Check now**. It takes about a minute.

## Couldn't read this paper
1. The page shows "Couldn't read this paper". **What Claude sent back** under it shows exactly what went wrong.
2. Click **Try again**.
3. If it still fails (a blurry scan, an unreadable file), open the paper yourself and check it by hand, then click **Not a confirmation: set aside** (or the invoice version) to clear it.

## Good to know
- Checks Claude could not read stay on the Dashboard card until someone handles them.
- Long papers (a 10-page invoice) are read in parts, so every line gets read. If a paper is too long even for that, the first lines are read and the note says to check the rest by eye.
