Some LS vendor names carry a rep or parent-company tag. A rep's name (like Donna or Maryellen) is taken out of the name when VMS cleans up the LS names, with no card. For a parent-company tag VMS proposes a cleaner name here.

## How to
1. Open **Review queue** and find **Name clean-ups**.
2. Each card shows the proposed name, any rep group, and any alias it keeps.
3. Edit the name in the box if you like, then click **Use this name**. Or click **Keep as is**.
4. To accept every proposal at once, click **Apply all as proposed** at the top of the group and confirm.

## Good to know
- The old LS name always stays as an alias, so imports and mail still match.
- Nothing changes in LS itself.
- A rep's name is taken out after a dash with or without spaces, or with no dash ("EXTENDED EXPOSURE-DONNA HOFFMAN" became **EXTENDED EXPOSURE**), and the vendor goes under that rep's group.
