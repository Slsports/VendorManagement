Answer a vendor right from the conversation. The reply goes out from orders@ under your name, with your signature and the logo.

## How to
1. Open the conversation and open the message you are answering.
2. Click **Reply**, **Reply all** or **Forward** at the top of the email, just under who it was sent to.
3. Type your message above the quoted email. For a forward, type who it goes to in **To**.
4. Click **Send**.

## Good to know
- **Reply all** only shows when the email went to more than one person.
- Drafts VMS writes for you in a conversation always go to everyone on the vendor's email (reply all), except our own addresses.
- A forward sends the original attachments along; a note at the top says how many.
- After a reply, VMS takes you back to the list you came from. After a forward you stay on the conversation.
- Replies always go from the conversation's own mailbox: orders@, or your own mailbox for your private mail.
- In someone else's private mailbox the reply buttons are hidden; only its owner answers from it.
- Once sent, you become the owner, the conversation waits on the vendor, and the follow-up clock starts.
