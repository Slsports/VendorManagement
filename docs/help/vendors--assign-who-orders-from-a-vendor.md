Each vendor can be assigned to one person: the one who orders from it and runs its sales report. That person gets its review questions and its mail.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page.
2. In the **Overview**, use the **Assigned to** box and pick a person, or **Nobody yet**.
3. It saves right away.

## Good to know
- You can also set it on the **Edit** form (**Assigned to**, under Basics).
- The review queue sometimes asks "who orders from this vendor?" with a suggested person. Click **Keep …** to agree, or pick someone else and click **Assign**.
- On the Vendors page, the second filter box shows **Mine**, one person's vendors, or **Unassigned** ones.
