Some vendors credit the freight if we pay by a certain date. Unpaid orders with that offer are listed here, soonest pay-by date first. The card's title is **Pay on time for the freight back**.

## How to
1. On the dashboard, click **Pay on time for the freight back**.
2. Each line shows the vendor, how much freight comes back (if known) and the pay-by date.
3. Click a line to open the order.

## Good to know
- The pay-by date turns amber within 3 weeks and red within a week or once it has passed; then the number turns red too.
- "no date" means the pay-by date is missing; add it on the order.
- The card only shows when there is at least one such order.
- In **Customize** this card is called "Freight allowances".
