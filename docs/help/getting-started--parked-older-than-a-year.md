Anything VMS asks a person to do or look at that is about something more than 12 months old is **parked**: out of every list and count, kept where it can be found, until someone brings it back. This keeps old history from flooding today's work while VMS reads the old mail.

## Where to find parked things
- **Tasks**: the **Parked** tab. **Unpark** on a row puts it back on the list.
- **Mail**: the **Parked** tab shows conversations that were waiting on us more than a year ago. Open one and click **Unpark**. New mail in a parked conversation unparks it by itself.
- **Review queue**: the line "N parked · Show" above the cards lists parked cards and paperwork checks, each with **Unpark**.
- **Accounting › Documents**: the **Parked** tab. Answering a paper's question or filing it brings it back by itself. Dana has **Unpark all**.

## Good to know
- A new task, card, conversation or paper about something older than a year parks itself the moment it is made.
- Nothing is deleted or forgotten. Parked things keep their links to the email, order or paper they came from.
- VMS reads the old mail a month further back each day, newest first, while the day's Claude spending stays under the limit, until it reaches the oldest email on file. Orders it finds from more than a year ago are filed as received history, not open work.
