The review queue holds the questions VMS will not decide on its own: who sent an email, whether two vendors are the same, a name clean-up, and so on. Nothing in these changes until a person answers.

## How to
1. Click **Review queue** in the menu (under Overview).
2. Cards are grouped by kind, each group with a short explanation at the top.
3. Use **Show** to pick whose cards you see: **Everyone**, **Mine**, **Unassigned**, or one person. The page opens on yours when you have any.
4. Read a card and click one of its buttons. The card leaves the queue once it is settled.

## Kinds of cards
- **Who is this mail from?** (new senders), **Does this need an answer?** (unsure emails), **Artwork to approve?**
- **Possible duplicates**, **Merged at import**, **Name clean-ups**, **Lightspeed markers**
- **Who orders from these vendors?**
- Freight: unmatched delivery receipts and payments ("which vendor?", "which bill?").

## Good to know
- The **Review queue** card on the dashboard shows how many are waiting and how many are yours.
- Viewers do not see the review queue in the menu and cannot answer cards.
- Category clean-up batches for LS will come here once that tool is built.
