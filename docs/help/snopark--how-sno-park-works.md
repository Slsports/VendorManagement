We sell Sno-Park permits for the State of California. Nothing is prepaid: the State sends books of permits, we sell them, and every month we report the empty books, pay for the permits in them and mail the books back. At season end everything goes back and a final report settles up. **Sno-Park** in the menu is where it all lives.

## The season, step by step
1. **The State's packet arrives** (an email to billing@ called "Vendor Registration", usually in September). VMS opens the season and gives Dana a task to return the packet with the first order. Only Dana emails the State.
2. **The books arrive** by FedEx, 5 to 10 business days after the order. Whoever opens the box enters them on the Sno-Park page with **Add books** (see "Enter the books when they arrive").
3. **Every month in season**, the person who runs Sno-Park gets a task on the 1st, due the 15th. She ticks the books that are empty, VMS fills the State's Excel and works out the check, and the report, the check and the empty books go by mail (see "The monthly report").
4. **Season end** (May 30): the final report lists every book, sold or not, and all the books go back by June 20 (see "The final report").

## Good to know
- Season permits and day permits are different books with different prices. VMS knows both.
- The first report of a season is for November, due December 15. There is no snow before that.
- Nothing monthly is emailed. The State needs the check and the books in the envelope. Mail to OHMVR Division, P.O. Box 942896, Sacramento, CA 94296.
- The Sno-Park page shows what Lightspeed says we sold this season next to what the books say. It is a check, not the source of the numbers: permits sold from a book that is not empty yet are not on a report until that book is empty.
- The reports and the scans are filed under the vendor CA Snow Park in the **Sno-Park** folder.
