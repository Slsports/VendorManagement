We sell Sno-Park permits for the State of California. Nothing is prepaid: the State sends books of permits, we sell them, and every month we report the empty books, pay for the permits in them and mail the books back. At season end everything goes back and a final report settles up. **Sno-Park** in the menu is where it all lives.

## The season, step by step
1. **The State's packet arrives** (an email to billing@ called "Vendor Registration", usually in September). VMS opens the season and gives Dana a task to return the packet with the first order. Only Dana emails the State for orders and the packet.
2. **The books arrive** by FedEx, 5 to 10 business days after the order, with the State's sheet saying what they sent. Whoever opens the box clicks **Receive books**, scans the sheet and enters every book; VMS checks the box against the sheet and, when it matches, puts the books into Lightspeed itself (see "Receive the Sno-Park books").
3. **Every month in season**, the person who runs Sno-Park gets a task on the 1st, due the 15th. She ticks the books that are empty, VMS fills the State's Excel and the printable form and works out the check, she photographs the covers of the books going back, and the form, the check and the empty books go by mail (see "The monthly report").
4. **Season end** (May 30): the final report lists every book, sold or not, and all the books go back by June 20. VMS then checks Lightspeed's sales against everything reported and emails Dana if they are off (see "The final report").

## Good to know
- Season permits and day permits are different books with different prices. A day book holds 25 permits, a season book 10. VMS knows both.
- The first report of a season is for November, due December 15. There is no snow before that.
- Nothing monthly is emailed. The State needs the check and the books in the envelope. Mail to OHMVR Division, P.O. Box 942896, Sacramento, CA 94296.
- Our contact at the State is Monica Perez, Analyst I (on the CA Snow Park vendor page as Main rep, with her phone and hours). A box that does not match its sheet gets an email draft to the State that the person receiving sends, with Dana copied.
- The Sno-Park page shows what Lightspeed says we sold this season next to what the books say. It is a check, not the source of the numbers: permits sold from a book that is not empty yet are not on a report until that book is empty.
- The State sends a new Report of Operations form every season. The 2026-2027 form is built into VMS. From next season on, put the new file on file with **Upload the State's form for this season** on the Sno-Park page, under the season, and VMS fills that one.
- Files under the vendor CA Snow Park: the reports, forms, shipment sheets and cover pictures in the **Sno-Park** folder; each month's packet as mailed in **Submitted permit payments**; the final packet in **Season final reports**.
