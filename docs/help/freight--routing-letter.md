Our freight routing instructions go to every vendor, one email per person, and each person clicks a link to confirm they received them.

## How to
1. Click **Freight**, then **Routing letter**.
2. Click **Upload the letter** (or **Upload a new version**), pick the PDF, add the effective date and click **Save**. Save it from Word as a PDF first.
3. Open **The email that sends it** to check or change the message. Keep {receipt_link} in it.
4. Click **Prepare emails: vendors with open orders**. One email per person lands in **Mail › Drafts**, with their open orders listed and the letter attached.
5. In **Mail › Drafts**, open a few to check them, then click **Send all**.

## Good to know
- The table shows each person: vendor, name, email, version, the date it was sent and the date they confirmed. **Not confirmed** lists who hasn't clicked yet.
- Anyone who hasn't confirmed after 7 days gets one reminder email.
- A new version: upload it, then click **Everyone who had an earlier version**. They each confirm the new one.
- Vendors with open orders and no email address are listed so you can add one.
- Each vendor's page has a **Routing letter** box with the same list for that vendor. When writing any email you can tick **Attach our routing letter**.
- Web-order vendors (like Kelli's) are left out.
