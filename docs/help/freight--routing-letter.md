Our freight routing instructions go to every vendor, one email per person, and each person clicks a link to confirm they received them.

## Change the letter
1. Click **Freight**, then **Routing letter**.
2. Click **Edit the letter**. It opens with the newest version's words.
3. Change the title, the shaded box at the top, the sections (each has a heading and its text), the closing line and the footer. **Add a section**, the arrows and the bin move or remove sections.
4. In the text: start a line with **- ** for a bullet, put two stars around words for bold (\*\*like this\*\*), and write a link as \[words people see\](https://…). A blank line starts a new paragraph.
5. Click **Preview the PDF** to see exactly what vendors will get.
6. Set the **Effective date**, add **What changed** if you like, and click **Save as version …**. VMS makes the PDF; every earlier version is kept.

You can still click **Upload a PDF instead** to use a letter made in Word.

## Send it
1. Open **The email that sends it** to check or change the message. Keep {receipt_link} in it.
3. Click **Prepare emails: vendors with open orders**. One email per person lands in **Mail › Drafts**, with their open orders listed and the letter attached.
4. In **Mail › Drafts**, open a few to check them, then click **Send all**.

## Good to know
- The table shows each person: vendor, name, email, version, the date it was sent and the date they confirmed. **Not confirmed** lists who hasn't clicked yet.
- Anyone who hasn't confirmed after 7 days gets one reminder email.
- A new version: save or upload it, then click **Everyone who had an earlier version**. They each confirm the new one.
- The letter says: freight 10% or less of the merchandise cost, the vendor ships and adds it to the invoice; over 10%, they send their quote and fill out our Shipment Details form, and Trevor schedules the shipment. It also explains how to pack boxes for UPS.
- The newest version also goes with every shipment details email.
- Vendors with open orders and no email address are listed so you can add one. Vendors ticked **No routing letter** (websites, locals who drop off), retail stores and Faire vendors are left out.
- Each vendor's page shows it in the **Freight** card: whether they have the current letter, and **Every send** for that vendor. When writing any email you can tick **Attach our routing letter**.
- Web-order vendors (like Kelli's) are left out.
