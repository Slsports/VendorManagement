This box lists artwork a vendor is waiting on us to approve, oldest first.

## How to
1. Click **Artwork approvals** on the dashboard to see the list.
2. Click a row to open the email and approve it or ask for changes.

## Good to know
- Each row shows the vendor, subject and days waiting; red after 2 days.
- "Changes to send" means you marked **Needs changes** and still owe the vendor an email.
- "Changes sent, waiting on a new proof" rows are listed last in gray.
- The box hides when there is nothing to approve.
