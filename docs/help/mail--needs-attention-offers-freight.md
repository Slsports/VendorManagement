The four big tabs split mail by what kind it is, the way Outlook had Focused and Other.

## How to
1. Click **Mail** in the menu.
2. Click one of the tabs:
   - **Needs attention**: replies, confirmations, invoices, questions. Anything someone has to act on.
   - **Offers & catalogs**: specials, price lists, catalogs and newsletters.
   - **Freight**: mail from carriers (PartnerShip, Worldwide Express, Priority One, Pinnacle and others).
   - **Everything**: all mail, for searching.

## Good to know
- Mail opens on **Needs attention**.
- VMS sorts each email into a tab on its own; when the rules are unsure, Claude reads it, and anything still unsure stays in Needs attention.
- If something lands in the wrong tab, open it and click **Move to Offers & catalogs** (or **Move to Needs attention**). That teaches VMS where that sender belongs.
- Price lists and catalogs from offers are saved to the vendor's Documents on their own.
- Freight bills also wait on the Freight bills page.
