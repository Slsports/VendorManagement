Mail in your own mailbox is private. Share a conversation when the rest of the team needs to see it.

## How to
1. Click **Mail**, then **My mail**, and open the conversation.
2. Click **Share to Orders** at the top. The tag changes to "shared to Orders".
3. To make it private again, click **Take back out of Orders**.

## Good to know
- Once shared, it shows in the Orders (shared) mail for everyone. It stays yours.
- Until it is shared, only you and Dana see it.
- Conversations where a known vendor, rep group or freight company writes to you are shared to Orders by themselves, in Trevor's and Jarrett's mailboxes and the Old Gmail (not Dana's own mailbox). Mail from anyone else stays private.
- An email that names one of our PO numbers (a freight quote, pickup or tracking email) is also filed to that order's vendor and to the order.
- If one was shared by mistake, click **Take back out of Orders**. It stays private after that, even when the vendor writes again.
