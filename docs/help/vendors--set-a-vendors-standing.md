A vendor's standing warns everyone before they order. It is a warning on every screen, never a block.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and click **Edit**.
2. Under Basics, set **Standing** to one of:
   - **Fine to order** (the usual)
   - **On hold: not for now, look again on the review date**
   - **Last resort: only if the items are nowhere else**
   - **Do not order**
3. Under **Why?**, tick the reasons: Shipping fees, Damaged goods or junk, Order mistakes, Does not deliver what was ordered, Bad attitude, Slow with credits, Out of business, Discontinued line, Overstocked, Other.
4. Optional: set **Look at this again on** (for example a hold until January).
5. Write the reason in your own words in the box below.
6. Click **Save changes**.

## Good to know
- The vendor page shows a colored banner at the top with the reasons; the Vendors list and Scorecard show the standing too. Do-not-order names show in red.
- **On hold** with a **look again** date: VMS puts a task on that date for whoever the vendor is assigned to, with your hold note (for example "TY INC: look again (on hold)" on Jan 18). Change the date and the task moves; set the vendor back to **Fine to order** and the task closes.
- Ordering is still possible if you change your mind.
- Setting it back to **Fine to order** clears the reasons.
- Use **Do not order** on the Vendors page to list them all.
