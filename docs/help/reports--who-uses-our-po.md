Some vendors put their own number on their invoice instead of our PO. This report shows who, so it can be raised with them.

## How to
1. Click **Reports** in the menu, then **Who uses our PO**.
2. Each vendor shows how many WWD invoices used our PO, our PO written differently, **Their own number**, or no PO, and examples of **What they put**. The worst are first.
3. Click a vendor to open its page.

## Good to know
- It is built from Edenred's invoice lines (loaded on the **Payments** page).
- A vendor page shows the same thing as a tag, for example "Uses our PO: 3 of 11 invoices".
