When two vendor records look alike, a **Possible duplicates** card asks whether they are the same.

## How to
1. Open **Review queue** and find **Possible duplicates**.
2. Read why they look alike. Use **Open (vendor)** to look at either record.
3. If they are different, click **Keep both, not the same**.
4. If they are the same, click **Same vendor, merge**, then:
   - Choose which record to keep. The other one's contacts, routes and notes move into it.
   - Optionally set the route (WWD, Faire, Not WWD, Prepaid Direct), or leave as is.
   - Click **Merge now**.

## Good to know
- Merges are listed on the **Lightspeed merge report** so the same merge can be done in LS.
