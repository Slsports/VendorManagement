When two vendor records look like the same company, VMS asks in the review queue instead of merging on its own. You decide.

## How to
1. Click **Review queue** in the menu and find **Possible duplicates** (the same card also shows on both vendors' pages).
2. Click **Open …** on the card to look at either vendor first, if you need to.
3. If they are different companies, click **Keep both, not the same**.
4. If they are the same, click **Same vendor, merge**. Choose which record to keep.
5. Optional: pick the billing route for the result (**WWD (Worldwide)**, **Faire**, **Not WWD (direct)**, **Prepaid Direct**), or leave **Route: leave as is**.
6. Click **Merge now**.

## Good to know
- The other record's contacts, routes, ordering windows, notes and names move into the one you keep. The old record turns inactive and says "Merged into another vendor", kept for history.
- **Merged at import** cards are vendors VMS already joined from several LS names. Click **Yes, one vendor** to confirm, or **No, split one out**, pick the name that is a different vendor, and click **Split it out**.
- Every merge goes on the **Lightspeed merge report**, so it can be repeated in LS.
