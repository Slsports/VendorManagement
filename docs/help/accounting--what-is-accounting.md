Accounting is Dana's bookkeeping in one place: the billing@shaverlakesports.com mail, the documents that come in with it, bills that are not vendor invoices, the people she deals with, and the tasks and reminders that go with all of it.

## How to
1. Click **Accounting** in the menu. It only shows for people with the Accounting tick.
2. The first box shows the billing@ mailbox: how many emails are in VMS and whether the last 12 months are in yet.
3. Click **Open the billing@ mail** to read and answer it.

## Good to know
- Everything in Accounting is private. Managers and buyers never see it, and being admin is not enough on its own.
- Accounting mail can never be shared to Orders. A vendor's invoice or confirmation forwarded from billing@ still files to the vendor as usual.
- Mail views, automatic filing, a bills list, contacts, tasks and a calendar are being added in that order.
