A task is a to-do with an optional due date and reminder. You can keep it for yourself or give it to someone else.

## How to
1. Click **Tasks** in the menu, then **New task**. You can also click the **+** on the **My tasks** box on the dashboard, or the **Task** button on a vendor, order or email page.
2. Type **What needs doing**.
3. Pick a **Due** date if it has one.
4. Pick when to **Remind me** (date and time). If you set a due date, the **Due day, 8 AM** link fills it in for you.
5. Under **Assigned to**, leave yourself or pick someone else.
6. Under **About**, tag it to a vendor, freight company, rep group, order or something else (see "Tag a task").
7. Add **Notes** if you like, then click **Add task**.

## Good to know
- Giving a task to someone puts it on their list and reminds them the way they chose.
- Added from a vendor, order or email page, the task is already tagged to it.
