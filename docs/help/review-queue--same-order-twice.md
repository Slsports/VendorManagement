VMS looks every day for an order that was entered twice, for example once from the order sheet and once from an email. It shows each pair to you as a card **The same order twice?** in the **Review queue** under **Needs a hand**. Nothing is merged until you answer.

## How to
1. Open **Review queue** and click **Needs a hand**.
2. On a **The same order twice?** card, look at the two orders side by side: PO, what was ordered, date, status and amount. Click a PO to open that order.
3. If they are the same order, click **Keep this one** under the order to keep. The other is merged into it: all its notes (each marked "From the merged copy"), papers, emails, tasks, invoices and freight move to the one you kept, and the copy disappears from every list. It is never marked **Cancelled**: Cancelled is only for an order we or the vendor cancelled.
4. If they are two real orders, click **Not the same order**. That pair is never asked about again.

## Good to know
- VMS only asks when both orders are from the same vendor, from the same show (or placed within a week of each other), have the same kinds of items, and at least one is still open.
- A ship-now order and a ship-later order from the same show each have their own confirmation, so VMS never asks about them.
- The order you keep gets anything it was missing from the copy, like the show, who placed it and the amount, and the further-along status (Paid wins over Entered). Its notes say what was merged and when.
- Reloading the order sheet never brings a merged copy back.
