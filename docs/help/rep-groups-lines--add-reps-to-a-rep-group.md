List the people at a rep group. Mail from any of their addresses is filed to the rep group.

## How to
1. Open the rep group's page and find **Reps**.
2. Click **Add rep**.
3. If the rep was already typed on one of the group's vendors, they are listed under **Already on its vendors**. Click **Add to this group** next to them and you are done: they move to the rep group and show on all its vendors.
4. Someone new? Fill in a **Name**, **Title** (optional, like "Show rep") and **Email**. Under **Phone numbers**, pick **Work**, **Cell**, **Fax** or **Other** (type what it is) for each number; **Add phone** adds another.
5. Click **Save rep**.
6. To change a rep, click their card (or their name, or the pencil), change what you need and click **Save changes**.
7. Someone left the group? Click the person-with-an-x button on their row (**No longer with them**). They move to **Former reps** at the bottom, with when we emailed them, so their old mail can still be found. **Current again** brings them back.
8. The trash can (**Delete**) is only for a mistake. A deleted rep is never added back by the Worldwide portal.

## Good to know
- These reps also show on each of the group's vendors, under **Reps & contacts**, and are the vendor's main rep unless someone else is picked there with **Make main rep**.
