List the people at a rep group. Mail from any of their addresses is filed to the rep group.

## How to
1. Open the rep group's page and find **Reps**.
2. Click **Add rep**.
3. Fill in a **Name**, **Title** (optional, like "Show rep") and **Email**. Under **Phone numbers**, pick **Work**, **Cell**, **Fax** or **Other** (type what it is) for each number; **Add phone** adds another.
4. Click **Save rep**.
5. To change a rep, click their card (or their name, or the pencil), change what you need and click **Save changes**.
6. To remove a rep, click the trash can on their row and confirm.

## Good to know
- These reps also show on each of the group's vendors, under **Reps & contacts**, and are the vendor's main rep unless someone else is picked there with **Make main rep**.
