List the people at a rep group. Mail from any of their addresses is filed to the rep group.

## How to
1. Open the rep group's page and find **Reps**.
2. Click **Add rep**.
3. Fill in a **Name**, **Title** (optional, like "Show rep"), **Email** and/or **Phone**.
4. Click **Save rep**.
5. To remove a rep, click the trash can on their row and confirm.

## Good to know
- These reps also show on each of the group's vendors, under **Reps & contacts**. Star one there to make them our assigned rep for that vendor.
