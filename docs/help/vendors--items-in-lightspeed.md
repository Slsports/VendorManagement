VMS counts each vendor's items in Lightspeed: **Active in LS** means it has active items, **Inactive in LS** means it has only archived items, and **No items in LS** means none. Use it to see which vendors matter most.

## How to
1. Click **Vendors** in the menu.
2. In the **LS items** box, pick **Active in LS**, **Inactive in LS**, **No items in LS** or **Not tied to LS**.
3. Or click the **LS items** column header to sort: active vendors with the most items first. Click again to reverse.
4. On a vendor page, the tag at the top and **Items in Lightspeed** in the overview show the counts, like "212 active, 40 archived items", with the date VMS read them.

## Good to know
- VMS reads all Lightspeed items again every week on its own. To read now, open **Settings** › **Lightspeed** and click **Read LS items now** (it takes a few minutes).
- A vendor shows counts only once it is tied to its Lightspeed vendor (**Lightspeed** box on the vendor page, **In Lightspeed as**).
- This tag does not change the vendor's own **Active** switch, which hides a vendor from pick lists.
- Nothing in Lightspeed changes; VMS only reads.
