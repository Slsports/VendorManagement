Add your own rating to any of a vendor's six scores, with a one-line reason. It replaces the automatic score for that line.

## How to
1. Open the vendor and find the **Scorecard** box.
2. Each line shows the stars and what the order history says.
3. Click **Rate** on the line.
4. Pick a score from **5 · Excellent** to **1 · Poor** and type **Why (one line)**.
5. Click **Save**.

## Good to know
- The line then reads "Rated ... by staff", with your reason, and "history says N" when the history disagrees.
- Ease of ordering and Communication and help only get a score from staff ratings for now.
- Change a rating by clicking **Rate** again.
