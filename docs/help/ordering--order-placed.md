When you send the order to the rep, click **Order placed**. The new order form opens already filled in.

## How to
1. In the suggested order, set the quantities, then click **Order placed**.
   For a vendor on **To order** with no sales report, click **Order placed** on its row on the Ordering card.
2. Check the form: what was ordered, total cost, placed by, PO number, store.
3. Click **Add order**.

## Good to know
- The suggested order's items become the order's item lines.
- The vendor leaves Check stock or To order, and the order shows under **Placed** until the vendor confirms.
