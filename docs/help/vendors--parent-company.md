Some brands are separate vendors in VMS and Lightspeed but are owned and billed by one parent company. For example, JollyBuyer Inc bills Anngoti, Oeelinn and Pom Pom Tail.

## Good to know
- The vendor page shows the parent under **Parent / billing company**.
- Typing the parent's name in the search bar at the top (like "JollyBuyer" or "Jolly Buyer") lists every brand it owns.
- WWD bills all of these brands under the parent's name. VMS puts each WWD invoice on the right brand by our PO on it (ANNGOTI…, OEELINN…).
- An invoice with no order VMS can find goes to the brand named in WWD's name, like "Jolly Buyer DBA **Pom Pom Tail**".
- If VMS can't tell which brand an invoice is for, it stays under **WWD names to match** on Payments for you to pick.
