Flag a vendor when something about it needs checking. Questions from the review queue about a vendor also show on its page.

## How to
1. To flag: open the vendor's page, click **Edit**, tick **Flag for review**, write what needs checking, and click **Save changes**.
2. To clear: open the vendor's page. Under the yellow **Flagged for review** banner, click **Checked, clear the flag**.
3. If the banner says "Answer the question below", answer the review card under it instead (for example **Keep both, not the same** or **Same vendor, merge**). The flag clears itself.

## Good to know
- The **Needs review** button on the Vendors page lists every flagged vendor, and the **Review** tag shows in the Flags column.
- Review cards on a vendor page are the same ones as in the **Review queue**, with the same buttons.
