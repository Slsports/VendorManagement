The Rep groups page shows who reps what: each group with its contact and how many of our vendors and other lines it carries.

## How to
1. Click **Rep groups** in the menu.
2. Each card shows the group's name, contact, phone and email, and "… vendors · … other lines".
3. To sort, use the **Sort** box: **Name**, **Contact**, **Vendors** or **Other lines**. The arrow beside it flips the order.
4. Click a card to open the rep group.

## Good to know
- Rep groups come from rep line lists and the vendor form.
