Every item in the side menu, in order, with one line about each. "Not ready yet" pages show what they will do once built.

## Overview
- **Dashboard**: your number boxes and cards (orders, mail, reviews, tasks, freight); click one to see the list.
- **Mail**: orders@ and your own mailbox, filed by vendor; read, answer and send from VMS.
- **Tasks**: to-dos with a due date and reminders, for you or someone else.
- **Review queue**: proposals waiting for a decision (duplicates, new senders, renames and more). Admin, managers and buyers.
- **Team**: what waits on each person, and for how long. Dana only.

## Procurement
- **Vendors**: every vendor with route, standing, contacts, documents, scores and buying rules.
- **Rep groups**: who reps what, their people and lines.
- **Lines**: catalogs from rep line lists and WWD show listings; promote one to a vendor.
- **WWD contacts**: the Worldwide Distributors roster with key contacts first.
- **Orders**: every order from placement to payment; the arrow lists orders by status.
- **Purchase Orders**: not ready yet (POs pushed into LS).

## Operations
- **Returns & Credits**: not ready yet (returns, credits and RMAs).
- **Incoming Shipments**: not ready yet (what is due to arrive, on a calendar).
- **Freight**: bills from our freight companies, matched to orders, what's left to pay, and every freight company's emails.
- **Buying Shows**: not ready yet (show planning and prep).
- **Payments**: Bill.com and WWD payments that mark bills and orders paid. Admin and managers.

## Insights
- **Sales Reports**: not ready yet (vendor and category reports from LS).
- **Reports**: Vendor scores now; more reports later.
- **Product Sourcing**: not ready yet (items you are looking to source).

## System
- **File Manager**: not ready yet (all documents in one place).
- **Settings**: organization, stores, WWD roster, review assignments and mail. Dana only.
