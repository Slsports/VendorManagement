Delete removes a conversation from every VMS list and moves it to Gmail's Trash. You can bring it back for 30 days.

## How to
1. Open the conversation and click **Delete**, or tick rows in the Mail list and click **Delete**.
2. Confirm when asked.
3. To bring one back, click the **Deleted** tab on the Mail page.
4. Click the round arrow on its row, or tick several and click **Restore**.

## Good to know
- Gmail empties its Trash after 30 days; after that it cannot be restored.
- The **Deleted** tab shows who deleted each one and when.
- Files already saved to a vendor's Documents stay where they are.
- If a new message arrives in a deleted conversation, it comes back on its own.
