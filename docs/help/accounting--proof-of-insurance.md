Some companies must get our policies every year: the lender on the commercial loan and the companies we have contracts with. VMS keeps the list, makes a send task when a new policy comes in, and logs what went to whom.

## How to
1. Click **Accounting** in the menu, then **Insurance**, then the **Who needs proof** tab.
2. **Find requesters in the mail** looks through billing@ for people who asked for a certificate or proof of insurance and lists them as **To confirm**. Open one, tick **Which policies** they need, say why, and save as **Confirmed**. ✓ confirms, ✕ ignores.
3. **Add someone** adds a person by hand: company, person, email, which policies, and whether they need it at renewal or by a date each year. If they take it on a website instead of by email, fill in **How to send it**.
4. When a policy's paper arrives, click **Policy received** on the policy (or let VMS do it: when the new policy paper is filed to the policy under **Documents**, the tasks come on their own; old papers filed later never make one). Each confirmed person who needs it gets a task "Send … to …", due in a week.
5. Click the task. It opens the person with the email ready: right address, the policy attached if it is on file, standard wording, from billing@. Click **Send**. VMS logs the date and policy year and closes the task.
6. If Hub International sent it instead, click **Hub sent it** and enter the date.

## Good to know
- **Community West Bank** (the commercial loan) needs proof every year by about Nov 6. The bank takes it through **myinsuranceinfo.com**, so its task says to upload it there. When Myinsuranceinfo emails billing@ that it got the file (whether you or Hub uploaded it), VMS logs the send and closes the task on its own.
- Anyone who needs proof by a date each year gets a task 30 days before that date, unless it was already sent for the year.
- When someone on the list emails asking for proof, their row shows **They asked** and the Accounting overview lists them. Sending clears it.
- **What was sent** on each person shows every send with the policy year and who sent it.
- A send task not done within a week keeps reminding you the way you chose under Notifications.
