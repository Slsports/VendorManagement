Every order gets our PO number as soon as it's in VMS: the vendor's **PO abbreviation** and the order date, like **YAKIMA080626** or **PLANET102026**. Give that number to the vendor when you place the order. Nothing is made in Lightspeed yet.

## How it works
1. Make or import the order. The PO shows on the order right away.
2. To use a different number, open the order, click **Edit** and change **PO**. Leaving it empty gives the order our PO again.
3. When the vendor's confirmation comes in, VMS checks their PO against ours (see "The PO on a confirmation").
4. The PO is made in Lightspeed only once, with all the items, when Trevor sends the approved LS PO to Lightspeed (see "The LS PO"). Its **Reference #** is our PO.

## The vendor's PO abbreviation and Lightspeed vendor
- On the vendor page, the **Lightspeed** box shows which Lightspeed vendor it is and its **PO abbreviation**. It's also in **Edit** › **PO abbreviation**, under Minimum order, with the next PO shown under it.
- Type the abbreviation you want for long names (WFS for World Famous Sports, PLANET for Planet Cotton). With none typed, VMS makes one from the vendor name (KELLIS, SHARKEYES, YAKIMA).
- If it says **not matched yet**, type part of the name under **Find it in Lightspeed** and click the right one. **Refresh from Lightspeed** brings in vendors added in LS since. The LS PO can't go to Lightspeed until the vendor is matched.

## Good to know
- A second order to the same vendor on the same day gets **-2** at the end.
- If you change the order date before any paperwork comes in, a PO VMS made changes with it. A PO you typed never changes on its own.
- Retail stores (Costco, Amazon, Walmart…) keep their own order numbers; they don't get ours.
- There are no **Create PO** tasks any more.
- Lightspeed also gives the PO its own number (like #10). We don't use it; VMS keeps it only to find the PO again.
