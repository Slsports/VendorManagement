Sign out when you are done on a shared computer or someone else's phone.

## How to
1. Click your name or initials at the top right of the screen.
2. Click **Sign out**.

## Good to know
- You go back to the sign-in page.
- On your own devices you can stay signed in; VMS remembers you until you sign out.
