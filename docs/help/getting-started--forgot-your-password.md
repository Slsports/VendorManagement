If you can't remember your password, VMS emails you a link to choose a new one.

## How to
1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?** (next to the Password box).
2. Type the email address you sign in with and click **Send reset link**.
3. You'll see "Check your email". Open the email and click the link in it.
4. VMS opens **Choose a new password**. Type it in **New password** and again in **Confirm new password**.
5. Click **Save password**. You are signed in and taken to the dashboard.

## Good to know
- The password must be at least 8 characters.
- The link only works for a short time. If it has expired, start again from **Forgot password?**.
- Until the new password is saved, every page in VMS takes you back to the new-password form.
- Click **Back to sign in** to leave without resetting.
- No email? Check your spam folder, and make sure you used the same email address you sign in with.
