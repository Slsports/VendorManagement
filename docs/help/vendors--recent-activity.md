At the top of every vendor's page, **Recent activity** lists the last 5 things that happened with that vendor, newest first. Each line is the date and one sentence, for example "10/19/26 Dana sent the order for kids baseball shirts to Maryellen and asked about ink suggestions."

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page.
2. Read **Recent activity** just above **Overview**.
3. Click any line to open that email. It opens already scrolled to it.

## Good to know
- Claude writes the sentence once, when the email comes in. A brand-new email shows its subject for a minute or two until its sentence is ready.
- Plain thank-yous, "got it" and out-of-office replies are left out. Emailed invoices, credits, confirmations and tracking notices stay in.
- It covers the same mail as the vendor's **Mail** box (Primary, not Promotions), including mail from Worldwide or a rep group that's tagged to this vendor.
- Mail in someone's own inbox shows only for that person and the admin, unless it was shared to Orders.
- A vendor with no mail in VMS doesn't show the box.
