The LS PO is made in LS after the vendor confirms the order, so it matches what they are shipping.

## How to
1. When an order is confirmed, it shows **LS PO needed** on the Ordering card and on the order page.
2. Make the PO in LS.
3. On the order page, type the number in the **LS PO** box and click **Save**. Saving the LS PO file to the order's **LS POs** folder also clears it.

## Good to know
- Once VMS is connected to LS, the order will get a **Create PO in LS** button: VMS builds the PO from the confirmed items, you approve it, and VMS sends it and saves the number.
