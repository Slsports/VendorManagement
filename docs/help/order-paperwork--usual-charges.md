Some vendors always add a charge, like Planet Cotton's tags at $0.15 each. Save it once and Claude stops flagging it on their confirmations and invoices.

## How to
1. On a confirmation or invoice check, find the red row for the charge.
2. Click **This is a usual charge for this vendor** under its name.
3. Check the **Charge**, **Price** and **Per** (Claude fills them in from the paper) and click **Save and check again**.
4. Claude compares the paper again, and the charge comes off the issues and the draft email.

## Good to know
- You can also add or change them on the vendor's **Edit** page, under **Usual charges**.
- At a different price than saved, the charge is still flagged.
- Planet Cotton starts with Tags at $0.15 each.
