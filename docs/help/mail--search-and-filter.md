Narrow the list to find a conversation or to see someone else's mail.

## How to
1. On the Mail page, type in **Search subjects…** and press Enter. Clear the box to see everything again.
2. Use the drop-down beside it to pick whose mail: **Mine**, **Everyone**, **Nobody's yet** or one person by name.
3. Tick **Not filed to a vendor** to see only mail that is not on a vendor yet.

## Good to know
- Search looks at subjects. To find everything with one person, click their name in a conversation instead (see "See all mail with one person").
- Use the **Everything** tab and **All** status to search across all mail, handled included.
- **Mine** is the mail you own. Dana also sees all freight mail under Mine while Trevor is new.
- For Dana, the **Showing** switch in the top bar sets who Mail opens on (Me, Everyone or one person). A choice made on the Mail page wins.
- The "whose mail" drop-down is hidden in a personal mailbox; all of it is the owner's.
