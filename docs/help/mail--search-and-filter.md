One search box finds mail by anything in it: a person's name or email address, a vendor (or its other names), a contact, a rep group, words in the email, a PO, or an attachment's file name.

## How to
1. On the Mail page, start typing in the search box. Suggestions come up under it: **People** (with their email address), **Vendors**, **Rep groups** and **Orders**.
2. Click a suggestion to see all mail with that person, vendor, rep group or order. Or press Enter (or click **Search all mail for …**) to search for the words you typed.
3. Narrow it with the buttons under the box: **Has attachment**, **No attachment**, **PDF**, **Spreadsheet**, **Pictures**, **Last 7 / 30 / 90 days**, **From date** and **To date**, **Received** or **Sent**, and what Claude found: **Confirmation**, **Invoice**, **Shipping notice**, **Artwork**. Click a button again to turn it off.
4. Each conversation found shows why it came up (**From**, **Sent to**, **Contact**, **Vendor**, **Attachment**, **PO**, **Subject** or **In the email**) with the matching words marked. Click the subject to open the exact email that matched.
5. Click **Clear the search** to go back to your lists.

## Good to know
- Every word you type must match, anywhere in the email, a name, an address or a file name. Fewer words find more.
- The search looks at all the mail in the mailbox you are in, whatever tab you are on, handled and waiting alike.
- Shortcuts you can type, if you like them: from:sharon, to:jarrett, has:attachment, has:pdf, has:spreadsheet, has:picture, no:attachment, after:2026-09-01, before:2026-10-01, in:sent.
- **Back** from an email returns to your search with its results.
- Private mailboxes stay private: search only shows mail you can already see. Billing@ is searched on the Accounting mail page.
- The same search is in a vendor's **Mail** box (only that vendor's mail) and on the Accounting mail page.
- When not searching, use the drop-down to pick whose mail (**Mine**, **Everyone**, **Nobody's yet** or one person) and tick **Not filed to a vendor** for unfiled mail.
