The round buttons at the top of the Mail page switch between the shared orders@ mail and your own private mailbox.

## How to
1. Click **Mail** in the menu.
2. Click **Orders (shared)** for orders@, or **My mail** for your own mailbox.
3. Dana also sees a button for each person's mailbox and one for **Old Gmail**.

## Good to know
- **Orders (shared)** is everyone's: every conversation there is seen by the whole team.
- **My mail** is private: only you and Dana (the admin) see it. A note under the buttons says so.
- In your own mailbox every conversation is yours, so there is no "whose mail" filter.
- **Old Gmail** is the old shaverlakesports@gmail.com, forwarded into VMS. Only Dana sees it.
- The buttons only show once a personal mailbox has been set up for you.
