Every imported Bill.com payment is kept as history, on the Payments page and on the vendor's own page.

## How to
1. To see one vendor's payments: open the vendor and find the **Payments** box. It lists the date, invoice, method, confirmation number and amount, with the total at the top. Click **Order** to open the order it paid.
2. To see the latest across all vendors (admin and managers): click **Payments** in the menu and scroll to **Recent payments**. Click **Freight bill** or **Order** beside a payment to open what it paid.

## Good to know
- The vendor's **Payments** box only shows once there is at least one payment.
- WWD payments show in the vendor's **WWD invoices** box instead.
