Each vendor has one page with everything about them: details, documents, mail, people, orders, payments and notes.

## How to
1. Click **Vendors** in the menu and click the vendor's name (or pick it from the top search bar).
2. Read the tags under the name: billing routes, standing (**Do not order**, **Last resort**, **On hold**), **WWD 0% upcharge**, **Fishing**, **Delivery vendor**, **Inactive**.
3. Right under the name is **Our rep**: name, email and phone. Click the email to write to them, or the phone number to call. No rep yet? Click **Add our rep**: **Reps & contacts** opens right there with **Rep** ticked. Fill in the name, email and phone and click **Save contact**; they become our rep.
4. Below the **Overview** are the other boxes: **Ordering windows**, **Ordering** (flags, sales report, suggested order), **Tasks**, **Documents**, **Mail**, **Rep group**, **Reps & contacts**, **Worldwide shows**, **Scorecard**, **What to order from them**, **Order history**, **Payments**, **WWD invoices** and **Notes**.
5. Each box starts closed, showing just its name and a count, for example "Mail · 142 conversations" or "Documents · 37 files". Click the box's title bar to open it, and click it again to close it.
6. Click **Edit** (top right) to change the vendor's details.
7. Click **Back** (top left) to return to where you came from.

## Good to know
- The **Overview** only shows details that are filled in: LS name, assigned person, aliases, our rep, payment terms, orders email, phone, website, account #, address, ordering frequency, minimum order, free shipping, freight routing, pickup, shipping contact and more.
- A colored warning at the top shows when a vendor is on hold, last resort or do not order, with the reasons.
- Boxes start closed so you do not have to scroll past long lists of mail and files. The count shows what is inside before you open it; "None" means it is empty.
- Buttons on a box's title bar, like **New email**, **Upload**, **Add order** and **Task**, work while the box is closed.
- **Payments** and **WWD invoices** only appear once there is at least one.
- Viewers see the page but not the Edit, Upload or Add buttons.
