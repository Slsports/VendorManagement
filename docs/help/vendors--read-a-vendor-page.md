Each vendor has one page with everything about them: details, documents, mail, people, orders, payments and notes.

## How to
1. Click **Vendors** in the menu and click the vendor's name (or pick it from the top search bar).
2. Read the tags under the name: billing routes, standing (**Do not order**, **Last resort**, **On hold**), **WWD 0% upcharge**, **Fishing**, **Delivery vendor**, **Inactive**.
3. Scroll down through the boxes: **Overview**, **Ordering windows**, **Ordering** (flags, sales report, suggested order), **Tasks**, **Documents**, **Mail**, **Rep group**, **Reps & contacts**, **Worldwide shows**, **Scorecard**, **What to order from them**, **Order history**, **Payments**, **WWD invoices** and **Notes**.
4. Click **Edit** (top right) to change the vendor's details.
5. Click **Back** (top left) to return to where you came from.

## Good to know
- The **Overview** only shows details that are filled in: LS name, assigned person, aliases, our rep, payment terms, orders email, phone, website, account #, address, ordering frequency, minimum order, free shipping, freight routing, pickup, shipping contact and more.
- A colored warning at the top shows when a vendor is on hold, last resort or do not order, with the reasons.
- **Payments** and **WWD invoices** only appear once there is at least one.
- Viewers see the page but not the Edit, Upload or Add buttons.
