Some vendors credit the freight back when the invoice is paid by a date. Record it on the order so VMS reminds you before the date passes.

## How to
1. Open the order.
2. If the vendor is set up as one that credits freight, the **Freight allowance** box says so: click **Add it to this order**. Otherwise click **+ Freight allowance for paying on time**.
3. Fill in **Freight they credit back** (leave blank if not known yet), **If paid by** (starts at the due date) and, later, **Credit received on**.
4. Click **Save**. Use **Edit** to change it later, or **Remove** to take it off.

## Good to know
- The box tells you where things stand: days left to pay, "Pay today to keep it", "Paid on time. Watch for the credit", or a red warning if it was paid late.
- With a pay-by date, Dana gets a task "Pay … by … to get the freight back", reminded 5 days before (see "Pay-by reminders").
- A WWD credit on the order marks the credit as received.
- To have every order from a vendor show this, tick "Credits the freight back when we pay on time" on the vendor's edit form.
