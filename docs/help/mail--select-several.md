Tick several conversations to mark them handled, snooze them or delete them in one go.

## How to
1. On the Mail page, tick the box at the start of each row you want. The box in the header ticks every open row.
2. A bar appears above the list showing how many are selected.
3. Click **Mark handled**, **Snooze** or **Delete**.
4. Click **Clear** to untick them all.

## Good to know
- Handled rows cannot be ticked; there is nothing left to do with them.
- **Mark handled** offers **Undo** for a few seconds.
- In the **Deleted** tab the bar shows **Restore** instead.
