A few things work the same on every page.

## How to
1. **Go back:** click **Back** at the top of a detail page (a vendor, order, rep group, review item). It returns to where you came from, with your filters still set.
2. **Sort a list:** click a column heading. Click it again to reverse the order. The small arrow shows which way it is sorted.
3. **Save a long form:** **Save** and **Cancel** stay pinned to the bottom of the screen while you scroll. "Saving…" shows while it saves, and any problem shows right on the bar.

## Good to know
- If you opened a page from a link or bookmark, the back link names the list it goes to instead, for example **Dashboard**.
- Short forms and pop-up boxes keep their buttons where they are.
