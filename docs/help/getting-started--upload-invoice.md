Invoice uploaders see only one page, **Upload invoice**. It is not ready yet.

## How it will work
1. Sign in; you land on **Upload invoice** straight away.
2. Scan or upload a paper invoice from your phone.
3. It goes to the review queue with your name and store on it.

## Good to know
- Today the page says "Arrives in Phase 3". Until then, keep handling paper invoices the way you do now.
- Invoice uploaders don't see the rest of VMS or the search box.
