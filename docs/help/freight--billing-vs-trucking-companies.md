VMS keeps two kinds of freight companies apart, because the company that bills us is often not the one driving the truck.

## Billing companies
- We book shipments with them and pay them: Priority One, PartnerShip, Worldwide Express (WWEX / ShipStation), UPS DIRECT and WWD.
- Freight bills always belong to a billing company. They show as cards at the top of the **Freight** page.

## Trucking companies
- They haul the freight, hired by a billing company: for example XPO and Oak Harbor Freight.
- They are listed under **Trucking companies** with who usually hires them.
- We do not pay them directly, but VMS still reads their tracking and delivery receipts.

## Good to know
- To change which kind a company is, click the pencil beside it and change **What they do**.
- WWD freight bills come from the WWD portal and are uploaded like any other bill.
