Worldwide can't export their vendor directory, so VMS reads it from the portal pages you send it with a bookmark. First you send two pages so VMS can learn how the portal is laid out; after that, one click sends every vendor.

## How to
1. On a computer, open **Settings** › **Worldwide**.
2. Drag the green **Send to VMS** button up to your browser's bookmarks bar.
3. Sign in to the Worldwide portal and open the **Vendors** list. Click the **Send to VMS** bookmark. A message says "Sent to VMS".
4. Open one vendor, open all of its sections (Address, Contacts, Programs, Product Lines, Resources), and click **Send to VMS** again.
5. Back in **Settings** › **Worldwide**, both pages show under **Worldwide portal → VMS**.

## Good to know
- If the portal won't let the page go to VMS, the page downloads as **wwd-page.html**. Click **Upload a saved page** in **Settings** › **Worldwide** and pick that file.
- The bookmark carries your own private key. Don't share it.
- Only the admin can see the pages sent.
