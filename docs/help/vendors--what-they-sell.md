Each vendor's page shows what they sell from two sides, so you can see what else they carry beyond what we buy from them.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page.
2. In the Overview, click **Show** on **Their info and what they sell** (it stays closed until you open it, and remembers your choice) and find **What they sell**.
3. Read the two lists side by side:
   - **We buy from them (our LS categories)**: the Lightspeed categories of their items, with how many items we still carry in each. Categories with nothing left say **no longer carried**, in gray.
   - **Worldwide lists (their Product Lines)**: every product line the Worldwide portal shows for them, in Worldwide's words, like **TOYS › SLEDS & SNOW TOYS**.
4. Below them, **Added by us or from their form** holds anything typed in by hand or sent on their vendor form. To add one, type it and click **Add**.

## Good to know
- Worldwide's names are not the same as ours, so VMS does not try to match them. Look down the Worldwide list for lines we don't buy.
- The lists fill themselves: ours from Lightspeed, Worldwide's from the portal whenever it is read again.
- A vendor that is not matched to a Lightspeed vendor shows "Not matched to a Lightspeed vendor yet."
