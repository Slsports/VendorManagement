VMS is updated often. A page that has been open a while keeps running the version it loaded, so new buttons and cards may not show yet.

## How to
1. When a new version is out, a green bar appears at the top: **A new version of VMS is ready. Tap here to refresh.** Tap it.
2. No bar but something looks out of date? Reload the page: in Safari or Chrome tap the reload arrow; on an iPad or phone home-screen icon, swipe the app closed and open it again.

## Good to know
- VMS checks for a new version every 5 minutes and whenever you come back to it.
- Signing out and back in does not reload the page; refreshing does.
