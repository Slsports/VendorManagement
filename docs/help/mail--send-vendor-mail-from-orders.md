Orders, returns and problems with vendors should go out of orders@ so everyone can follow them. When you send from your own inbox, VMS checks first.

## How to
1. Write or reply from your own inbox as usual and click **Send**.
2. If it is going to a vendor, rep group or carrier, or looks like an order, a return or a problem with an order, a box asks **Send this from orders@?**
3. Click **Send from orders@ instead** to send it from orders@. It starts a new conversation there, filed to the vendor.
4. Or click **Send from my inbox anyway** to send it from your own inbox.
5. **Go back** returns to the email without sending.

## Good to know
- Mail that has nothing to do with vendors goes out with no question.
- Mail only to our own people never asks.
- Email you send from orders@ never asks.
