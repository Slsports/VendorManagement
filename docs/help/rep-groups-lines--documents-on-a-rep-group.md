A rep group's own papers, the ones that are not about one vendor, live on the rep group's page: show information, buyer letters, line lists, specials sheets. A paper about one of the rep group's vendors (an order form or invoice for Toysmith) lives under that vendor instead.

## Where to look
- On the rep group's page, the **Documents** box lists the group's files, newest first, with the folder they belong in (Other, Show specials, Price lists, Order forms, Catalogs). Click a name to open it in VMS.

## How files get there
- From **Settings** › **Dropbox**: Claude proposes "Rep group" for a paper that is clearly the rep's, and you can send any file there yourself with **Rep group…** on the file.
- Files about a vendor never go here; they go under the vendor.
