Snooze a card to hide it from your queue until later. It comes back at the top when the time is up.

## How to
1. On the card, click **Snooze**.
2. Pick **Later today (3 hours)**, **Tomorrow morning**, **In 2 days**, **Next Monday**, or a date and time.
3. To see snoozed cards, click **Snoozed (N)** at the top of the queue. **Back to the queue** returns.
4. To bring one back early, click **Snooze** on it and choose **Unsnooze now**.

## Good to know
- A snooze is yours only; others still see the card.
- Mornings mean 8 am.
- A returning card is marked **Back from snooze** until it is settled.
- Snoozed cards also leave your dashboard count.
