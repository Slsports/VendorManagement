Dana can look at the Orders page the way one person sees their work: the orders they placed and the orders for vendors assigned to them.

## How to
1. In the top bar, open the **Showing** box and pick the person (or **Everyone**).
2. Click **Orders** in the menu. The list now shows only that person's orders.
3. Pick **Showing: Me** to go back to the full list.

## Good to know
- "Their orders" means orders they placed, plus orders for vendors where they are the assigned buyer.
- A coloured bar across the top reminds you whose view you are looking at.
- The Dashboard's **Confirmations & invoices to review** card follows the same switch.
