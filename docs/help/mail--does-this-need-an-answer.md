Claude reads new mail waiting on us and keeps "Needs an answer" for mail that really needs one. When it is not sure, it asks you.

## How to
1. Click **Review queue** and find a **Does this need an answer?** card (it goes to the conversation's owner).
2. Read the sender and first words, or click **Open the email**.
3. Click **Needs an answer** to keep it on your list, or **No answer needed** to mark it handled.
4. If the email is an order, click **Create an order**. (File the email to a vendor first if it is not.)

## Good to know
- Tracking updates, delivery notices, automatic invoice notices, receipts, ads and "thanks" are marked handled on their own.
- Questions and requests stay in **Needs an answer**.
- Say **No answer needed** three times for one sender and their unsure emails are handled on their own after that (a real question still stays).
- Mail from Marketing senders is handled without asking.
