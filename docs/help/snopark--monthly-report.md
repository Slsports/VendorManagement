On the 1st of each month in season, VMS makes a task for the person who runs Sno-Park, due the 15th. The task opens the report page. Only empty books go on a monthly report; a partly sold book stays with us until it is empty.

## How to
1. Open the task, or **Sno-Park** in the menu and the report under **Reports**.
2. **1. The books**: tick every book that is empty (every permit in it sold). Click **Work out the report**.
3. **2. The State's numbers**: VMS lists each empty book as a From / Including range, the quantity, what we collected, our commission and what is due to the State, the way their form does, and the grand total. Click **Make the State's Excel**: the file is made from the State's own form and filed, and Dana gets a task to write the check for the total. Then click **Print the form**: VMS's printable copy of the form opens with the same numbers and our store information; print it for the envelope.
4. **3. The book covers**: click **Photograph the covers** and take a picture of the cover of every empty book going back (several at once is fine), on your phone or from a scan. They are our proof of what went back.
5. Put the printed form, the check and the empty books in the envelope and mail it to OHMVR Division, P.O. Box 942896, Sacramento, CA 94296.
6. **4. Mail it**: enter the date (and the tracking number if there is one) and click **Mailed**. The books are marked returned, both tasks close, and VMS files the packet: one PDF with the form first, then the cover pictures in the order you added them, under CA Snow Park in **Submitted permit payments**.

## Good to know
- Ticked the wrong book? Change the ticks and click **Work it out again**, then **Make the Excel again** and **Print the form** again. The old files are kept but no longer current. Nothing can be changed after **Mailed**.
- A wrong permit range comes from the book's entry: fix it with **Change** in the **Books** table on the Sno-Park page, then work the report out again.
- The Excel is named the State's way: the report number in the season, the date it was made and the month, like "02 - 01-15-27 DECEMBER 2026 SNOPARK SUBMISSION".
- Added a cover picture after the report was mailed? Click **File the packet again** and the PDF is rebuilt with it.
- If no book is empty in a month, there is nothing to report; leave the task until a book is empty or tell Dana.
- The Lightspeed line under the books is a check: permits sold in LS this season next to what the books say. The difference is permits sold from books that are not empty yet.
