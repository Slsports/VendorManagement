Before answering who a sender is, you can read their emails and file them one by one.

## How to
1. On a **Who is this mail from?** card, click **See the N emails**.
2. A panel opens with their emails. Click one to read it, or **Open the whole conversation**.
3. Tick the emails to file, then click **File to a vendor** (pick the vendor), **Marketing** or **Other – not a vendor**.
4. To answer for the whole sender, including future mail, tick **Select all** first.
5. Close the panel with the **X**.

## Good to know
- Filing some emails one by one does not decide the sender; new mail from them will ask again.
- The card closes when every email is handled.
- Back from an email returns you to this panel.
