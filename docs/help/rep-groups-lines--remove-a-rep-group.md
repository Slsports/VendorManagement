Remove a rep group that no longer exists or was added by mistake.

## How to
1. Open the rep group's page.
2. Click **Remove rep group** (top right).
3. Click **OK** to confirm.

## Good to know
- Its vendors stay vendors, just without this rep group. Its reps are removed.
