Bring whole vendor folders (for example from Dropbox) into VMS at once. VMS sorts each file to its vendor, folder and year, and you check the list before anything is filed.

## How to
1. Click **Vendors** in the menu, then **Bulk import documents**.
2. Drop vendor folders on the box (like "Stansport" with its Invoices and Catalogs folders inside), or click **Choose a folder** or **Choose files**.
3. Look at **Need a look**: files where the vendor or folder was not clear. Click **Ask Claude about …** to have Claude read them, or pick the vendor (**Which vendor?**), the folder and the year yourself. When Claude is unsure, check it and click **Looks right**.
4. Look at **Ready to file**, grouped by vendor. Untick any file you do not want, or change its vendor, folder or year.
5. Click **File … documents**.

## Good to know
- Sorting uses the folder names first ("Stansport/Invoices/2024"), then the file name, then the file's date.
- Files already in VMS (same vendor, name and size) are skipped and listed under **Already in VMS**.
- Files over 50 MB are left out.
- After filing, **Filed** links to each vendor so you can check.
- **Clear the list** removes what has not been filed yet.
