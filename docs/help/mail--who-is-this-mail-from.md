When mail comes from a sender VMS does not know, it asks once in the Review queue. Your answer covers all their mail, now and later.

## How to
1. Click **Review queue** in the menu and find a **Who is this mail from?** card.
2. Click **See the N emails** to read them first.
3. Pick an answer:
   - **Yes, [vendor]** when VMS proposed the right vendor, or **Pick or add the vendor** / **Another vendor**.
   - **A rep group**: each email is then filed by the line it names.
   - **A service like Bill.com or Faire**: each email is filed by the vendor it names.
   - **A freight carrier**: their mail moves to Freight.
   - **Marketing**: ads from a company we never bought from. Their mail goes to Offers & catalogs.
   - **Other – not a vendor**: someone who may need an answer. Their mail stays in Needs attention.

## Good to know
- The card shows recent subjects and, when VMS had no guess, what Claude read in the mail.
- In **See the N emails**, tick some to file just those; **Select all** answers for the whole sender.
- If you only file some, new mail from that sender asks again.
- Private mailboxes never make these cards.
