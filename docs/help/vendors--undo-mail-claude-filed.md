When Claude decides an unknown sender is one of our vendors, it files that sender's mail to the vendor. If it got it wrong, undo it from the vendor page.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and find **Mail**.
2. Under the conversations, **Mail filed here automatically** lists each sender Claude filed, with its reason. Click the sender to see all mail from them (a sender like @company.com opens everyone at that company).
3. Click **Undo** next to the sender, then **OK**.

## Good to know
- Its conversations leave the vendor and the sender goes back to **Who is this?** in the Review queue for a person to answer.
- Conversations someone filed by hand are not touched.
- Senders filed with **This is…** from the Never bought from list show here too.
