When you email a vendor and hear nothing back, VMS brings the conversation back to you with a one-click follow-up.

## How to
1. Open the conversation from **No answer yet** (Mail page or the **Mail for you** box on the dashboard).
2. Click **Follow up**. A reply opens to the same people, saying "Just following up on my email below."
3. Change the words if you like and click **Send**.

## Good to know
- After you send, the conversation shows **Waiting on vendor**. If there is still no answer after the follow-up days (5 unless Dana changed it in **Settings > Mail**), it turns red as **No answer yet**.
- **Follow up** shows on conversations waiting on the vendor; it is the main button once one is overdue.
- When the vendor answers, it moves to **Needs an answer** on its own.
