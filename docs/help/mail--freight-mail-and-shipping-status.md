Mail from freight carriers is sorted, read and tied to vendors on its own.

## How to
1. Click **Mail**, then the **Freight** tab to see carrier mail.
2. Bills are waiting on the Freight bills page; for an odd one, use **Freight bill** on the PDF.

## Good to know
- Carrier mail goes to Trevor; Dana sees it all for now.
- Carrier status updates show a tag on the conversation: **Picked up**, **In transit**, **Out for delivery**, **Delivered** or **Shipping problem**. The update is filed to the vendor that shipped.
- Claude reads carrier bills (such as Worldwide Express PDFs) and makes the freight bill.
- Delivery receipts are read: the shipper is the vendor, the PO finds the order, and the receipt is saved to the vendor's and order's files.
- An email saying a bill was paid marks the bill paid.
- A freight rate quoted in an email shows as a yellow note on the conversation.
