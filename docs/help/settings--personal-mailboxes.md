The **Personal mailboxes and the old Gmail** box on the Mail tab shows whether each personal mailbox is syncing.

## How to
1. Open **Settings** and click **Mail**.
2. Find **Personal mailboxes and the old Gmail**. Each line shows the name, address and a status: **Synced …**, **still loading**, **Waiting for its first sync**, **Not connected** (with the problem underneath) or **arrives in orders@** for the old Gmail.

## Good to know
- Personal mail is private to its owner and Dana.
- Personal mailboxes load their last 90 days, then keep up every minute.
- The old Gmail is what it forwards to oldslsgmail@, which arrives with orders@.
- The box only shows once a personal mailbox is set up.
