Some carriers email that a bill is ready but do not attach it. Those bills show **Needs the PDF** until you add it.

## How to
1. Open the bill from the **Freight** page.
2. Click **Log in to (carrier)** on the bill, download the bill PDF from their website.
3. Back on the bill, click **Add the bill PDF** and choose the file.
4. Claude reads it and fills in the shippers and amounts. Check the lines below and confirm them.

## Good to know
- **Replace PDF** swaps in a different file; Claude reads the new one.
- **Read again** asks Claude to read the same PDF again if something came out wrong.
- If Claude had trouble, a yellow note on the bill says why.
- If the bill does not say which billing company sent it, a yellow box asks **Which billing company sent this bill?** Pick it from the list.
- The PDF, the email it came in and any payment receipt are linked at the top of the bill.
