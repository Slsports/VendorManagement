For a Worldwide vendor, the **Worldwide portal** box on the vendor page shows what the portal says: the buying programs, product lines, returns and brands.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page.
2. Click **Worldwide portal**.
3. Read each program: the Standard terms and each show (Fall Show 2026, Spring Show 2026) with discount, terms, freight, minimum, where it ships from and the ship dates.
4. Below, read zero upcharge (Yes or No), the product lines, brands, returns and the files the portal has for the vendor (order writers, specials).

## Good to know
- A green tag shows how long a show program runs, amber means it ends within 30 days, and gray means expired. Expired programs stay, so you can check an order against the terms it was placed under.
- The people the portal lists are in **Reps & contacts** as not yet confirmed. The portal lists reps for every area of the country, not only ours; the territory and rep company are in each person's note.
- The box only shows for vendors found in the portal. Direct and Faire vendors are not in the portal.
