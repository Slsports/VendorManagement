The season ends May 30. Every book goes back to the State, sold or not, with the final report and the last check, by June 20. VMS makes the final report's task on June 1.

## How to
1. Open the task, or the **Final report** under **Reports** on the Sno-Park page.
2. **1. The books**: tick every book that is sold out. For a book that is only partly sold, tick it and type the **Last permit sold** in it. Leave an untouched book unticked. Click **Work out the report**.
3. **2. The State's numbers**: the sold ranges and the money, plus **Unsold permits going back**: the rest of each partly sold book and every untouched book. Click **Make the State's Excel**, then **Print the form**: the printable form has the Unsold box and the declaration to sign, which the State's Excel does not.
4. **3. The book covers**: photograph the cover of every book going back, sold or not.
5. Sign the declaration. Put the form, the check and all the books in the envelope and mail it to OHMVR Division, P.O. Box 942896, Sacramento, CA 94296.
6. **4. Mail it**: enter the date and click **Mailed**. Every book is marked returned, the season closes, and VMS files the packet (the form first, then the covers) under CA Snow Park in **Season final reports**.

## Good to know
- **The season-end check**: when the final report is mailed, VMS compares what Lightspeed sold this season, day and season permits, with everything on the monthly and final reports. If they match, the season card says so. If they are off, VMS emails Dana the summary itself, with the numbers; no one has to send it. **Run the check now** on the season card runs it again.
- Nothing can be sent to the State next season while any permit or payment is outstanding, so every book must be on this report.
- The next season opens on its own when the State's new vendor packet email arrives.
