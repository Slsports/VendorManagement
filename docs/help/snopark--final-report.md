The season ends May 30. Every book goes back to the State, sold or not, with the final report and the last check, by June 20. VMS makes the final report's task on June 1.

## How to
1. Open the task, or the **Final report** under **Reports** on the Sno-Park page.
2. **1. The books**: tick every book that is sold out. For a book that is only partly sold, tick it and type the **Last permit sold** in it. Leave an untouched book unticked. Click **Work out the report**.
3. **2. The State's numbers**: the sold ranges and the money, plus **Unsold permits going back**: the rest of each partly sold book and every untouched book. Click **Make the State's Excel**. The unsold ranges are written under the sold ranges on the sheet.
4. Upload the scan of the sold passes under **3**.
5. Print the report. Put it, the check and all the books in the envelope and mail it to OHMVR Division, P.O. Box 942896, Sacramento, CA 94296.
6. **4. Mail it**: enter the date and click **Mailed**. Every book is marked returned and the season closes.

## Good to know
- The State's printed form (OHV 54SP) has an Unsold permits box and a declaration to sign; their Excel does not. VMS writes the unsold ranges on the sheet, and the person mailing it signs the declaration on the printout if the State asks for it.
- Nothing can be sent to the State next season while any permit or payment is outstanding, so every book must be on this report.
- The next season opens on its own when the State's new vendor packet email arrives.
