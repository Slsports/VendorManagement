Claude checks each email for whether someone needs to reply. When it is not sure, it asks you with a card.

## How to
1. Open **Review queue** and find the card. It shows who sent it, the date, a bit of the email and Claude's note.
2. Click **Open the email** if you need to read it all.
3. Click **Needs an answer** to keep it in the Needs an answer list, or **No answer needed** to mark it handled.
4. If the email is really an order, click **Create an order** (see "Create an order from a review card").

## Good to know
- After three **No answer needed** for the same sender, that sender's unsure emails are handled on their own.
