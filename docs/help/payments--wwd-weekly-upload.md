Once a week, download this week's files from WWD and Edenred and drop them all in one place in VMS. VMS then files the invoices, marks bills and orders paid, sets the payment reminders and matches up the rest.

## Your Monday task
- Every Monday you get the task **WWD weekly upload**. Click **Go to it** to open the upload box.
- The task closes by itself once you have uploaded.
- If it isn't done by Wednesday morning, Dana gets a note.

## How to
1. In the WWD portal, download the **Payment History** export.
2. In Edenred, download the **invoice export**. Pick the dates from the day after the last upload to today, and choose **Select all** for the columns. The box shows when the last upload was.
3. Get any new **WWD payment sheets** Dana made when paying, and WWD's **Payments Due** list.
4. Click **Payments** in the menu. At the top, in **WWD weekly upload**, click **Choose files** and pick all of the files at once.
5. Each file shows what VMS took it for. Check the summary, then click **Import**.
6. A green note says what was added and what VMS is doing next.

## What VMS does after the upload
- Edenred invoice files in orders@ that were waiting for this upload are read again and filed, usually within a few minutes.
- A freight bill sent through WWD, like XPO, becomes a freight bill under that freight company.
- Paid bills and orders are marked paid, and the payment reminders are set. Bills with a discount are reminded 3 days before the discount date.
- WWD names that don't have a vendor yet are listed under **WWD names to match**:
  - **Add as new vendor:** for a name that is on the WWD portal list. VMS makes the vendor with the portal's details and moves its invoices there.
  - **Pick vendor:** for a name that belongs to a vendor already in VMS.
  - **Freight company:** for trucking companies.

## Good to know
- Uploading the same file twice adds nothing, so an overlap day is fine.
- Anything VMS still couldn't place is on Dana's task list with the email it came in.
