These three pages are in the menu but are not built yet. Each one shows a "coming soon" note.

## What they will do
- **Purchase Orders**: purchase orders made from vendor confirmations and sent into LS, with receiving read back.
- **Returns & Credits**: returns, credits, defectives and warranties with RMA tracking, emailed to the rep.
- **Buying Shows**: show planning, vendors to visit with booth numbers, checklists, budgets and a phone scan of show orders.

## Until then
- Track credits on the order itself (**Edit** › Credits due, Credit notes, Credits received).
- See what is coming on **Incoming Shipments** and on the dashboard: the **Ordering** card (Placed, Confirmed, Overdue) and the **Future-dated orders** box.
