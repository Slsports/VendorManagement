The **Stores** tab lists the stores with their code, name, aliases, city and whether they are active.

## How to
1. Open **Settings** and click **Stores**.
2. Click a column heading to sort.

## Good to know
- Aliases are the other names spreadsheets use for a store (for example TOWN for SLS, MARINA for SLM, HAPPY or HC for GS). Imports read them as the store code.
- The list can only be looked at for now. Editing addresses, phone numbers and aliases is coming.
