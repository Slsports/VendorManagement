There are two kinds of notes on a vendor: a running list of dated notes anyone can add, and the vendor's standing notes on the Edit form.

## How to
1. To add a dated note: open the vendor's page, scroll to **Notes**, type in **Add a note…** and click **Add note**.
2. To change the vendor's standing notes: click **Edit**, scroll to **Notes**, change **Notes** or **Return notes**, and click **Save changes**.

## Good to know
- Dated notes show who wrote them and when, newest first. They cannot be edited.
- **Return notes** are for how returns and claims work with this vendor: RA numbers, photos, deadlines. They show in the Overview.
- The standing **Notes** show in a gray box in the Overview.
