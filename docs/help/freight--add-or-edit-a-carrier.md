Carriers are the shipping companies. Adding one lets VMS recognize its emails, bills and delivery receipts.

## How to
1. Click **Freight** in the menu.
2. Click **Add carrier** (the dashed box under Billing companies), or the pencil on a carrier to edit it.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name**.
   - **What they do**: **Billing company** (we book and pay them) or **Trucking company** (hauls it). For a trucking company, add **Usually hired by**.
   - **Ships**: **Parcel** or **LTL**.
   - **Email domains**: where its emails come from, like xpo.com.
   - **Website to log in**, **Our UPS account # through them**, **Our account with them**.
   - **Its mail goes to**: who handles its mail (Trevor by default).
4. When editing, you can also tick **Our default for UPS (parcel) shipments**, or untick **Active** to retire a carrier.
5. Click **Add carrier** or **Save**.

## Good to know
- Saving picks up mail already in VMS from those domains, so its bills and delivery receipts are read too.
- A new sender in the review queue can also be marked **A freight carrier** (see "Who is this mail from?").
