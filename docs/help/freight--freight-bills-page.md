The Freight page holds the freight bills (the invoices from the companies we book and pay for shipping) and every freight company we get mail from. Each shipment on a bill is matched to the vendor that shipped it, so the freight lands on that vendor's order.

## How to
1. Click **Freight** in the menu (under Operations).
2. At the top you see the **Billing companies** cards, then the **Trucking companies** list.
3. Below them, pick a tab:
   - **To match**: bills that still need their shipments matched to vendors (the page opens here).
   - **To pay**: bills with an amount that are not marked paid yet.
   - **Matched**: bills that are done.
   - **All**: every bill.
4. Click a bill to open it.
5. At the bottom, **Other freight companies** lists carriers we have no account with, like FedEx and USPS. Click a name for its emails.

## Good to know
- Each row shows the company, our UPS number through them, the invoice number, the shippers on it, the date, the total, where it stands and whether it is paid (with the due date when unpaid).
- Where a bill can stand: **Needs the PDF**, **Reading…**, **To match** (or "3 to match"), **Done**, **Could not read**.
- New bills arrive on their own from the carriers' emails. You can also upload them (see "Upload a batch of freight bills").
- Each billing company card has an **Emails** link; trucking company names open their emails too.
- Everyone can look. Admin, managers and buyers can match, upload and edit.
