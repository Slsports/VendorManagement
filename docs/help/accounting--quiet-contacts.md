Some companies send lots of update emails. For those, VMS files everything under their contact and keeps it out of **Needs an answer**. It only puts a task on your list when an email gives you a deadline for something you have to do.

## How it works
1. **Paychex** and **Blue Shield of California** are set up this way. All of Paychex's mail is filed under **Paychex**, including the Mid Atlantic 401(k) statements, Mineral, Paychex Flex, their surveys and webinars, and the Zurich EPD policy. All of Blue Shield's mail is filed under **Blue Shield of California**.
2. Their emails go straight to **Handled**. Open the contact under **Accounting** → **Contacts** to see all of them in one place.
3. Claude reads each new one. If it says you must do something by a date (sign a form, approve a renewal, finish open enrollment), you get a task like **Paychex: Sign the form by Nov 20**. It reminds you right away, weekly, 3 days before and on the day. Click it to open the email.

## Good to know
- To make another company quiet, open its contact, click **Edit**, and tick **Only tell me about deadlines**.
- A conversation where you are waiting on their answer stays under **Waiting on them**.
- When this started, the last 90 days of their mail were read for deadlines still ahead. Older mail was just filed.
