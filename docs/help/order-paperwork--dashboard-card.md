The Dashboard card **Confirmations & invoices to review** counts the checks waiting on you.

## How to
1. On the **Dashboard**, click the card **Confirmations & invoices to review**.
2. It opens a list: vendor, confirmation or invoice, PO, how long it has waited, and where it stands (for example "2 issues to review", "Everything matches: look it over", "Which order is this for?").
3. Click a row to open the check.

## Good to know
- It shows checks assigned to you. Dana can switch **Showing** to see someone else's or everyone's.
- The count turns red when one has waited 2 days or more.
- The card hides itself when there is nothing to review.
- You can move or hide the card with **Customize**.
