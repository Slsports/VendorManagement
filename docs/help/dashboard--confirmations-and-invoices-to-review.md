When a vendor sends an order confirmation or invoice, Claude checks it against the order. The ones waiting for you to look at are listed here, oldest first.

## How to
1. On the dashboard, click **Confirmations & invoices to review**.
2. Each line shows the vendor, whether it is a confirmation or an invoice, the PO number, how long it has waited and the result.
3. Click a line to open the check, review it and act on it.

## Good to know
- Each check goes to whoever placed the order: Jarrett's orders to Jarrett, everything else to Dana. It can be handed to someone else from the check.
- A wait of 2 days or more shows in red, and the number turns red.
- The card only shows when there is something to review.
- With **Showing** set to Everyone, Dana sees everyone's, with the person's name on each line.
