Each vendor is scored 1 to 5 on six things. Four score themselves from the order history; two come from staff ratings.

## Automatic (from the order history)
- **On-time fulfilment**: received within a week of the ship date they gave. Needs two orders with both dates.
- **Order accuracy**: right items, quantities and hang tags, nothing damaged, from check-in notes. Needs three orders.
- **Shipping**: freight as a share of product cost, free shipping honored, late or lost shipments. Needs two orders with a freight figure.
- **Resolving issues**: credits owed to us, how many came through and how fast.

## Staff ratings
- **Ease of ordering**: forms, portals, confirmations.
- **Communication and help**: does the rep answer and help get the order right?

## Good to know
- Under an automatic line (on time, accuracy, shipping, credits), click **Show the orders** to see the orders it counted and why. Each opens with one click.
- A staff rating always replaces the automatic score for that line.
- **Overall** is the average of the scores the vendor has, to one decimal. The vendor's standing (on hold, last resort, do not order) shows beside it.
