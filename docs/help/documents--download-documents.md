Download a single file, a whole folder, or one year of a folder as a zip.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page, find **Documents** and open a folder.
2. One file: click **Download** under its name.
3. A whole folder: click **Download all** at the top right of the open folder.
4. One year: click **Download year** beside that year.

## Good to know
- Zips are named after the vendor and folder (and year), and keep the folder layout inside.
- While a zip is being made, the button reads "Zipping…".
- Links cannot be downloaded; a folder holding only links says "Only links here, nothing to download."
