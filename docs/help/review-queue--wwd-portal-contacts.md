The Worldwide portal lists people for each vendor, and many are out of date. When the portal lists someone we haven't emailed about that vendor in the last 24 months, VMS asks you on a **WWD portal contacts to check** card, one card per vendor.

## How to
1. Open **Review queue** and click **Vendor changes**.
2. Find the **WWD portal contacts to check** card. It shows each person, their email and phone, and the last time we emailed them (about this vendor, about another vendor, or never).
3. For each person, click:
   - **Keep**: they really are this vendor's contact.
   - **No longer their rep**: they moved on. They're kept under **Former contacts** on the vendor page, so their old mail can still be found.
   - **Delete**: a mistake, never this vendor's contact (like a rep listed under the wrong company).
4. When everyone on the card is answered, the card closes by itself.

## Good to know
- Until you answer, they show in **Reps & contacts** tagged **From WWD · not confirmed**, and they're never the main rep.
- If we email one of them about the vendor, they're confirmed on their own and drop off the card. VMS checks every hour.
- Someone you deleted or marked former is never added back when the portal is read again.
- Your answers are the list of outdated portal info to take to Worldwide at the show.
- Vendors ticked **Retail store** (Costco, Walmart, Amazon…) are left out (see "Retail stores").
