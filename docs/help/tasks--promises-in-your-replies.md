When you reply to a vendor with a promise, like "I will have a chance to look at this by tomorrow", Claude notices. The conversation stays **waiting on us** instead of **waiting on vendor**, and you get a task due that day.

## How it works
1. You reply from orders@ (in VMS or in Gmail) and promise something: look at it, check, confirm, send, call back.
2. Within a minute or two Claude reads your reply and adds a task for whoever signed it, for example "WFS: check order S102112", due the day you promised, by end of business (6 pm).
3. The task is tagged to the vendor and the email, and reminds you the way you chose in your reminder settings (9 am that day).
4. Do what you promised and click the task's circle to finish it. The conversation goes back to **waiting on vendor**.

## Good to know
- Sending another reply in the same conversation finishes the old promise task on its own.
- No day in the promise ("I'll get back to you")? The task is due the next day.
- Only orders@ mail is read for this, not personal inboxes.
- Answers, approvals, orders and "thanks" are not promises, so they still wait on the vendor.
