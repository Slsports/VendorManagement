When the Worldwide portal says something different from what VMS has for a vendor (a phone number, an address, free shipping, freight terms, zero upcharge), VMS keeps yours and asks with a **Worldwide portal differences** card.

## How to
1. Open **Review queue** and click **Vendor changes**.
2. Read the card: the vendor, what **VMS has** and what the **Portal says**.
3. Click **Keep ours** or **Use the portal's**.

## Good to know
- Empty fields were filled from the portal without asking; only differences come here.
- Once you answer, the same difference is not asked again when the portal is read again.
