When an order went out as the vendor's own order form (their Excel sheet filled in and emailed from orders@), you can change it in VMS and send the changes without downloading anything. The **What we ordered** box at the top of the order page shows the items straight off that form.

## How to
1. Open the order. **What we ordered** lists each item with its cases by color, units, cost and total, and says when the form was sent and to whom (click **the email** to see it).
2. Click **Edit the order**. The whole form opens: every item under its section, with a box for each color (or one quantity box on a plain form). Type the number of cases, the way the form is filled in; clear a box to take that item off. Changed rows turn amber and the units, totals and order total update as you type.
3. Add a line under **Anything to tell them** if there is something to say (why you are adding an item, for example). VMS lists the changes itself.
4. Click **Send the changes**. VMS writes your numbers into the vendor's form (nothing else on it moves), files the revised copy on the order as "… REVISED M-D-YY.xlsx" and marks the earlier copy old, brings the order's items and total up to date, and writes the reply in the same email conversation with the revised form attached.
5. You land on that email in **Drafts**. Read it over, change anything you like, and press **Send**. Nothing goes to the vendor until you do.

## Good to know
- Only orders with an Excel order form on file get the box. An order sent as a PDF or typed into the email has no form to edit: change the lines in **Check-in pricing** and write the vendor from the conversation.
- VMS keeps the form's own arithmetic. Cases × case pack = units, units × price = total, and the grand total add up the way the vendor built them; if someone typed a number over one of those formulas, VMS puts the formula back on the row you changed.
- The email is written to the same people the order last went to. Add or remove people in the draft before you send.
- Once the email is sent, VMS reads it like any other order email, so the order shows the new lines and the revised file, and the **No reply yet** reminder counts from this send.
- If VMS cannot work out where the items are on a form, the box says so and nothing is changed; tell Dana which vendor's form it was.
