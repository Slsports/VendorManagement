Every book has a number on its cover and the permits inside run in order. When the FedEx box arrives, the sheet in it (the State's Vendor Permit Request Form) says which permit numbers and how many books they sent. VMS checks the books you enter against that sheet, so a missing book is caught the day the box arrives, not at the end of the season. When it all matches, the books go into Lightspeed by themselves.

## How to
1. Open **Sno-Park** in the menu, click **Receive books**, check the date and click **Start**.
2. **1. The State's sheet and the books**: click **Upload or scan the sheet** and pick the scan or PDF from your computer, or take a picture on your phone. VMS reads the permit ranges from the State's verification box, one row each: Kind, From, Including, and it works out the Books and Permits in the range (25 in a day book, 10 in a season book). Check the numbers against the paper.
3. Click **Photograph the first and last books** and take one picture showing the covers of the first and last book of each kind (or upload it). VMS reads each cover's book number and its beginning and ending permit numbers and puts them on the matching row as **First book #** and **Last book #**. You can also type them; VMS fills in the last one from the first. A box that did not come in order gets another range with **Add another row**. Then click **Confirmed, books received** (or **Confirmed, already in Lightspeed** for a box someone already entered by hand). VMS makes every book with its permit numbers.
4. **2. The check**: VMS compares the books with the sheet.
   - **It matches**: the books go into Lightspeed as a purchase order under CA Snow Park, checked in, and each permit item gets a dated note: who received how many day and season passes, which book numbers, which permit numbers. The State's order is marked received.
   - **It does not match**: the differences are listed (a book that is short, a book missing from the box, a count that is off). Look at the box again and fix any row entered wrong. If the box really is different, click **Email the State**: you get a task and an email draft to the State, with Dana copied, waiting in your Drafts. Read it over and press Send. Nothing goes into Lightspeed until the box balances or Dana accepts it.

5. **3. The Sno-Park map** (first box of the season only): print this season's map, make copies for the register, and click **Printed and copied**. Whoever received the box also gets a task for it.

## Good to know
- Nobody opens a book. A range that is not whole books, or book numbers that do not add up to the range, is marked before you confirm: that is how a missing book shows. A book whose number or permits overlap one already on file is refused, so a box cannot be entered twice.
- A book's number or permits can be corrected later with **Change** in the **Books** table, as long as the book is still on hand.
- The **Books** table shows every book this season: on hand, on a report, or returned, with the date it went back to the State.
- Only Dana can accept a box that does not match the sheet (when the State has answered or she knows why). She says why, and the books go into Lightspeed as entered.
- No sheet in the box? Enter its numbers by hand, or skip it: VMS then checks only the book sizes.
- Clicked **Receive books** twice? Open the empty box and click **Remove this empty box** at the top. A box with a sheet or books cannot be removed.
- A box that someone already put into Lightspeed by hand: **Confirmed, already in Lightspeed**. The check still runs; VMS records the box without making a second purchase order.
- **Receive books** asks which order to the State the box answers; pick it so the order is marked received.
- The State's sheet is filed under CA Snow Park in the **Sno-Park** folder, with the order.
