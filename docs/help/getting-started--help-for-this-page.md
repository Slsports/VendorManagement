Every page has a **Help** link at the top right. It opens the help for the page you are working on, beside it, so you never lose your place.

## How to
1. Click **Help** (the question mark at the top right of the screen).
2. The panel lists the help for this page first. Click a title to read it.
3. Click **Help for …** at the top of the panel to go back to the list.
4. Type in **Search all help** to look for anything else.
5. Click **All help** (or **Open on the Help page**) to go to the full Help page. Click the **X** or press Esc to close the panel.

## Good to know
- The Help page in the menu has every article, by topic.
