A freight bill that came some other way (forwarded to us, or sent by a broker) can be turned into a freight bill right from the email.

## How to
1. Open the email in **Mail**.
2. Next to the PDF attachment, click **Freight bill**.
3. Pick the billing company that sent it.
4. Click **Make the bill**.
5. A message says Claude is reading it. Click **the freight bill** in that message, or find it on the **Freight** page in a minute or so.

## Good to know
- The **Freight bill** button shows on PDF attachments only.
- Bills that come straight from the carriers' own emails are picked up on their own; you only need this for the odd ones.
