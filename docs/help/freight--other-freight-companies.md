Freight companies we have no account with, like FedEx, USPS and UPS tracking, are listed at the bottom of the Freight page. Their mail is freight mail, so it goes to Trevor.

## How to
1. Click **Freight** in the menu.
2. Scroll to **Other freight companies** at the bottom.
3. Click a company's name. Its page lists every email from it, newest first. Click one to open it.
4. For billing companies, click **Emails** on the company's card. On its page, **Its bills** shows just that company's bills.

## Good to know
- When Claude finds a new freight company emailing us, it adds it here on its own, so it never sits in "Who is this?".
- Their mail also shows on the **Freight** tab in Mail.
- To change one (name, email domains, or make it a billing or trucking company), click the pencil next to it, or **Edit** on its page.
