VMS adds the people we email with to each vendor's **Reps & contacts** by itself. Claude reads their email signatures for their name, title, phone numbers and what they do for us.

## How it works
1. When someone at a vendor writes to orders@, or we write to them, Claude reads their signature.
2. They are added to that vendor's **Reps & contacts** with their name, title and phone numbers (Work, Cell, Fax…). The boxes are ticked for what they do: **Rep**, **Orders**, **AR / Accounting**, **Customer service**, **Warehouse / Shipping** or **Owner**.
3. They show **Learned from email** next to their name.
4. Someone already on the list only gets what is missing, like another phone number or their title.

## Good to know
- It looks back 12 months, then keeps up as new people write.
- Only Orders mail and shared conversations are read, never anyone's private mailbox.
- No-reply and system addresses, freight companies and Worldwide are left out.
- What you typed is never changed, and the starred rep stays the same.
- To remove a wrong contact, click the trash can on its row. It will not come back.
- People at a rep group are added to the rep group's reps.
