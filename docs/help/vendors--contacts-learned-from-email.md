VMS adds the people we email with to each vendor's **Reps & contacts** by itself. Claude reads their email signatures for their name, title, phone numbers and what they do for us.

## How it works
1. When someone at a vendor writes to orders@, or we write to them, Claude reads their signature.
2. They are added to that vendor's **Reps & contacts** with their name, title and phone numbers (Work, Cell, Fax…). The boxes are ticked for what they do: **Rep**, **Orders**, **AR / Accounting**, **Customer service**, **Warehouse / Shipping** or **Owner**.
3. They show **Learned from email** next to their name. Click it to open the email they were learned from. For contacts learned before Oct 21, 2026, it opens all mail with that person instead.
4. Someone already on the list only gets what is missing, like another phone number or their title.
5. **Who is our rep:** when we email back and forth with someone about placing an order (prices, catalogs, quotes, the order, a PO), VMS makes them a **Rep**. If our emails with them are only about shipping, only customer service (returns, damages, account questions) or only invoices and payments, they get that role instead. Click **Why rep** (or **Why shipping**…) next to their name to open the email that showed it.
6. If the vendor has no main rep yet and only one person is a rep, that person becomes the **Main rep**.

## Good to know
- It looks back 12 months, then keeps up as new people write.
- Only Orders mail and shared conversations are read, never anyone's private mailbox.
- No-reply and system addresses, freight companies and Worldwide are left out.
- What you typed is never changed, nor anyone the vendor confirmed, and a main rep you picked stays the same. A rep from the Worldwide portal stays a rep.
- The Worldwide portal fills in what is missing too: someone learned from email first gets the title, phone numbers and rep role WWD lists for them.
- After new mail with someone who isn't a rep, VMS reads it again.
- When the vendor has a rep group, the company's own people are added as **Customer service** (or shipping, billing), never as **Rep**: our rep is the rep group's person.
- To remove a wrong contact, click the trash can on its row. It will not come back.
- People at a rep group are added to the rep group's reps. Those learned from Oct 21, 2026 on also show **Learned from email** on each vendor the group reps. If someone already typed them on one of its vendors, that card moves to the rep group, so they are never listed twice.
- Someone whose email address is at a company already on file for that vendor or rep group (for example @jrasales.com for JRA Sales) is always counted as theirs.
