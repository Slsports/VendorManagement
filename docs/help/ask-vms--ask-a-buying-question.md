Ask VMS is a chat where you ask buying questions in plain words, like "What do we have on order for goggles across all brands, and how does that compare to what sold last winter and what's in stock?" It looks up the answer in VMS and links every number to where it came from.

## How to
1. Click **Ask VMS** at the top of any page (next to **Help**; on a phone it's the speech-bubble icon), or in the menu. From a vendor or order page, the chat already knows which one you mean, so you can say "this vendor" or "this order".
2. Type your question in the box and click **Ask** (or press Enter). Shift+Enter starts a new line.
3. Wait a moment. While it works it says what it's checking, like **Checking sales and stock in LS…**.
4. Read the answer. Click any link in it to open the order, the file, the email or the vendor it came from.
5. Ask a follow-up in the same chat, like "Now just the kids goggles", and it remembers what you were talking about.
6. Click **New chat** to start on something else. Your chats are listed on the left.

## What it can look up
- Sales from Lightspeed by store and season, and what's in stock now, by item, category or brand.
- Orders, their lines, and the paperwork VMS has read (our orders, confirmations, invoices).
- A vendor's filed papers, rep group, main rep and alternate.
- The mail you can already see. Private mail stays private.

## Good to know
- Ask VMS only reads. It never changes an order, a contact or anything in Lightspeed, and it never sends email.
- If you ask for an email, it writes a draft for you in **Mail › Drafts**. Read it over and send it yourself.
- If something isn't in VMS, it says "I haven't confirmed this" and tells you what's missing, instead of guessing.
- Under each answer, **What I looked up** shows the steps it took and what that answer cost.
- Each person can spend $5 a day. The amount you've used today shows under the box. At $5 the box stops until tomorrow.
- Dana can read everyone's chats, to help you learn which questions get the data you need.
