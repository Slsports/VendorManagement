An invoice someone uploaded on **Invoice Uploads** waits here when VMS could not file it by itself: it could not tell the vendor or the store, or the scan is unclear.

## How to file one
1. Open **Review queue › Needs a hand**. The cards are under **Scanned invoices to file**. The card says what was missing.
2. Click **Open the scan** to look at it.
3. Pick the vendor, the store and the folder (Invoices, Credits, Shipping or Confirmations).
4. Click **File it**. It goes into the vendor's Documents with its store, and to Bill.com if the vendor's invoices go there.

## Good to know
- A store you pick for a store name VMS read (like "Lodge") is saved in that vendor's **Invoice alias**, so the next one files itself.
- If the same invoice is already on file, it is not saved again.
- **Not an invoice: set aside** leaves it unfiled. The person who uploaded it sees **Set aside**.
