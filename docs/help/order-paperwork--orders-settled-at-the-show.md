Some vendors (like World Famous) change the order with you at the show: items are discontinued or updated, and you pick new ones. Mark them so their new items are not flagged as mistakes.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page, click **Edit** and tick **Order is finalized at the show**. Click **Save**.
2. When their confirmation comes in, the check flags quantity and price differences and items you ordered that are missing, as usual.
3. Items on the confirmation that were not on your order or sales report show under **New items to double check**. They are not issues and are not put in the email to the vendor.
4. Once you have looked it over, click **This is our order**. The confirmation's lines become the order (a new order if there was none), and the vendor's invoices are checked against it.

## Good to know
- World Famous is marked this way already.
- **This is our order** replaces any lines the order already had with the confirmation's lines; it asks first.
