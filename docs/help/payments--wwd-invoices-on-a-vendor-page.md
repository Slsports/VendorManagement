Each vendor page lists its WWD invoices, and you can fix which order an invoice belongs to.

## How to
1. Open the vendor and find the **WWD invoices** box (it only shows when there are some).
2. Each invoice shows WWD's number, date, their invoice number, our PO, the amount, any discount, and when it was paid with which WWD payment #.
3. To tie an invoice to an order, click **Tie to an order** (or **Change order**) and pick the order.
4. To take it off an order, click **Untie**.
5. Click **Show all** to see more than eight.

## Good to know
- The header adds up what is not paid yet.
- Order pages show the same list for that order (see "See the WWD invoices for an order").
- Admin, managers and buyers can tie and untie; everyone else can look.
