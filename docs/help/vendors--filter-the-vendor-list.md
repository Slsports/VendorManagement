The row of filters under the search box narrows the Vendors list: by active or inactive, by who orders from them, by billing route, and by flags.

## How to
1. Click **Vendors** in the menu.
2. Use the first box to choose **Active** (the usual view), **Inactive** or **All**.
3. Use the second box to choose **Everyone's vendors**, **Mine**, one person's name, or **Unassigned** (nobody orders from them yet).
4. Use the third box to pick a billing route (**Worldwide**, **Faire**, **Direct**, **Prepaid Direct**) or **No route yet**.
5. Click **Needs review**, **Do not order** or **Fishing** to show only those vendors. Click the same button again to turn it off.

## Good to know
- The numbers on the buttons, like "Do not order (14)", count active vendors only.
- When Dana uses the **Showing** switch at the top to look at one person, the list starts on that person's vendors. Choosing something else in the second box wins.
- In the **Flags** column: **Do not order**, **Last resort** and **On hold** show the vendor's standing; **Fishing**, **Review** and **Delivery** are the other flags. Do-not-order vendors' names are red; inactive ones are gray with an **Inactive** tag.
