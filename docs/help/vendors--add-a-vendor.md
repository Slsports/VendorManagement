Use this when you start buying from someone who is not in VMS yet.

## How to
1. Click **Vendors** in the menu.
2. Click **Add vendor** (top right).
3. Type the **Name**. It is the only thing you must fill in.
4. Fill in what you know: **Aliases**, **Billing routes**, **Assigned to**, **Rep group**, **Payment terms**, contact details and so on.
5. Click **Create vendor** in the bar pinned to the bottom of the screen.

## Good to know
- The new vendor's page opens when it saves. Add reps and contacts there, under **Reps & contacts**.
- Before adding, search the list first (including **Inactive**) so you do not make a duplicate.
- You can also add a vendor without leaving what you are doing: see "Add a vendor from a vendor box".
- A vendor started from an email (Mail, **New vendor**) arrives with the name, email and website filled in as a guess. Check the name. When you save, that email (or all mail from that sender) is filed to the new vendor.
- **Cancel** takes you back without saving.
