Worldwide can't export their vendor directory, so VMS reads it from the portal with a bookmark. One click reads every vendor in the portal: address, contacts and reps, programs, product lines and returns information. You don't open any vendors yourself.

## How to
1. On a computer, open **Settings** › **Worldwide**.
2. Drag the green **Read all vendors** button up to your browser's bookmarks bar.
3. Sign in to the Worldwide portal and open the **Vendors** tab.
4. Click the **Read all vendors** bookmark. A green box in the corner shows how far it is ("Reading vendors: 120 of 700…").
5. Leave the page open until the box says **Done**. Back in **Settings** › **Worldwide**, the vendor details show under **Worldwide portal → VMS**.
6. Click **Bring into VMS**. It takes about a minute.

## What Bring into VMS does
- A portal vendor with the same name as one of ours (or one of its other names, or a show-list line already tied to it) fills in what the vendor page left empty: address, phone, fax, minimum order, payment terms, free shipping, freight terms and returns. The portal's people are added to **Reps & contacts** as not yet confirmed.
- Where you typed something different, yours stays. A **Worldwide portal differences** card in the review queue lets you keep yours or use the portal's.
- Programs (Standard and each show) go in the vendor's **Worldwide portal** box. Old ones are never dropped.
- A name that only looks alike ("Tara Toy Corp" and our TARA TOYS) becomes a line with a card asking if it is our vendor.
- Everyone else becomes a line, "not bought from yet". Find them under **Lines**. **Make it a vendor** brings its portal details along.

## Good to know
- Read the portal again any time (before a show, say) and click **Bring into VMS** again. Nothing is added twice.
- If the portal signs you out partway, the box says so. Sign in again and click **Read all vendors** to start over.
- **Send to VMS** sends just the page you are on. If the portal won't let a page go, it downloads as **wwd-page.html**; click **Upload a saved page** and pick that file.
- The bookmarks carry your own private key. Don't share them.
- Only the admin can see what was sent. Vendors you buy from directly or through Faire are not in the portal.
