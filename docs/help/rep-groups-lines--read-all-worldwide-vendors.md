Worldwide can't export their vendor directory, so VMS reads it from the portal with a bookmark. One click reads every vendor in the portal: address, contacts and reps, programs, product lines and returns information. You don't open any vendors yourself.

## How to
1. On a computer, open **Settings** › **Worldwide**.
2. Drag the green **Read all vendors** button up to your browser's bookmarks bar.
3. Sign in to the Worldwide portal and open the **Vendors** tab.
4. Click the **Read all vendors** bookmark. A green box in the corner shows how far it is ("Reading vendors: 120 of 700…").
5. Leave the page open until the box says **Done**. Back in **Settings** › **Worldwide**, the vendor list and the batches of vendor details show under **Worldwide portal → VMS**.

## Good to know
- If the portal signs you out partway, the box says so. Sign in again and click **Read all vendors** to start over.
- **Send to VMS** sends just the page you are on. If the portal won't let a page go, it downloads as **wwd-page.html**; click **Upload a saved page** and pick that file.
- The bookmarks carry your own private key. Don't share them.
- Only the admin can see what was sent. Vendors you buy from directly or through Faire are not in the portal.
