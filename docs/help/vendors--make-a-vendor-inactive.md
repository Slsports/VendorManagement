Make a vendor inactive when you no longer buy from them. It drops out of the lists but keeps all its orders, files and history.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page.
2. Click the **Active** switch at the top right. It turns gray and reads **Inactive**.
3. To bring the vendor back, click the switch again so it reads **Active**.

## Good to know
- To find inactive vendors, choose **Inactive** or **All** in the first filter box on the Vendors page.
- Inactive vendors still show in every vendor box, grayed out. Picking one asks **Reactivate this vendor?**; **Reactivate and use** brings it back right there.
- The **Active** tick on the Edit form does the same thing.
- Inactive is not the same as **Do not order**: use the standing for a vendor you still deal with but want to warn about.
- The vendor page says why a vendor is inactive (**Inactive because**). On Oct 24, 2026, 237 old vendors were made inactive because nothing new had been entered for them in LS since we moved to it (SLS since May 7, 2021; the marina and the Happy Camper since June 2020), and they had no order or WWD invoice in the last two years.

## When an inactive vendor comes back
- If one of them shows up again (an order made in VMS, an order or quote by email, or we ask them for a catalog or price list), Claude puts an **Inactive vendors coming back?** card in the Review queue (**Vendor changes** tab).
- Click **The email** or **The order** to see what it was, then click **Make active again** or **Keep inactive**.
- Newsletters and ads from them don't make a card.

## Vendors bought for the hardware store
- VMS can't see the hardware store's (SLH) LS account. Tick **Bought for SLH** on the vendor's Edit page for vendors we buy for SLH, so they are never made inactive for having nothing new in LS. The vendor page shows a **Bought for SLH** label.
