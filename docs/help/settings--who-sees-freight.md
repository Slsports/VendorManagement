Freight mail and bills go to the person who owns the freight companies (Trevor). Tick anyone else who should see all of it too.

## How to
1. Open **Settings** and click **Mail**.
2. Find the **Freight** box.
3. Tick a person's name to let them see freight; untick to stop.

## Good to know
- A ticked person sees freight mail under Mine in Mail, in Mail for you, and the Freight bills card on the dashboard.
- Dana is ticked while Trevor is learning; untick when he has the hang of it.
