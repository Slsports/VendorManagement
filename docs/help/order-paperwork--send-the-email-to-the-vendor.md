When a confirmation or invoice does not match, Claude drafts an email to the vendor. You read it, change anything you like, and send it.

## How to
1. Open the check. The draft is in the box **Email Claude drafted to the vendor**, with who it goes to and the subject.
2. Click **Review & send email**.
3. Edit the To, subject or text as needed.
4. Click **Send**.

## Good to know
- The email goes to whoever sent the paper, or the vendor's email if it came another way. If there is no address on file, the box says so; add one before sending.
- It is sent as a reply in the same email conversation, and your signature is added when it sends.
- Once sent, the check is marked "Done, email sent" and you go back to where you were.
- Nothing is sent until you press **Send**.
