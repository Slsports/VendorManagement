Sometimes a name is saved on a vendor that really belongs to someone else, like a rep agency's name saved on one of its lines ("TOYOLOGY" on Blokees). Mail that says that name is then taken to be about that vendor. VMS asks you about it with a **Names on the wrong vendor?** card in the Review queue (**Vendor changes** tab).

## How to
1. Open **Review queue** and click **Vendor changes**.
2. Read the card: why the name looks wrong, the vendor, and **the mail that says** the name (one click to see it).
3. Click **Remove "(name)"** to take the name off the vendor, or **Keep it**.

## Good to know
- After you remove a name, VMS checks the old mail again within a minute or two: mail that named the vendor only by that name is no longer connected to it. The vendor page shows the counts under **Name re-check**.
