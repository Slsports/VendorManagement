The Worldwide show listings that have been loaded show who was at each show and in which booth.

## How to
1. Click **Lines** in the menu.
2. In **Any show**, pick the show.
3. Choose **All** to include lines that are already vendors.
4. Each card shows the booth number and the exhibitor's name for that show.
5. For one vendor, open its page and look at **Worldwide shows** (booth, new exhibitor, who shared the booth).

## Good to know
- The **Buying Shows** page in the menu is not built yet. It will hold show planning: vendors to visit with booth numbers, checklists, budgets and pre-show reports.
