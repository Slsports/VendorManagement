Cards about a single vendor have a red **Not a vendor, delete it** button, for names that were never a vendor (for example a shipping company imported from LS).

## How to
1. On the card, click **Not a vendor, delete it** (bottom right).
2. Confirm. The vendor is deleted.

## Good to know
- A deleted name never shows in VMS again, and imports skip it.
- It cannot delete a vendor that has orders.
- Duplicate cards do not have this button; use merge or keep both instead.
