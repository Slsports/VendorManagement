The billing route says how we are billed for a vendor. A vendor can have more than one; one is the usual route.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and click **Edit**.
2. Under **Billing routes**, tick every way we can be billed:
   - **Worldwide**: billed and paid through the WWD portal.
   - **Faire**: ordered on Faire, paid by credit card.
   - **Direct**: the vendor bills us directly, paid through Bill.com.
   - **Prepaid Direct**: paid up front, directly. Choose **Credit card** or **ACH**.
3. If you ticked more than one, choose **Usual route** under the one you use most.
4. Click **Save changes**.

## Good to know
- Routes show as colored tags on the vendor list and page; the usual one comes first. Prepaid Direct shows how it is paid ("Prepaid Direct · ACH").
- On the vendor page the Overview ends with a line explaining each route, with "(default)" beside the usual one.
- A WWD vendor's page also links to **WWD contacts** in the Overview.
- On the Vendors page, the route filter and **No route yet** help you find vendors still missing a route.
