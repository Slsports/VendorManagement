When an email on a "Does this need an answer?" card is actually an order, start the order right from the card.

## How to
1. On the card, click **Create an order**.
2. The new order form opens for the email's vendor. Fill it in and click **Add order**.
3. The order is filed to that vendor and linked to the email.

## Good to know
- The email must be filed to a vendor first. If it is not, a message says so: click **Open the email**, use **File to a vendor**, then come back and click **Create an order**.
- Creating the order does not settle the card; still click **Needs an answer** or **No answer needed**.
