Some vendors still write to the old Gmail, billing@ or someone's own address. Forward the email to orders@ and VMS handles it as the vendor's own email.

## How to
1. Forward the vendor's email to **orders@shaverlakesports.com** (keep the attachments).
2. VMS reads the forwarded part ("From: … Sent: …") to see which vendor it came from, and files it to that vendor.
3. From there it is handled like any vendor email: confirmations and invoices go to their folders and are checked against the order, orders are read, pictures are sorted.
4. In the email, the sender line says **forwarded from** and the vendor's address.

## Good to know
- Forwards from our own addresses never make a **Who is this?** card for us. If VMS does not know the vendor's address yet, the card is for the vendor's address instead; answer it once and all their mail is filed.
- Forwarding a vendor's invoice back to the vendor (to ask them to update their records) also files the invoice.
