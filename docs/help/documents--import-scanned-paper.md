For stacks of scanned paper, Claude reads every page, splits a stack into separate documents, and files each one with its vendor, folder, number, date and total.

## How to
1. Click **Vendors** in the menu, then **Bulk import documents**.
2. Tick **Scanned paper** before adding the files.
3. Drop the PDFs, or click **Choose a folder** or **Choose files**.
4. Wait while Claude reads the scans (a progress bar shows how far along it is).
5. Check **Need a look** (faded, handwritten or unclear pages, or a vendor name Claude could not find) and fix the vendor, folder or year, or click **Looks right**.
6. Click **File … documents**.

## Good to know
- A stack becomes one file per document ("pages 3–4").
- The date on each paper sets its year folder. Credits go to **Credits**, packing slips to **Shipping**.
- The number, date and total show beside each file and can be searched in the vendor's Documents.
