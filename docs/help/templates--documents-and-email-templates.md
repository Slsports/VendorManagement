**Templates** in the menu shows the letters and emails VMS writes to vendors, sorted into groups (Freight first), each with what it says right now.

## Change one
1. Click **Templates** in the menu.
2. Find the email and click **Edit**.
3. Change the subject or the wording. Keep the words in curly brackets, like **{po}** or **{first_name}**: VMS fills them in for each email.
4. Put two stars around words for bold (\*\*like this\*\*). A link looks like \[Click Here\]({form_link}).
5. Click **Save**. Emails VMS writes from then on use the new wording. Drafts already waiting keep the old wording; change those in **Mail › Drafts** if needed.

## The routing letter
The routing letter (the PDF) is listed under **Freight**. **Open and edit the letter** goes to **Freight › Routing letter**, where **Edit the letter** changes it.

## Good to know
- More emails move onto this page group by group: orders, vendors and credits come next.
