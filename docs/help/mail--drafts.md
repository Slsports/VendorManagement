The **Drafts** tab in Mail holds emails saved before sending: ones you saved, and ones Claude prepared for you to look over.

## How to
1. While writing an email, click **Save draft**. It goes to **Mail** › **Drafts**.
2. To finish it, open **Mail**, click **Drafts** and click the email.
3. Change anything you like, then click **Send**. It leaves Drafts once it is sent.
4. To throw one away, click the trash can on its row.

## Good to know
- Drafts marked **Prepared by Claude** were written for you; read them over before sending.
- Some prepared drafts also sit in orders@'s Gmail **Drafts**. Send from either place; sending from VMS removes the Gmail copy.
- Files attached from your computer are not kept in a draft; attach them again before sending.
- Your drafts are yours; the admin can see everyone's.
- Routing letter emails prepared from **Freight › Routing letter** show a **Send all** button at the top of Drafts. They go out one at a time from orders@, each with the letter attached.
