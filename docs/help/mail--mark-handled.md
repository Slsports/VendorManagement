Marking a conversation handled takes it off everyone's open lists once nothing more needs doing.

## How to
1. In the Mail list, click the **✓** at the start of the row. You do not need to open it.
2. Or open the conversation and click **Mark handled**. VMS takes you back to the list you came from.
3. Changed your mind? Click **Undo** on the message at the bottom of the screen (it shows for a few seconds).
4. To reopen one later, find it under **Handled**, open it and click **Open again**.

## Good to know
- Handled mail stays under **Handled**, on its vendor's page and on its carrier's.
- If the vendor writes again, the conversation comes back as **Needs an answer** on its own.
- To mark many at once, use the checkboxes (see "Work on several conversations at once").
