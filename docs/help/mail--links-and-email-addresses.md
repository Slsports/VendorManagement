Links in an email open in a new tab, and email addresses start a new email in VMS.

## How to
1. Open the conversation.
2. Click a web address to open it in a new browser tab.
3. In plain text, click an email address to start a **New email** to that person from VMS, filed to the same vendor.
4. Click an outside address in the **To** or **Cc** line to see all mail with that person.

## Good to know
- In the formatted view, links also open in a new tab.
- Our own addresses (orders@ and staff) are not links.
