The **Showing** switch lets Dana look at the dashboard and lists the way one person sees them, or everyone's at once, while the staff learns.

## How to
1. At the top of the screen, open the **Showing: Me** drop-down (on a phone, the small bar under the top bar).
2. Choose a person's name, or **Everyone**.
3. A purple bar appears under the top bar: "Showing Trevor's view" or "Showing everyone".
4. To go back, click **Back to mine** on the purple bar, or choose **Me**.

## Good to know
- The dashboard, Mail, Tasks, Review queue, Vendors and Orders follow the switch. For example the Mail for you card becomes "Mail for Trevor".
- You are still yourself: anything you do is done as Dana, not as that person.
- The choice is remembered on that device until you change it, so watch for the purple bar.
- Clicking a number on the Team page switches to that person for you.
- Only the administrator has this switch; everyone else always sees their own.
