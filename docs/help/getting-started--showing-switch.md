The **Showing** switch lets you look at the dashboard and lists the way another person sees them. Dana can choose anyone, or everyone at once. Trevor and Jarrett can see each other, so when one is out the other can see what is waiting and pitch in without calling.

## How to
1. At the top of the screen, open the **Showing: Me** drop-down (on a phone, the small bar under the top bar).
2. Choose a person's name (Dana also has **Everyone**).
3. A purple bar appears under the top bar: "Showing Trevor's view".
4. To go back, click **Back to mine** on the purple bar, or choose **Me**.

## Picking up their work
- Open a task, review card or order from their view and change **Assigned to** to yourself, or just finish it.
- You are still yourself: anything you do is done as you, not as that person.

## Good to know
- The dashboard, Tasks, Review queue, Orders, Vendors and Mail follow the switch. For example the Mail for you card becomes "Mail for Trevor".
- Their personal mail and anything in Accounting stay private. A task tied to their personal mail does not show.
- The choice is remembered on that device until you change it, so watch for the purple bar.
- Clicking a number on the Team page switches to that person (Dana).
- Who can see whom is set by Dana in **Settings › Users**, in the **Can see** column. People with nobody ticked do not get the switch.
