Orders billed through Worldwide show their WWD invoices on the order page, so you can see what was billed and when it was paid.

## How to
1. Open the order.
2. Find the box **WWD invoices for this order** (it only shows when there are some).

## What you see
- WWD's number, the date, the vendor's own invoice number, our PO, the amount and any discount.
- A **Credit**, **Debit** or **Membership fee** tag when it is not a plain invoice.
- When it was paid and with which WWD payment number, or "Not paid yet" (in amber) with the due date.
- The box header adds up what is not paid yet.

## Good to know
- VMS ties invoices to orders by our PO, or by the vendor's only order for exactly that amount.
- To tie an invoice to a different order, or untie it, use the WWD invoices box on the vendor page.
- When all of an order's WWD invoices are paid, the order is marked paid through the Worldwide portal.
- WWD invoices are loaded on the **Payments** page (WWD box).
