The Tasks page lists your tasks. The **My tasks** box on the dashboard shows how many you have and turns red when one is late.

## How to
1. Click **Tasks** in the menu.
2. Pick a tab: **My tasks** (assigned to you), **Given to others** (you gave them, still open) or **Done**.
3. Click a column name (**Task**, **Due**, **Remind**, **Assigned to**, **From**, **About**) to sort; click again to reverse.
4. Click a task's name to see or change it.

## Good to know
- Late tasks show in red ("2 days late"); today's in amber.
- On the dashboard, click the **My tasks** box to open the list; click **+** to add one.
- Dana can use **Showing** at the top of the screen to see anyone's tasks, or everyone's.
- Click a task's name to go straight to its work; click **Edit** next to it to change it (see "Go straight to a task's work").
