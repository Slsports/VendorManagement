Vendor scores puts every vendor's scorecard in one table, best first, so you can compare them before buying.

## How to
1. Click **Reports** in the menu, then **Vendor scores**.
2. Read across a row: **Standing**, **Overall**, the six scores, **Orders**, **Freight %** and **Issues**.
3. Click any header to sort by it; click again to reverse. Hover a score header for what it means.
4. Use the box at the top right to switch between **Scored vendors** and **All vendors**.
5. Click a vendor's name to open its page and full scorecard.

## Good to know
- Five is best. Green is 4 or more, amber 3, red below 3; a dot means no score yet.
- **Issues** adds up damage or shortage notes, late or lost notes, and freight charged when it should have been free.
- The six scores and where they come from: see "How vendor scores are worked out".
