This dashboard box counts the mail waiting on you, and opens the list.

## How to
1. Click **Mail for you** on the dashboard to open the list. Click it again to close it.
2. Click a row to open the conversation, or **Open Mail** for the Mail page.

## Good to know
- It lists vendor replies to mail you own and mail you sent that has had **No answer yet**.
- It turns red when something has had no answer.
- The same number shows beside **Mail** in the side menu.
- Snoozed conversations leave it until their time.
