The Mail page is orders@ inside VMS, filed by vendor. You read, answer and sort vendor mail here; Gmail is only the backup.

## How to
1. Click **Mail** in the menu. The number beside it is how many conversations are waiting on you.
2. Across the top, pick the mailbox: **Orders (shared)**, **My mail**, and (for Dana) the other mailboxes and **Old Gmail**.
3. Pick the view: **Primary**, **Promotions**, **Freight**, **Everything**, **Drafts** or **Sent**.
4. Pick where it stands: **Needs an answer**, **No answer yet**, **Waiting on vendor**, **All open**, **Handled**, **All**, **Snoozed** or **Deleted**.
5. Click a subject to open the conversation.

## Good to know
- Each row shows who wrote last (or "We wrote last"), the subject and first words, the vendor, the owner, where it stands and the date. A paperclip means it has attachments.
- The page opens on **Primary** and **All open**, showing your own mail (**Mine**).
- New mail shows up within a few minutes of arriving; orders@ is checked every minute.
- The newest 300 conversations are shown; search or filter to narrow it down.
- Viewers can read mail but cannot answer, file or change it.
