Permit books are ordered from the State with their permit request form (OHV 145SP). VMS fills the form, names it the way Dana names it, files it and writes the email. Once a year the State emails the vendor packet; VMS files it and prepares the registration for Dana.

## A new order (Cat or anyone who can edit)
1. Open **Sno-Park** in the menu and click **New order**.
2. Type how many **Day books** and **Season books** (a day book holds 25 permits, a season book 10) and click **Fill the form and draft the email**.
3. The email opens in your **Drafts**: the filled form is attached, it goes to the State's permits mailbox with a copy to Dana. Read it over and press **Send**. You also have a task for it, which closes when the email goes.
4. The order shows under **Orders to the State** on the Sno-Park page with its number, who made it and whether it was sent. When the box arrives, receive it with **Receive books** and pick this order.

## The yearly packet (Dana)
- When the State's "Vendor Registration" email arrives at billing@, VMS opens the season and, within a few minutes, unzips the packet into the vendor's **Vendor Packet** folder (the forms, the example forms, the map, the flowchart, the print-only packet). The Report of Operations Excel becomes the season's form on its own.
- VMS fills in the vendor agreement (OHV 56SP) with the store's standing answers and Dana's signature, fills the first permit request with the same books as last season's first order, and writes the reply in the State's email conversation from billing@ with both attached. Dana's task opens the draft: she checks the two forms and presses **Send**.
- The map is printed when the first box arrives (see "Receive the Sno-Park books").

## Good to know
- Files are named Dana's way: "#1 - 11-2-26 - OHV 145SP-2026-2027 - SLS Vendor Permit Request Form". The email subject is "Sno-Park Permit Request #1 – 2026-2027".
- Dana's signature picture is uploaded once in **Settings › Mail**, under her name (**Signature picture**). Without it the forms are filled but not signed, and the page says so.
- The store's answers on the agreement (business name, numbers, addresses, hours) are kept in VMS from the 2026-2027 agreement. Tell Dana if any of them change.
- **Make the form again** on an order that is not sent yet fills the form afresh, for instance after the signature was added.
