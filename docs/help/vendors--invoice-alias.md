Some vendors print their own names for our stores on their invoices. Mountain Milk writes Town Store for SLS, Camp Store for GS and Marina for SLM. The **Invoice alias** tells VMS which store each name means, so scanned invoices file to the right store with no one picking it.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and click **Edit**.
2. In **Invoice alias**, click **Add a store name**.
3. Type the name exactly as it is on their invoice (like Town Store) and pick our store.
4. Click **Save**.

## Good to know
- VMS already knows TOWN means SLS, MARINA means SLM, and HAPPY or HC means GS, for every vendor.
- When VMS reads a store name it doesn't know, the invoice waits in **Review queue › Needs a hand**. The store you pick there is added to the vendor's Invoice alias for you.
- Each scanned invoice shows its store on the vendor's Documents.
