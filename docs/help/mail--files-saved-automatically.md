Many attachments are saved to the vendor's Documents without anyone clicking Save.

## How to
1. Nothing to do. Look for the green **Saved to Folder › Year** tag beside a file in the email.
2. If it is in the wrong folder, click the tag to move it.

## Good to know
- **Price lists, catalogs, show specials and order forms** (links too) are saved; the newest of each is the current one, older ones stay as history.
- **Confirmations and invoices** attached to a vendor's email are saved to their folder, and Claude checks them against the order.
- **Pictures of items** (real photos, not logos or signature pictures) go to **Images**; the same picture is saved once.
- **Damage photos** (subject about damage, defects, wrong items, order issues or returns) go to **Damaged Items**.
- **Delivery receipts** from carriers go to **Shipping**.
- Artwork proofs are not saved on their own; save the approved one to **Approved proofs**.
- The email must be filed to a vendor; mail filed later catches up.
