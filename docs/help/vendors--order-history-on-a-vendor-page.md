**Order history** sits inside the **Ordering Reports & History** box, folded; click **Order history** to open it. It lists every order placed with the vendor, newest first, with totals.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page, open **Ordering Reports & History**, then click **Order history**.
2. Read the summary at the top right: number of orders, total spent, last order date, which seasons and shows they are bought for.
3. Click a column header (**Ordered**, **Status**, **What**, **Store**, **By**, **Cost**, **Ship**, **Paid**) to sort. Click again to reverse.
4. Click an order's date to open the order.
5. To record an order, click **Add order**, fill in **What was ordered**, the date, cost, who placed it and the rest, and save.

## Good to know
- Under each cost: **Free freight**, **1/2 freight**, **Freight capped** at a percent, or the freight charged ("+$45.50 freight"), as the order's **Shipping** box says. Blank when nothing is known yet.
- Orders arrive from the Placed Order Summary import and from mail as well as by hand.
- **Bought through a hardware supplier:** some SLS goods are ordered from Emery Jensen (EJD), the hardware store's weekly supplier, because their freight is a flat $65 per truckload. When the PO names one of our SLS vendors (for example EJDPARICON11526 for Paricon sleds) or says SLS (like BBQSLS…), VMS makes it an SLS order. It shows on EJD's page as **SLS order for PARICON**, and on Paricon's page as **Bought through EMERY JENSEN DIST**, so all your Paricon buying is in one place.
- To make sure VMS can tell, put the vendor's name or SLS in the PO, the way you already do.
