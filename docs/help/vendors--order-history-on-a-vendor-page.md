The **Order history** box lists every order placed with the vendor, newest first, with totals.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and find **Order history**.
2. Read the summary at the top right: number of orders, total spent, last order date, which seasons and shows they are bought for.
3. Click a column header (**Ordered**, **Status**, **What**, **Store**, **By**, **Cost**, **Ship**, **Paid**) to sort. Click again to reverse.
4. Click an order's date to open the order.
5. To record an order, click **Add order**, fill in **What was ordered**, the date, cost, who placed it and the rest, and save.

## Good to know
- The Cost column notes "free ship" or the freight added when known.
- Orders arrive from the Placed Order Summary import and from mail as well as by hand.
