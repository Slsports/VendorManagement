Freight mail and bills go to the carrier's owner (Trevor). Anyone else can be ticked to see all of it too, for example while someone new is learning.

## How to
1. Click **Settings** in the menu, then the **Mail** tab.
2. Find the **Freight** box.
3. Tick a person to let them see all freight; untick to stop.

## Good to know
- A ticked person sees freight conversations under **Mine** in Mail and in the **Mail for you** card, and gets the **Freight bills** card on the dashboard.
- The carrier owner is not in the list; they always see it. To change the owner, edit the carrier (**Its mail goes to**).
