The **Tasks** box on a vendor page shows the open tasks about that vendor and lets you add one already tagged to it.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and find **Tasks** (near the top).
2. Click **Task**.
3. Fill in **What needs doing**, and if you like **Due**, **Remind me**, **Assigned to**, **Repeat** and **Notes**. The vendor is already tagged.
4. Click **Add task**.

## Good to know
- The open tasks about the vendor are listed in the box, with a count.
- The same tasks also show on the Tasks page and on the dashboard of whoever they are assigned to.
