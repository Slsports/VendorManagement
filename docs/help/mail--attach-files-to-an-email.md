Add files from your computer or from the vendor's Documents to any email you write in VMS.

## How to
1. In the email box (new, reply or forward), click **Attach from computer**, right under **Subject**, and pick one or more files (on a phone it opens your photos and files).
2. If the email is about a vendor, click **Attach from the vendor's files**. For a file of another vendor, or when the email isn't tied to a vendor, click **Attach from any vendor's files**, type the vendor's name, then open their files the same way. You can pick files from several vendors in one email.
3. The files for this order (or from this conversation) are at the top. Below them are the vendor's folders, like **Confirmations**, **Invoices** and **Credits**. Click a folder to see its files, newest first, with their dates.
4. Tick the files you want. Or type a number or name in the **Find a file** box.
5. The files you picked show above in yellow. To take one off, click the **x** beside its name.
6. Click **Send**.

## Good to know
- Attachments can add up to 20 MB per email.
- A forward already carries the original email's attachments.
- **Save draft** keeps every attachment with the draft: the vendor files you picked, files from your computer or phone, and files VMS made (like the suggested order's spreadsheet). Open the draft later and they are still attached; take one off with its **x**.
- When VMS writes a draft that needs a file (a credit follow-up, for example), the file is already picked and shows in yellow.
- To send one of a vendor's documents, you don't need to start an email first: on the vendor's page, in **Documents**, click **Email it** on the file. A new email opens to the main rep with the file attached and its name as the subject.
