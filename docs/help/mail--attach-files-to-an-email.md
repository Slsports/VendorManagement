Add files from your computer or from the vendor's Documents to any email you write in VMS.

## How to
1. In the email box (new, reply or forward), click **Attach from computer** and pick one or more files.
2. If the email is about a vendor, click **Attach from the vendor's files**.
3. The files for this order (or from this conversation) are at the top. Below them are the vendor's folders, like **Confirmations**, **Invoices** and **Credits**. Click a folder to see its files, newest first, with their dates.
4. Tick the files you want. Or type a number or name in the **Find a file** box.
5. The files you picked show above in yellow. To take one off, click the **x** beside its name.
6. Click **Send**.

## Good to know
- Attachments can add up to 20 MB per email.
- A forward already carries the original email's attachments.
- A draft keeps the vendor files you picked when you click **Save draft**. Files from your computer have to be added again.
- When VMS writes a draft that needs a file (a credit follow-up, for example), the file is already picked and shows in yellow.
