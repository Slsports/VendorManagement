Add files from your computer or from the vendor's Documents to any email you write in VMS.

## How to
1. In the email box (new, reply or forward), click **Attach from computer** and pick one or more files.
2. If the email is about a vendor, open **Attach from the vendor's files** and tick the ones you want.
3. To take one off, click the **x** beside its name.
4. Click **Send**.

## Good to know
- Attachments can add up to 20 MB per email.
- A forward already carries the original email's attachments.
