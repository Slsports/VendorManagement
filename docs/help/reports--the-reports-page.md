The Reports page collects VMS's reports: Sales reports (from Lightspeed) and Vendor scores.

## How to
1. Click **Reports** in the menu (under Insights).
2. Click **Sales reports** to run a vendor or category report from Lightspeed, or **Vendor scores** for the scorecards.

## Good to know
- More reports are coming later: spending, order status, payments, buying show results and cost trends, with date and store filters and a spreadsheet download.
