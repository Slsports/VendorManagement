The Insurance page is the tracker: every policy, when it renews, what each year cost and who paid, and the renewal task that appears 30 days ahead.

## How to
1. Click **Accounting** in the menu, then **Insurance**. The **Policies** tab lists them by renewal date; the badge says how soon.
2. Click a policy to see the facts, every policy year and its open tasks. **Edit** changes the facts. **Add a year** records an older premium.
3. When a policy renews, click **Renewed**: enter the policy year, the premium, the date paid, which account paid, and the new policy number if Cal Fair gave one. The renewal date moves a year on and the renewal task closes.
4. When the new policy paper arrives, click **Policy received**. Everyone on **Who needs proof** who needs that policy gets a task to send it.
5. **Add policy** adds one by hand; untick **Active** to retire a policy without losing its history.

## Good to know
- A renewal task appears 30 days before each active policy's renewal date, with the checklist from the renewal procedure.
- Policies on **autopay** say so on the task: confirm the payment went through, do not pay twice.
- Cal Fair issues a new policy number if a policy lapses; the old ones are kept under Prior numbers.
- The agent on each policy is a contact (Sam Gibbs at Hub International) with phone numbers.
