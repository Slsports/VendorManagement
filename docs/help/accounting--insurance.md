The Insurance page is the tracker: every policy, when it renews, what each year cost and who paid, and the renewal task that appears 30 days ahead.

## How to
1. Click **Accounting** in the menu, then **Insurance**. The **Business** tab lists the business policies by renewal date; the badge says how soon. The **Personal** tab lists our personal insurance (Goosehead): the homes at 35775 and 35771 Aunt Julia Lane and SJ&E Road, and the family vehicles.
2. Click a policy to see the facts, every policy year and its open tasks. **Edit** changes the facts. **Add a year** records an older premium.
3. When a policy renews, click **Renewed**: enter the policy year, the premium, the date paid, which account paid, and the new policy number if Cal Fair gave one. The renewal date moves a year on and the renewal task closes.
4. When the new policy paper arrives, click **Policy received**. Everyone on **Who needs proof** who needs that policy gets a task to send it. When Claude files the new policy paper to the policy under **Documents**, this happens on its own.
5. **Add policy** adds one by hand (on the **Personal** tab it starts ticked **Personal insurance**); untick **Active** to retire a policy without losing its history.

## Good to know
- A renewal task appears 30 days before each active policy's renewal date, with the checklist from the renewal procedure.
- Policies on **autopay** say so on the task: confirm the payment went through, do not pay twice.
- Cal Fair issues a new policy number if a policy lapses; the old ones are kept under Prior numbers. A policy that was replaced (SJ&E Road's Cal Fair and Steadily, replaced by Orion180) is retired: tick **Show retired policies** to see it.
- The agent on each policy is a contact with phone numbers: Sam Gibbs at Hub International for the business, David Wagner at Goosehead for personal insurance.
- Personal insurance contacts and papers are filed under **Personal insurance** in Contacts, Mail and Documents, apart from the business insurance.
- An insurer's billing emails to billing@ that show the policy's number (Travelers shows "Billing account 7276G0232") make a task **Pay …: $… due …**, reminded 5 days before the due date. A "due soon" or "past due" email reminds you right away (past due says so in the title). Clicking the task opens the bill email.
- The insurer's payment confirmation closes that task and shows on the policy year under **Paid**: the date, the amount and **the payment email**.
