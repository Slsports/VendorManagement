Each vendor's page has one **Freight** card with everything about their freight in one place. Vendors marked as having no freight don't have it.

## Routing letter
- The top line says whether they have our current routing letter:
  - **Has the current letter**: who received it, and when.
  - **Not confirmed yet**: it went to them but nobody has clicked to say they received it.
  - **Older version**: they had an earlier letter.
  - **Not sent yet**.
- The current letter goes with their next **Freight email**. When they click its "click here", the card shows it as received.
- **Open the letter** opens the current PDF. **Every send** lists who it went to, which version and when.

## Freight email
- Each of their orders still to ship has a **Freight email** button: their freight terms, the form for that order and our routing letter, ready to send. Once sent it shows the date and **Send again**.

## Shipments
- Click **Shipments** to see the carrier bills (PartnerShip, UPS, FedEx and the rest) that carried this vendor's shipments. Each line shows the date, carrier, invoice number, the PO when VMS matched it to an order, whether it is paid, and the amount for that vendor's part.
- **Open the bill** goes to the bill on the Freight page. **Order** goes to the order.
- A bill only shows here once it names the vendor. Match a bill's shipments to vendors on the Freight page (**Match a bill to vendors** in Help).

## Good to know
- A bill still waiting for its PDF shows **PDF missing**.
- The amount shown is that vendor's shipments on the bill, with the bill's fee on the largest one, not always the whole bill.
- **Freight › Routing letter** lists every vendor's routing letter in one table.
