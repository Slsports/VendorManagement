A carrier's bill (PartnerShip, UPS, FedEx and the rest) is one file on the **Freight** page. When the bill says which vendor a shipment came from, the same bill also shows on that vendor's page, so you can see freight from either side without keeping two copies.

## Where to look
- On a vendor's page, the **Freight** box lists the carrier bills that carried that vendor's shipments: date, carrier, invoice number, the PO when VMS matched the shipment to an order, whether it is paid, and the amount for that vendor's part of the bill.
- **Open the bill** goes to the bill itself on the Freight page. **Order** goes to the order the shipment was for.
- The box only appears once a bill names the vendor. If a bill's shipments have not been matched to vendors yet, do that on the Freight page (**Match a bill to vendors** in Help) and the box fills in.

## Good to know
- A bill still waiting for its PDF shows **PDF missing**; dropping the PDF on the bill, or filing it from Dropbox, fills it.
- The amount shown is that vendor's shipments on the bill, with the bill's fee on the largest one, not always the whole bill.
