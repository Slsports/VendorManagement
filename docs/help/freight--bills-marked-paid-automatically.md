VMS marks a freight bill paid on its own when it sees proof of payment, so you rarely have to do it by hand.

## What counts as proof
- A carrier's payment receipt: Claude reads the PDF, and the receipt is kept on the bill (**Payment receipt** link).
- Our own email saying it was paid, for example "This order was paid by ACH 10/8/26 by Dana".
- A Bill.com payment imported on the **Payments** page.

## How VMS finds the bill
- By invoice number; else the bill that email conversation is about; else the only unpaid bill from that carrier for that amount.
- The bill then says **Marked paid from our email** or **Marked paid from the receipt**, linked to the email.

## When it cannot tell which bill
1. A card appears in the **Review queue** for the carrier's owner, with the amount, date, method and links to **The email** and **The receipt**.
2. In **Which bill did it pay?**, pick the unpaid bill.
3. Click **Mark this bill paid**.

## Good to know
- Wrong? Open the bill and click **Not paid**.
