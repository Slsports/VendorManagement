In the upper right of every vendor's page, under **Tasks**, the **Activity feed** lists everything done with that vendor, newest first. Each line is the date and what happened, for example:
- "11/2/26 Steve Bahr wrote about Routing request for PO# BECKER2926"
- "Received invoice 184482 for PO BECKER-1-13-26 ($10,606.60)"
- "Received order PO BECKER-1-13-26" and "PO BECKER-1-13-26 entered to LS by Jarrett"
- "PO BECKER2926 moved to Shipped"

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page.
2. Read **Activity feed** on the right, under **Tasks**.
3. Click any line to open what happened: the email (already scrolled to it), the invoice or file, the order, or the task. A note opens **Notes** further down the page.
4. Click **Show more** at the bottom for older activity.

## Good to know
- Emails, papers filed (invoices, credits, confirmations, price lists, freight bills and the rest), orders (placed, confirmed, shipped, received, entered to LS, paid), finished tasks and notes all show. Pictures don't, so they don't crowd it.
- An invoice shows on its own invoice date, so old invoices filed later (like the LaserVault history) sit in their own year.
- Each email line is one sentence Claude writes when the email comes in. A brand-new email shows who wrote and the subject until its sentence is ready.
- Plain thank-yous, "got it" and out-of-office replies are left out.
- Mail in someone's own inbox shows only for that person and the admin, unless it was shared to Orders.
