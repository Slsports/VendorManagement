Rules decide who a new review item goes to. They run top to bottom and the first one that matches assigns the item.

## How to
1. Open **Settings** and click **Review assignments**.
2. Under **Add a rule**, choose **When the review is about**: **Fishing vendors**, **Lightspeed category**, **Kind of review** or **Everything else**.
3. For a category, start typing (Camping, Sunglasses…) and pick from the list. For a kind of review, pick it.
4. Choose who it **Goes to** and click **Add rule**.
5. To change a rule, pick another person in its **goes to** box. Untick **On** to pause it, or click **Remove** to delete it.

## Good to know
- Fishing rules run first, then categories, then kinds of review, then Everything else.
- A top-level category covers its subcategories.
- Adding a category rule sends "who orders from it" cards to the review queue for vendors in that category nobody orders from yet.
- Without rules, items wait unassigned. Anyone with edit rights can still hand an item to someone else from the queue.
