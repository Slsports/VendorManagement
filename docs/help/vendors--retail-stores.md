Some places we buy from are stores or local suppliers, not vendors we keep in touch with: Costco, Walmart, Amazon and the like. Tick **Retail store** on them so VMS leaves them out of the vendor upkeep.

## How to
1. Open the vendor and click **Edit**.
2. Tick **Retail store**.
3. Click **Save**. The vendor's page shows a **Retail store** label.

## What it changes
- No contact update email goes to them.
- No contact check cards in the Review queue.
- They are never put on the list of vendors to make inactive.
- Their orders, sales and Amazon purchases show the same as before.

## Good to know
- Ticked so far: Amazon, Costco, Dollar Store, Home Depot, Mountain Milk & Cream, Sam's Club, Smart N Final, Walmart and Frito-Lay.
- Untick it any time to treat them as a regular vendor again.
