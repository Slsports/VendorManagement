The **Scorecard** on a vendor page gives the vendor up to 5 stars on six things, plus an overall score.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and find **Scorecard**.
2. Read each line: **Ease of ordering**, **Communication and help**, **On-time fulfilment**, **Order accuracy**, **Shipping**, **Resolving issues**. Under each is what the score is based on.
3. To give your own score, click **Rate** on that line.
4. Pick a score (5 Excellent, 4 Good, 3 Fair, 2 Weak, 1 Poor) and write why in one line.
5. Click **Save**.

## Good to know
- Four lines score themselves from the order history (on time, accuracy, shipping, credits). A staff rating replaces the automatic score; the line then says what the history says, if different.
- Ease of ordering and Communication only get a score when someone rates them.
- The vendor's standing (on hold, last resort, do not order) and its reasons show at the top of the scorecard.
- The **Vendor scores** report on the Reports page compares all vendors.
