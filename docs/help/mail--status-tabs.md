The row of small tabs under the views shows mail by where it stands.

## How to
1. On the Mail page, click a tab:
   - **Needs an answer**: the vendor wrote last and we owe a reply.
   - **No answer yet**: we wrote, and the vendor has not answered within the follow-up days.
   - **Waiting on vendor**: we wrote last and are still inside the follow-up time.
   - **All open**: everything not handled (the standard view).
   - **Handled**: done.
   - **All**: open and handled together.
   - **Snoozed**: conversations snoozed until later.
   - **Deleted**: conversations someone deleted, with Restore.

## Good to know
- The same words show as a colored tag on each row and at the top of a conversation.
- Handled mail leaves every tab except **Handled** and **All**; it stays on its vendor's page too.
- A carrier update can also show a shipping tag, such as **Delivered** or **In transit**.
