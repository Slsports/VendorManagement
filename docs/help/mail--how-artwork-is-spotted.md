Claude reads incoming vendor mail and flags emails where the vendor is waiting on us to approve artwork.

## How to
1. Nothing to do. Flagged mail goes to Dana and onto the **Artwork approvals** box on the dashboard.
2. When Claude is unsure, Dana gets an **Artwork to approve?** card in the Review queue. Click **Yes, artwork to approve** or **Not artwork**.

## Good to know
- Only delivered artwork counts: the email carries the proof, mockup or design (attached, pasted or linked).
- Promises ("I'll mock something up"), requests for our logo, color options and product photos do not count.
- Ads, carriers and "not a vendor" senders are skipped.
