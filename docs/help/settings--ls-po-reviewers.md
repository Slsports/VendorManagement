After the person who placed an order approves its LS PO, it goes to the LS PO reviewer (Trevor) to check names and categories and send it to LS.

## How to
1. Open **Settings** › **Organization**.
2. In **LS POs**, tick the person who reviews them.

## Good to know
- With nobody ticked, LS POs go to Dana.
