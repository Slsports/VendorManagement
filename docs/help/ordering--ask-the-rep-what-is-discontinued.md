From a suggested order you can email the rep the item list and ask which items are discontinued and which can still be ordered. Claude reads the reply for you.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's suggested order.
2. Click **Email the rep** at the bottom.
3. An email opens with every item on the suggested order (Vendor ID, description and the quantity we would order), addressed to the vendor's rep. Change the wording or the address if you like.
4. Click **Send**.

## When the rep replies
- Within a few minutes Claude reads the reply (and any file they attach) and marks each item: available, discontinued, replaced (with what), out of stock (back when), or not clear.
- Anything not available goes to the **Unavailable items** tab of the **Review queue** for whoever sent the email. Nothing changes in Lightspeed until you decide on the card. Click **the rep's reply** on the card to see what they wrote.
- Your decision goes to Lightspeed, so every later sales report and suggested order shows it.
- The top of the suggested order says when you asked, links **the email**, and once the reply is read, **their reply** and **decide in Review**. Each line shows what the rep said about it.

## Good to know
- If no rep is on file, the email goes to the vendor's order address. Add the rep on the vendor's page so it goes to the right person next time.
- Only the rep's reply in the same email conversation is read.
