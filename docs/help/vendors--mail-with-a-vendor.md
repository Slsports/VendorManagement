The **Mail** box on a vendor page lists every conversation filed to that vendor. You can also start a new email from there.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and find **Mail**.
2. Click a conversation to open it. Sort by clicking the column headers.
3. To write, click **New email**. The **To** box starts with our starred rep (or the orders email); more addresses from the vendor are suggested.
4. Type the **Subject** and **Message** and click **Send**.

## Good to know
- Mail sends from orders@ with your signature, and is filed to this vendor.
- You can also click any email address on the vendor page (Overview or Reps & contacts) to write to that person.
- The box shows the newest 50 conversations.
- Mail from orders@ lands here once its sender is known as this vendor.
