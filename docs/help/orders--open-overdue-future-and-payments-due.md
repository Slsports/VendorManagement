Orders that need watching are counted on the dashboard: the **Ordering** card for placed, confirmed and overdue orders, and the **Payments due** and **Future-dated orders** boxes.

## How to
1. On the **Dashboard**, click **Pending**, **Confirmed** or **Overdue** on the Ordering card, or click the **Payments due** or **Future-dated orders** box.
2. The list opens. Click an order to open it.
3. From a number box, click **Open on the Orders page** to see the whole list with sorting and search.

## What they mean
- **Pending**: sent to the rep, or still being built with the rep, no confirmation yet.
- **Confirmed**: the vendor confirmed it.
- **Overdue**: confirmed, and more than 30 days past its ship date (red).
- **Payments due**: entered in LS, not yet paid.

## How an order leaves Payments due
- **Worldwide orders**: when the WWD **Payment History** you upload on the **Payments** page shows every WWD invoice on the order paid, the order moves to **Paid** with the date and payment number.
- **Bill.com orders**: VMS reads Bill.com every 15 minutes. When Bill.com shows the bill **Paid**, the order moves to **Paid**. VMS finds the bill by our PO on the bill, or by the invoice saved on the order. Bill.com's list has no payment date, so the order's note says it was paid in Bill.com instead.
- **By hand**: type a paid date on an order that is Entered or Ready to pay and it moves to **Paid**. If that was a mistake, change the status back; VMS won't change it again.
- An order still on the card after that has no payment VMS can find. Open it to check.
- **Future-dated orders**: pre-bookings shipping more than 30 days from now.
