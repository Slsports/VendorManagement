Four number boxes on the Dashboard count orders that need watching. Each one opens its list of orders.

## How to
1. On the **Dashboard**, click a box: **Open orders**, **Overdue orders**, **Future-dated orders** or **Payments due**.
2. The box opens and lists the orders. Click one to open it.
3. To see the whole list with sorting and search, click **Open on the Orders page**.
4. On that page, click **Back** to return to the Dashboard.

## Good to know
- **Open orders**: placed and not yet received (Open, Awaiting confirmation, Confirmed or Shipped).
- **Overdue orders**: not received and more than 30 days past their ship date. This box shows red.
- **Future-dated orders**: pre-bookings (Open, Awaiting confirmation or Confirmed) shipping more than 30 days from now.
- **Payments due**: entered in LS and not yet paid (Entered or Ready to pay).
- The box shows the first 25; the Orders page shows them all.
- You can move or hide these boxes with **Customize** on the Dashboard.
