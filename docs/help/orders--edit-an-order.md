Fix or fill in any date or detail on an order, such as the order date, ship date, costs or when it was paid.

## How to
1. Open the order.
2. Click **Edit** at the top right.
3. Change what you need. The form has three parts:
   - **Order**: Ordered, Placed by, PO, Route, Est. cost, Est. ship, What was ordered, Store.
   - **Freight, receiving and entry**: Freight cost, Freight notes, Received, Entered in LS, Entered by, Backorder, Shipment notes.
   - **Payment and credits**: Terms, Due, Final cost, Paid, Paid via, Payment reference, OK to pay, Credits due, Credits received, Credit notes, Dana's notes.
4. Click **Save** in the bar pinned to the bottom of the screen. **Cancel** leaves without saving.

## Good to know
- Changes made here are kept when the Placed Order Summary is imported again, so the sheet does not have to be fixed too.
- The status is changed on the order page itself, not in this form.
- Payment reference is the Bill.com number or the WWD payment number.
