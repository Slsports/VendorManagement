If an ad landed in Primary, or a real email landed in Promotions, move it. VMS learns from it.

## How to
1. Open the conversation.
2. Click **Move to Promotions**, or **Move to Primary** if it is in Promotions.

## Good to know
- VMS learns that kind of mail from that email address only, not the whole vendor. Moving a rep's newsletter to Promotions does not move the rep's own notes or the vendor's invoices.
- "That kind of mail" means newsletters, offers, or regular mail. Other emails of the same kind from the same address move too, and a message says how many. New mail like it follows.
- VMS learns from everyone together: a move by anyone, in Orders mail or in your own inbox, sorts that mail for everybody. Nobody has to move the same thing twice.
- Dana has the last say. Once Dana moves a sender's mail, a move by someone else only moves that one conversation, and a message says so. Otherwise the newest move wins.
- A move in your own inbox keeps that sender private: only you and the admin can see who it was.
- A small **Promotions** tag shows at the top of a conversation that is in Promotions.
