When a freight bill is paid, record when and how, so it leaves the **To pay** list.

## How to
1. Open the bill from **Freight bills** (the **To pay** tab lists unpaid ones).
2. Click **Mark paid**.
3. Fill in **Paid on** (today is filled in), **Paid by** (Card, Check, ACH / bank, Bill.com or Other) and, if you like, the **Check or confirmation number**.
4. Click **Mark paid**.
5. To undo it, click **Not paid** on the bill.

## Good to know
- The bill then shows a green "Paid" tag with the date, how and the number.
- Many bills are marked paid on their own: from a carrier's payment receipt, from our own email saying it was paid, or from the Bill.com import on the Payments page. See "Bills marked paid automatically".
- **Mark done** is separate: it means the shipments are all matched, not that the bill is paid.
