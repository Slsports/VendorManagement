**Incoming Shipments** (menu › Operations) lists every order that is Sent, Placed, Confirmed or Shipped, by the day it is expected. Received orders drop off.

## How to
1. Open **Incoming Shipments**. The table is soonest first; underneath, **Past their day, not marked received** holds the orders whose ship or arrival day has gone by (click the bar to open it): mark them received on the order, or chase them. **Expected** says "Arrives Nov 18" when a carrier gave a date, "Ships Nov 15" when only the ship date is known, or "Delivered Nov 12" when the carrier's email or receipt says so. 
2. The small line under each date says where it came from: the carrier's email (click it to open the email), the vendor's shipment form, or the order's own ship date (click to open the order).
3. Pick **From** and **To** dates to see everything expected in a window; **Any date** clears it. Nothing is hidden otherwise: show orders placed in August or February ship months later and stay on the list until they arrive. Narrow by store, by status, or type a vendor, PO or a few words.
4. Click **Week** or **Month** for the calendar. Click a day to list its orders on the right; each one opens the order or the email.
5. Click a column heading to sort by it; click again to reverse.

## Good to know
- The day is the carrier's arrival date when an email gave one, else the ship date from the vendor's shipment form, else the order's expected ship date. The page gets more exact as more carrier emails come in.
- The carrier column shows the carrier, the tracking or PRO number and the last status (picked up, in transit, out for delivery).
- Mark an order received on its page (**Shipping & receiving** › **Mark received**) and it leaves this list.
