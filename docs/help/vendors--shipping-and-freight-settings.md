Record a vendor's shipping rules so whoever orders knows when freight is free and how it must ship.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and click **Edit**.
2. Under Basics, set **Free shipping**: **Never free shipping**, **Sometimes (show special or above a volume)** or **Always free shipping**.
3. Type the amount in **Free shipping over (order value at cost)**, for example 500. (Not used when the vendor never ships free.)
4. Use **Freight program** for anything else about their freight, like "Free freight over $500".
5. Type **Freight routing instructions**, for example "UPS Ground collect on our account; never air".
6. Tick **Credits the freight back when we pay on time** if the vendor gives a freight allowance for paying on time.
7. Click **Save changes**.

## Good to know
- The Overview sums it up in one line, like "Free shipping over $500, or as a show special".
- With the freight allowance ticked, the vendor's orders show a pay-by reminder, and the Overview says "Credits the freight back when paid on time".
