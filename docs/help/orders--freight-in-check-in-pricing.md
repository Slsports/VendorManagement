Check-in prices include freight as a percent of the product cost. This explains where that percent comes from and how to change it.

## How it works
- **No freight bill yet**: the line says "Freight not billed yet" and prices use margin (and upcharge) only. Once the freight bill is matched to the order, click **Recalculate** to add freight.
- **Freight bill matched**: freight % = freight cost divided by the product total, e.g. "$85 on $1,200".
- **Free shipping**: if the Shipping box says the order ships free, freight is 0%.
- **Rate quoted in mail**: if a vendor email quoted a freight rate (like WWD's pallet rate), an amber note shows it, with a link to that email. Click **Use X%** to price with that rate; the rate's note keeps the link to the email.

## How to go back to the freight bill
1. In the grey line at the top of **Check-in pricing**, click **use the freight bill instead** beside the quoted rate.
2. Click **Recalculate** to update the prices.

## Good to know
- Prices you typed by hand stay as you set them when freight changes.
