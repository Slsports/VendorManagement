The menu down the left side of the screen takes you to every page. It is grouped into Overview, Procurement, Operations, Insights and System.

## How to
1. Click a page name, for example **Vendors**, to open it. The page you are on is highlighted.
2. **Orders** has an arrow next to it. Click the arrow to show the order statuses (**All orders**, **Open**, **Awaiting confirmation**, **Confirmed**, **Shipped**, **Received**, **Entered**, **Ready to pay**, **Paid**, **Cancelled**) and click one to see only those orders.
3. To make the menu narrow (icons only), click **Collapse** at the bottom of the menu. Click the same button again to widen it.

## Good to know
- The yellow number next to **Mail** is how many conversations are waiting on you. It updates every minute.
- When the menu is narrow, point at it with the mouse and it opens wide until you move away.
- On a tablet the menu is always the narrow icon strip; point at it or tap an icon.
- VMS remembers the narrow or wide choice on that computer.
- You only see the pages your role allows. See "Who can do what".
- For a list of every page, see "What's in VMS".
