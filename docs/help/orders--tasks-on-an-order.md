Add a to-do about an order, such as "call about the backorder", and see the open tasks already about it.

## How to
1. Open the order.
2. In the **Tasks** box at the top right, click **Task**.
3. Fill in the task. It is already tagged to this order and its vendor.
4. Save it.

## Good to know
- Open tasks about the order are listed in the same box, with a count.
- The task also shows on the Tasks page and in reminders, like any other task. See the Tasks section for due dates, reminders and repeats.
