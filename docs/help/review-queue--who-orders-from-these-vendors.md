These cards ask who orders from a vendor, for example when a category rule suggests someone.

## How to
1. Open **Review queue** and find **Who orders from these vendors?**
2. The card shows the vendor and why a person was suggested.
3. Keep the suggested person (the button says **Keep (name)**), or pick someone else and click **Assign**. Pick **Nobody yet** to leave it open.

## Good to know
- Only people marked as placing orders can be picked.
- The person assigned gets the vendor's review items and mail.
