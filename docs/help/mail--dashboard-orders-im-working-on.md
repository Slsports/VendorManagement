This box counts the conversations you flagged "Working on order".

## How to
1. Click **Orders I'm working on** on the dashboard to see them.
2. Click a row to open the email, or **Open the list** for the full page.

## Good to know
- Rows where the rep wrote last say **needs an answer**.
- Flag a conversation with **Working on order** to add it here.
