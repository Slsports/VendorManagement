Tie a vendor to the rep group that reps it.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and click **Edit**.
2. In the **Rep group** box, pick the group, or **None**. If it is not there, choose **+ Add new rep group…**.
3. Click **Save changes**.

## Good to know
- The vendor page's **Rep group** box then shows the group, its reps and the other vendors it carries.
