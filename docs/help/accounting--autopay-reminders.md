When a payment is scheduled to come out of the bank (an estimated tax payment MBS set up, an IRS Direct Pay or FTB Web Pay payment, an insurer's automatic payment), VMS puts a task on your list 5 days before, so you can make sure the bank account has enough to cover it.

## How it works
1. VMS reads the billing@ mail from MBS Accountancy, the IRS and the FTB, and any email about a scheduled or automatic payment, including the PDF it carries (like MBS's cover letter with the year's estimated payments).
2. Each payment with a date still to come becomes a task, for example **Autopay Dec 26: FTB $2,000.00 (2026 CA estimated tax)**. One that still has to be paid by hand says **Pay by …** instead.
3. The task reminds you 5 days before the date, the way you get reminders (text or email). Click it to open the email the payment came from.

## Good to know
- A payment scheduled months ahead still reminds you 5 days before it goes out.
- The same payment mentioned in two emails (MBS's letter and the FTB confirmation) is one task.
- CDTFA sales tax is not here: it has its own monthly prepayment and quarterly return tasks.
- Payments whose date has already passed make no task.
