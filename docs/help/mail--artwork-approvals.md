When a vendor sends a proof or design for us to approve, the conversation shows a pink artwork bar with the steps.

## How to
1. Open the conversation. The bar says **Artwork waiting on your approval** and since when.
2. If it is good, click **Approved**. Then save the final proof: **Save to documents** › **Proofs**, and click **Mark approved** on it.
3. If it needs changes, click **Needs changes**. It stays on your list as "Changes to send".
4. Once you have emailed the changes, click **Changes sent**. It now waits on the vendor's new proof.
5. Use **Waiting on approval again** to put it back, or **Not artwork** if it was never an approval.
6. To add one VMS missed, click **Artwork to approve** in the conversation's buttons.

## Good to know
- Your own reply saying it is approved closes it, and a reply asking for changes marks **Changes sent**, on their own.
- A new proof from the vendor puts it back to waiting on approval.
- Artwork approvals go to Dana, even when someone else orders from that vendor.
- Proofs are not saved to the vendor's Images folder on their own; you save the approved one by hand.
