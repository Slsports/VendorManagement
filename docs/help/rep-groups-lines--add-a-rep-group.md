Add a rep group that is not in VMS yet.

## How to
1. Click **Rep groups** in the menu.
2. Click **Add rep group**.
3. Type the **Rep group name** (for example "JRA Sales") and click **Save**.
4. Open the new group to add its reps and details.

## Good to know
- You can also add one while editing a vendor: in the **Rep group** box choose **+ Add new rep group…**.
