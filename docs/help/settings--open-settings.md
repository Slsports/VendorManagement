Settings holds the company-wide setup. Only Dana sees it.

## How to
1. Click **Settings** in the menu (under System), or click your name at the top right and choose **Settings**.
2. Pick a tab along the top. Settings opens on **Organization**.
3. Click **Back** at the top to return to where you were.

## The tabs
- **Organization**: the company's details and names deleted as "not a vendor".
- **Stores**: the four stores.
- **Worldwide**: our WWD member number and the WWD roster.
- **Review assignments**: who gets which review items.
- **Mail**: the orders@ connection, personal mailboxes, who sees freight, signatures and the follow-up wait.
- Not ready yet: **Users**, **Payment terms**, **Categories**, **Integrations** and **Branding**. They show "Arrives in Phase …" with what they will do.

## Good to know
- Switching tabs doesn't count as a step, so **Back** leaves Settings in one click.
