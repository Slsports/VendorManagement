Any PDF in an email can become a freight bill, for bills that came some other way (forwarded, or from a broker).

## How to
1. Open the email and find the PDF.
2. Click **Freight bill** beside it.
3. Pick the billing company that sent it and click **Make the bill**.
4. Claude reads it; click **the freight bill** in the message to see it a minute or so later.

## Good to know
- Bills from known carriers are read on their own; you only need this for the odd ones.
