Look over what Claude found and either send the vendor an email or mark it done.

## How to
1. Open the check: click **Confirmations** or **Invoices** on the Dashboard **Review** card and pick it, or find it in the order's **Paperwork** box.
2. Read the **Summary** and the table. Click a column header to sort it. Rows that differ are shaded red.
3. To see the papers themselves, click the file names under **This confirmation** (or invoice) and **Compared with**.
4. Read **The email conversation**: every email about this order with the vendor, oldest first. Click **Show all** for a long one, or **Open in Mail** to see it in Mail.
5. Then pick one:
   - **Looks good, done** when everything matches.
   - **Review & send email** when there are issues and the vendor needs to hear about it.
   - **Done, no email needed** when there are issues but no email is needed.

## Good to know
- The link at the top opens the order (PO, date and cost).
- Done checks stay on the order's **Paperwork** box marked "Done" or "Done, email sent".
- Marking a confirmation done sets an Open or Awaiting confirmation order to **Confirmed**.
- **Check again** asks Claude to compare once more, for example after you attached our order.
- If the emails are in someone's own mailbox (like Jarrett's), Claude can't use them for the check. A yellow note says so: click **Share to Orders** and Claude compares again with the whole conversation.
- The **Summary** is one paragraph; every item is in the table right under it, with its name and both quantities. When there is no table (nothing to compare line by line), the issues are listed under the summary instead, each ending with where Claude found it, like "(Sonal's email, Sept 24)".
