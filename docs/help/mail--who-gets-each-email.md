Every conversation gets an owner, and it lands on that person's Mail list and dashboard.

## How to
1. Nothing to do. To change it, open the conversation and pick another **Owner**.

## Good to know
- Mail you send from VMS is yours, and the vendor's reply comes back to you.
- New vendor mail goes to whoever the vendor is **Assigned to**, unless a rule in **Settings** says otherwise.
- **Greetings count:** an email that starts "Hi Jarrett" or "Dear Dana" goes to that person, even if the vendor is normally someone else's. "Hi all" or no name keeps the usual rule.
- Freight carrier mail goes to Trevor (Dana also sees it all for now).
- Artwork waiting on approval goes to Dana.
- "Other – not a vendor" senders go to Dana.
- Mail in a personal mailbox is always its owner's.
- Old mail read in from a mailbox's history is handled when one of us answered it (from any of our addresses), when we wrote last, or when it is over 6 months old. A question from the last 6 months that nobody answered shows under **Needs an answer**.
