The Lines page lists every brand we know of from rep line lists and Worldwide show listings. Most are catalogs to look at, not vendors.

## How to
1. Click **Lines** in the menu.
2. Search by line, rep group, exhibitor or booth (**Search a line, rep group, exhibitor or booth…**), then press Enter.
3. Narrow it with **Any rep group** and **Any show**.
4. Choose **Catalogs only** (the usual view), **Already vendors** or **All**.
5. Sort with the **Sort** box: **Name**, **Rep group** or **Latest show**.
6. Click **Catalog** on a card to open its catalog.

## Good to know
- Each card shows the route (Worldwide, Faire, Direct), **0% upcharge** when it has none, the shows it was at with booth numbers, and any specials.
- Lines that are already vendors have a **Vendor** tag and **Open vendor**.
- The page shows the first 300; narrow the search to see the rest.
