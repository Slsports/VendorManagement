There is no screen yet to add a person, change a role or turn off a login. The **Users** tab in Settings is a placeholder.

## Good to know
- The Users tab will let you invite people, set their role (admin, manager, buyer, viewer, uploader), choose their stores and deactivate them.
- Until then, new logins and role changes are set up for Dana behind the scenes; ask Claude.
- See "Who can do what" for what each role allows.
