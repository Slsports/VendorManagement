People are added and changed in **Settings › Users**. Only people on this list can sign in.

## How to
1. Open **Settings** and click **Users**.
2. Under **Add a person**, type their **Name** and **Email**, pick a **Role** and their **Stores**, then click **Add person**.
3. They sign in with **Sign in with Google** using that exact email, or click **Set-password email** on their row to send them a password link.
4. To change a role or stores later, change it on their row. To stop someone signing in, click **Deactivate**.

## Good to know
- The same row has the ticks for **Accounting**, **Can see**, **Enter counts** and **Send counts to LS**.
- Only Dana is admin; the role list never offers admin.
- See "Who can do what" for what each role allows.
