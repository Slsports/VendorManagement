The **Freight bills** card on the dashboard lists the bills waiting for you: ones to match and ones to pay.

## What it shows
- **To match**: the newest bills still being read, waiting for a PDF or waiting to be matched (up to six).
- **To pay**: unpaid bills, soonest due first, with the amount and due date. Overdue ones are in red, and the card is flagged.
- Click a bill to open it, or **Open Freight bills** for the full page.

## Who sees it
- The person who owns the carriers (Trevor), and anyone ticked to see freight (see "Let someone else see all freight").
- Everyone else does not see this card.
