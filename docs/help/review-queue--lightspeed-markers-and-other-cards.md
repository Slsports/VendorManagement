Some cards just need you to look and act yourself, then mark them done. For example **Lightspeed markers**: vendor names that carried an asterisk in LS.

## How to
1. Read the card and its question (for markers: what did the asterisk mean?).
2. Click **Open (vendor)** and make any change on the vendor page, for example set its standing to do not order with a reason.
3. Back on the card, click **Handled**, or **Dismiss** if nothing needs doing.
