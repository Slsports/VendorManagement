The **Reps & contacts** box on a vendor page holds everyone you deal with there. Star one as our assigned rep.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and find **Reps & contacts**.
2. Click **Add contact**.
3. Fill in a **Name**, **Email** and/or **Phone**, pick the **Kind** (**Rep**, **Orders**, **AR / Accounting**, **Customer service**, **Warehouse / Shipping**, **Owner**, **Other**) and an optional **Job title**.
4. Click **Save contact**.
5. To make someone our assigned rep, click the star beside their name. Click it again to take the star off.

## Good to know
- The starred person gets the **Our rep** tag and shows in the Overview. Follow-ups go to them, whoever took the order.
- The rep group's own reps are listed here too, tagged with the rep group's name. You can star them, but remove or change them on the rep group's page.
- Click an email address to write to them from VMS (from orders@, with your signature).
- To remove a contact, click the trash can on its row.
- Contacts also arrive on their own from the mailbox.
- If no contact has an email, a note reminds you: the discrepancy and status emails need one.
