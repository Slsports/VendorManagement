Accounting tasks are the bookkeeping to-dos: renewals, filings, payments, follow-ups. They live in Accounting, not on the general Tasks page, and only people with the Accounting tick see them.

## How to
1. Click **Accounting** in the menu, then **Tasks**. **Open** shows what is left; **Done** shows what was finished.
2. Click a task to go to its work (the policy, the person to send proof to, the email). **Edit** changes it.
3. **Add task** makes one by hand. Pick its **Procedure** so the steps open from the task. Repeats and reminders work as on the general Tasks page.
4. **Calendar** shows the same tasks by the day they are due. Switch **Week**, **Month** or **Year** at the top; click a day to see its tasks underneath.

## Good to know
- VMS makes many of these on its own: a renewal task 30 days before each insurance policy renews, and a "Send … to …" task for everyone who needs proof when a new policy comes in.
- Reminders reach you the way you chose under **Notifications** (dashboard, email, text).
- The Accounting overview shows what is overdue and what is due this week first.
