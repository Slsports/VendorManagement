Freight paid through WWD (XPO and the like) is filed to the freight company, but it also needs the vendor whose goods were carried, so it counts in that vendor's freight.

## How to
1. Click **Payments** in the menu and find **Freight to tie to a vendor**.
2. On a line, click **Pick vendor**.
3. Type and pick the vendor in **Shipped for…**. The line leaves the list.

## Good to know
- VMS fills this in on its own when an XPO delivery receipt with the same PRO number names the shipper.
- The section only shows when there are lines waiting.
