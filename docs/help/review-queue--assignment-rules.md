Rules decide who gets each new review card. They run top to bottom and the first match assigns the card.

## How to
1. Click **Settings** in the menu, then **Review assignments**. (Or click **Assignment rules** on the review queue.)
2. Under **Add a rule**, pick **When the review is about**:
   - **Fishing vendors**, **Lightspeed category** (type a category; a top level covers its subcategories), **Kind of review**, or **Everything else**.
3. Pick who it **Goes to**, then click **Add rule**.
4. On an existing rule, change the person, untick **On** to pause it, or click **Remove**.
5. Rules only run when a card is made. To run them over cards already waiting, click **Apply rules now**. Tick **Also re-assign items someone already has** to move those too.

## Good to know
- A new category rule also sends that category's vendors with nobody ordering to the queue as "Who orders from these vendors?" cards.
- Anyone who can answer cards can still hand a card to someone else.
