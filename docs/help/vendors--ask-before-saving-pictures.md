Some vendors send the same advertising over and over. Mark them so their pictures are only saved when you choose.

## How to
1. Open the vendor's page and click **Edit**.
2. Tick **Ask before saving pictures** and click **Save**. The vendor page shows **Pictures saved by hand**.
3. When you read one of their emails and want its pictures, click **Save pictures to Images** at the top of the email.

## Good to know
- Their proofs and things to approve still go to **Proofs** by themselves.
- Their invoices and confirmations are still saved and checked as usual.
- Logos are never saved, for any vendor.
- Untick the box to go back to saving their pictures automatically.
