Record an order straight from the conversation it came from, with the email linked to it.

## How to
1. Open the conversation. It must be filed to a vendor first (see "File an email to a vendor").
2. Click **Add order from this email**.
3. Fill in **What was ordered**, **Order date**, **Total cost**, **Placed by**, **Order taken by** and **PO or order number**.
4. Pick **Paid by**, and tick **Already paid** with the date if it is.
5. Add **Notes** if needed and click **Add order**.

## Good to know
- The order is filed to the conversation's vendor and linked to the email.
- **Placed by** can be anyone, even without a VMS login.
- Completing an order on the "Orders I'm working on" list also offers **Add order from this email**.
- A "Does this need an answer?" card in the Review queue has **Create an order** too.
