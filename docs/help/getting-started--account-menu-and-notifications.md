The top right corner of every page has a bell and your name. Your name opens your account menu.

## How to
1. Click your name or initials at the top right.
2. The menu shows your name, email and role (for example Manager).
3. Choose **Change password** or **Sign out**. Dana also sees **Settings** here.

## Good to know
- The bell (**Notifications**) is not in use yet; it says "Nothing yet". Overdue orders, payments due and reminders show on the dashboard instead.
- Your name and role also show at the bottom of the side menu on a computer.
